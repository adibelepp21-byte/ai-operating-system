4. Fundamental Change Principles
Principle 1 — Explicit Change
Material organizational change harus eksplisit.
Principle 2 — Authorized Change
Tidak ada material change tanpa authority yang berlaku.
Principle 3 — Impact Before Approval
Material change harus dinilai dampaknya sebelum approval.
Principle 4 — No Silent Structural Drift
Operating practice tidak boleh diam-diam mengubah canonical organization architecture.
Principle 5 — Ownership Preservation
Change tidak boleh secara implicit memindahkan ownership.
Principle 6 — Authority Preservation
Change tidak menciptakan authority baru hanya melalui structural rearrangement.
Principle 7 — Traceable Evolution
Perubahan harus dapat ditelusuri dari baseline menuju target state.
Principle 8 — Dependency Awareness
Perubahan harus mempertimbangkan dependency yang ditetapkan B8.
Principle 9 — Revalidation
Perubahan material harus divalidasi setelah implementation.
Principle 10 — Canonical Update
Organizational architecture hanya berubah setelah authorized change direfleksikan pada canonical record.

B4 tetap menjadi canonical Team architecture baseline.

---

# 18. Role Group Change Control

Role Group dapat berubah apabila responsibility cluster berubah.

Change harus menguji:

- responsibility coherence;
- duplication;
- accountability;
- capability support;
- Team boundary.

B5 tetap menjadi canonical Role Group baseline.

---

# 19. Ownership Change

> **Ownership cannot change implicitly through organizational rearrangement.**

Jika ownership berubah:

Ownership Change
→ Formal Organizational Decision
→ Boundary Update
→ Authority Alignment
→ Canonical Update

Collaboration, delegation, approval, dependency, atau temporary support tidak dengan sendirinya memindahkan ownership.

---

# 20. Authority Change

Structural Change
≠
Authority Change

Jika structural change memerlukan authority change:

Structural Impact
→ Authority Impact Identified
→ Applicable Authority Mechanism
→ Authority Decision
→ Organizational Change

B9 tidak menciptakan authority baru.

---

# 21. Cross-Platform Change

Jika perubahan PD-02 mempengaruhi Platform lain:

PD-02 Change Proposal
→ Cross-Platform Impact
→ Affected Platform Review
→ Applicable Coordination / Governance
→ Authorized Change

PD-02 tidak dapat mengubah organizational boundary Platform lain secara unilateral.

---

# 22. Change Review

Review memastikan:

- change objective valid;
- baseline valid;
- impact assessment complete;
- affected owners identified;
- dependencies assessed;
- authority identified;
- transition feasible;
- success condition defined.

Review ≠ Authorization.

---

# 23. Change Authorization

Change authorization berasal dari existing authority yang sesuai dengan materiality dan nature of change.

B9 tidak menetapkan authority baru.

B9 hanya memastikan:

> **the correct existing authority is engaged.**

---

# 24. Change Implementation

Implementation mengubah organization dari:

Current State

menjadi:

Target State

Implementation harus mempertahankan:

- accountability continuity;
- dependency continuity;
- decision continuity;
- operational continuity where required;
- canonical traceability.

---

# 25. Transition State

Material change dapat memiliki transition state:

Current State
→ Transition State
→ Target State

Transition state harus memiliki:

- defined duration;
- accountable owner;
- affected responsibilities;
- dependency treatment;
- exit condition.

Transition state tidak boleh menjadi permanent ambiguity.

---

# 26. Change Validation

Setelah implementation, perubahan harus divalidasi terhadap:

1. target organizational state;
2. responsibility allocation;
3. reporting hierarchy;
4. capability coverage;
5. Team boundaries;
6. Role Groups;
7. interfaces;
8. dependencies;
9. ownership;
10. authority alignment.

---

# 27. Change Closure

Change dapat ditutup apabila:

- target state implemented;
- validation complete;
- material findings resolved;
- dependencies revalidated;
- ownership confirmed;
- canonical records updated.

Canonical closure:

Implemented
→ Validated
→ Canonicalized
→ Closed

---

# 28. Change Failure

Change failure terjadi apabila:

- target state tidak tercapai;
- responsibility ambiguity meningkat;
- capability coverage menurun;
- critical dependency gagal;
- ownership menjadi ambiguous;
- reporting structure menjadi incoherent;
- cross-platform conflict meningkat.

Failure response:

Failure
→ Assessment
→ Corrective Change / Rollback / Escalation

---

# 29. Rollback / Reversal

Material change harus memiliki reversal strategy apabila consequence menjadi unacceptable.

Reversal dapat berupa:

- revert;
- redesign;
- partial rollback;
- controlled transition;
- replacement structure.

Rollback tetap membutuhkan applicable authorization.

Traceability harus menjelaskan:

# 36. Canonical Organizational Change Principle

> **PD-02 Organizational Change Architecture ensures that organizational architecture evolves through explicit, assessed, authorized, traceable, and validated change. Material changes to structure, responsibility, capability, teams, role groups, reporting, interfaces, dependencies, or ownership must not occur through silent organizational drift. Existing authority remains the basis for authorization, organizational ownership remains explicit, and the canonical organizational state is updated only after authorized change has been implemented and validated.**

Versi Bahasa Indonesia:

> **Organizational Change Architecture PD-02 memastikan bahwa organizational architecture berevolusi melalui perubahan yang eksplisit, dinilai, diotorisasi, dapat ditelusuri, dan divalidasi. Perubahan material terhadap structure, responsibility, capability, Team, Role Group, reporting, interface, dependency, atau ownership tidak boleh terjadi melalui silent organizational drift. Authority yang berlaku tetap menjadi dasar authorization, organizational ownership tetap eksplisit, dan canonical organizational state hanya diperbarui setelah perubahan yang sah diimplementasikan dan divalidasi.**
