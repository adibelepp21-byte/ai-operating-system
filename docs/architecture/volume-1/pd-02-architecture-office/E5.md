# 1. Purpose

E5 menetapkan bagaimana performance PD-02 Architecture Office dievaluasi, direview, diinterpretasikan, dan dikonversi menjadi finding, improvement signal, serta input bagi authorized decision-making.

E5 menjawab:

> **"Bagaimana performance PD-02 dievaluasi dan direview?"**

E5 mencakup:

1. Review Cycle;
2. Performance Assessment;
3. Executive Review;
4. Platform Review;
5. Improvement Decision.

E5 berada setelah measurement dan KPI architecture, serta sebelum improvement execution dan re-measurement.

Canonical flow:

```text
E3 — Performance Measurement
        ↓
E4 — KPI & Success Metrics
        ↓
E5 — Performance Review
        ↓
Finding / Assessment
        ↓
Improvement Signal / Decision Input
        ↓
Authorized Response
        ↓
Improvement
        ↓
Re-measurement
 
⸻
 
2. Constitutional Position
E5 harus mengikuti boundary yang ditetapkan E1.
Performance Review:
* measures;
* evaluates;
* interprets;
* identifies gaps;
* produces findings;
* produces improvement signals;
* supports authorized decisions;
* verifies improvement.
Performance Review tidak:
* mengambil alih Architecture Authority;
* mengubah Canonical Architecture;
* mengganti Architecture Decision;
* mengubah Architecture Standards;
* mengambil alih operational execution;
* memindahkan domain ownership;
* menciptakan authority baru.
 
⸻
 
3. PD-02 Performance Review Principle
Prinsip utama:
PD-02 Performance Review evaluates whether the Architecture Office is achieving its approved objectives and responsibilities with sufficient evidence, while preserving the authority and ownership boundaries of the Architecture domain.
Performance Review PD-02 harus menilai:
Objective
   ↓
Expected Condition
   ↓
Actual Condition
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Assessment
   ↓
Finding
   ↓
Response / Improvement Signal
 
⸻
 
4. Performance Review Boundary
E5 memiliki ownership atas performance review mechanism PD-02.
E5 tidak memiliki automatic authority untuk:
* mengubah architecture;
* mengubah architecture decision;
* mengubah architecture boundary;
* mengubah organizational ownership;
* mengubah governance policy;
* mengubah enterprise strategy;
* menjalankan architecture implementation;
* menjalankan operational execution.
Boundary:
Performance Review
        ↓
Assessment
        ↓
Finding
        ↓
Authorized Owner / Authority
        ↓
Decision / Action
 
⸻
 
5. PD-02 Review Scope
Performance Review PD-02 mencakup lima performance dimensions:
Strategic Performance
Operational Performance
Governance Performance
Capability Performance
Improvement Performance
Review harus melihat hubungan antar-dimension.
Contoh:
Capability Gap
      ↓
Operational Performance Decline
      ↓
Strategic Objective Risk
Finding tidak boleh berhenti pada satu metric apabila material context menunjukkan hubungan lintas dimension.
 
⸻
 
6. Strategic Performance Review
Strategic Performance Review mengevaluasi apakah PD-02 memenuhi strategic objectives yang telah disahkan.
Untuk PD-02, review harus dapat menelusuri performance terhadap strategic themes:
ST-01 Architecture Integrity
ST-02 Architecture Model & Structural Coherence
ST-03 Domain Boundary & Architectural Alignment
ST-04 Architecture Decision Integrity
ST-05 Reference Architecture & Controlled Evolution
Review tidak menciptakan strategic objective baru.
 
⸻
 
7. Operational Performance Review
Operational Performance Review mengevaluasi apakah Architecture Office menjalankan operating responsibilities secara efektif, reliable, timely, dan proportionate.
Area dapat mencakup:
* architecture review flow;
* architecture decision flow;
* architecture coordination;
* architecture control;
* architecture operating rhythm;
* architecture collaboration;
* operational improvement.
Performance Review tidak menjadi operational executor.
 
⸻
 
8. Governance Performance Review
Governance Performance Review mengevaluasi apakah performance-related governance dalam PD-02 berjalan sebagaimana ditetapkan.
Review dapat mencakup:
* authority compliance;
* review integrity;
* accountability;
* escalation;
* decision traceability;
* governance condition;
* cross-platform governance interface.
Governance finding tetap dapat memerlukan Governance Review.
 
⸻
 
9. Capability Performance Review
Capability Performance Review mengevaluasi capability PD-02 sebagaimana ditetapkan dalam Organization Architecture.
Review dapat mencakup:
* capability effectiveness;
* capability readiness;
* capability sufficiency;
* capability sustainability;
* capability dependency.
Capability owner tetap mengikuti Part B.
Performance Review tidak mengubah capability ownership.
 
⸻
 
10. Improvement Performance Review
Improvement Performance Review mengevaluasi apakah improvement yang dilakukan benar-benar menghasilkan intended improvement.
Canonical rule:
Action Completed
      ≠
Improvement Effective
Effectiveness harus diverifikasi melalui re-measurement.
 
⸻
 
11. Review Cycle Architecture
PD-02 menggunakan multi-level review cycle.
Continuous
    ↓
Periodic
    ↓
Executive
    ↓
Annual / Maturity
    ↓
Event-Based
Review cadence harus disesuaikan dengan materiality, operational context, dan approved performance requirements.
Cadence tidak boleh menciptakan authority baru.
 
⸻
 
12. Continuous Review
Continuous Review digunakan untuk mendeteksi material performance signal sebelum periodic review berikutnya.
Potential triggers:
* critical KPI deterioration;
* material architecture-performance gap;
* significant trend change;
* material governance exception;
* capability degradation;
* critical improvement failure;
* evidence anomaly;
* material cross-platform condition.
Continuous Review menghasilkan:
Signal
   ↓
Initial Assessment
   ↓
Monitor / Review / Escalate
Signal tidak otomatis menjadi decision.
 
⸻
 
13. Periodic Performance Review
Periodic Review digunakan untuk mengevaluasi performance dalam cadence yang telah ditetapkan.
Review package minimal mencakup:
* objective;
* success criteria;
* KPI;
* measurement result;
* trend;
* variance;
* evidence;
* context;
* previous findings;
* improvement status.
 
⸻
 
14. Executive Review
Executive Review menjadi formal interface ketika performance PD-02 memiliki:
* strategic implication;
* material risk;
* significant performance gap;
* cross-platform impact;
* unresolved accountability;
* material improvement requirement.
Executive Review tidak menggantikan Architecture Review.
 
⸻

20. Performance Gap
Performance Gap adalah perbedaan material antara:
Expected Condition
        vs
Actual Condition
Gap harus mempertimbangkan:
* materiality;
* duration;
* trend;
* scope;
* impact;
* confidence.
Tidak semua variance merupakan material performance gap.

22. Root Cause Boundary
Performance Review dapat mengidentifikasi:
* suspected cause;
* contributing factor;
* dependency;
* constraint;
* evidence gap.
Namun:
Suspected cause shall not be represented as validated root cause without sufficient evidence.
Jika root cause belum tervalidasi:
Observed Condition
      ↓
Possible Cause
      ↓
Further Analysis

24. Review Failure States
Review harus dapat berhenti atau menunda conclusion apabila:
Insufficient Evidence
Measurement Invalid
Criteria Missing
Objective Ambiguous
Authority Unclear
Material Context Missing
Dalam keadaan tersebut:
Do not manufacture a performance conclusion.
Review harus menyatakan limitation yang sebenarnya.

25. Review Independence
Reviewer harus memiliki independence yang proporsional terhadap materiality review.
Reviewer berfungsi untuk:
* challenge;
* validation;
* evidence scrutiny;
* assessment challenge;
* conclusion review.
Reviewer tidak boleh secara otomatis menjadi:
* decision owner;
* execution owner;
* target owner;
untuk matter yang sama apabila hal tersebut merusak independence yang diperlukan.

26. Performance Review vs Architecture Review
PD-02 memiliki Architecture Review sebagai bagian dari domain Architecture.
Performance Review berbeda.
Performance Review
        ↓
"How well is PD-02 performing?"
Architecture Review:
Architecture Review
        ↓
"Is the architecture valid / aligned?"
Performance Review dapat menemukan:
“Performance PD-02 rendah karena architecture constraint.”
Maka:
Performance Finding
        ↓
Architecture Dependency Identified
        ↓
Architecture Review
        ↓
Architecture Decision
Performance Review tidak mengubah architecture.
Architecture Review tetap memiliki authority sesuai Architecture Governance.
 
⸻
 
27. Performance Review vs Strategic Review
Aspect	Performance Review	Strategic Review
Primary Question	Apakah PD-02 mencapai expected performance?	Apakah strategic direction masih tepat?
Input	Metrics, evidence, outcomes	Performance + strategic context
Output	Assessment, finding, improvement signal	Strategic assessment / direction
Primary Owner	Performance Owner	Strategic Owner
Authority	Performance evaluation	Strategic authority
Performance Review dapat memicu Strategic Review.
Performance Review tidak menggantikan Strategic Review.
 
⸻
 
28. Performance Review vs Governance Review
Aspect	Performance Review	Governance Review
Primary Question	Apakah performance sesuai expectation?	Apakah governance mechanism berjalan efektif?
Focus	Performance condition	Authority / control / compliance
Output	Performance Assessment	Governance Assessment
Authority	Performance evaluation	Governance authority
Satu finding dapat memerlukan kedua review.
Performance Gap
      ↓
Suspected Governance Condition
      ↓
Performance Finding
      +
Governance Review
 
⸻

62. Review Closure Criteria
Review dapat ditutup apabila:
1. scope telah selesai;
2. evidence telah dinilai;
3. conclusion telah ditetapkan atau limitation dicatat;
4. findings telah dicatat;
5. required decisions telah diarahkan kepada authority yang tepat;
6. improvement actions telah memiliki owner jika diperlukan;
7. follow-up telah ditetapkan;
8. review record lengkap;
9. status telah ditetapkan.
Review tidak boleh ditutup hanya karena meeting selesai.

63. Review Success Criteria
E5 PD-02 dianggap berhasil apabila:
1. setiap formal Performance Review memiliki objective dan scope yang jelas;
2. review cycle dapat dijalankan sesuai cadence yang berlaku;
3. setiap performance assessment memiliki evidence traceability;
4. actual condition dapat dibedakan dari expected condition;
5. current state dapat dibedakan dari trend;
6. materiality diperhitungkan;
7. performance gap dapat diklasifikasikan;
8. suspected cause tidak dinyatakan sebagai validated root cause tanpa evidence;
9. Executive Review memiliki owner, reviewer, evidence, dan authority yang jelas;
10. Platform Review menghasilkan integrated assessment, bukan sekadar KPI average;
11. improvement decision dapat ditelusuri kembali ke finding;
12. improvement completion dibedakan dari improvement effectiveness;
13. target adjustment tidak dilakukan secara unilateral oleh Performance Management;
14. governance, architecture, strategy, dan operational boundaries tetap terjaga;
15. critical findings dapat dieskalasikan;
16. insufficient evidence dapat menghentikan conclusion;
17. improvement dapat diverifikasi melalui re-measurement;
18. review history dapat diaudit;
19. cross-platform findings tetap mempertahankan domain ownership;
20. Architecture Review tetap menjadi authority mechanism untuk architectural validity;
21. Performance Review tidak mengambil alih Architecture Authority;
22. framework dapat diwariskan ke Platform lain dengan domain adaptation;
23. tidak ada performance conclusion yang dibuat tanpa evidence yang memadai.
 
⸻
 
64. Canonical Boundary Rules
Rule 1 — Review Does Not Create Authority
Performance Review tidak menciptakan decision authority.
Rule 2 — Finding Is Not Decision
Finding bukan decision.
Rule 3 — Recommendation Is Not Decision
Recommendation bukan decision.
Rule 4 — Performance Review Is Not Architecture Review
Performance Review menilai performance.
Architecture Review menilai architectural validity.
Rule 5 — Evidence Before Conclusion
Tidak boleh ada conclusion tanpa evidence yang cukup.
Rule 6 — Action Completion Is Not Effectiveness
Improvement harus diverifikasi melalui re-measurement.
Rule 7 — Performance Failure Does Not Transfer Accountability
Accountability tetap mengikuti approved ownership.
Rule 8 — Cross-Platform Finding Does Not Transfer Domain Ownership
Dependency tidak sama dengan ownership.
 
⸻
 
65. Canonical Review Integrity Rule
A PD-02 Performance Review conclusion shall be issued only when the objective, criterion, measurement, evidence, context, and applicable authority basis are sufficiently established to support the conclusion.
 
⸻
 
66. Canonical Authority Boundary Rule
Performance Review may evaluate and recommend, but it shall not unilaterally alter Architecture Authority, Canonical Architecture, Architecture Decisions, governance authority, strategic direction, domain ownership, or operational execution.
 
⸻
 
67. Canonical Improvement Rule
An improvement action shall not be considered effective merely because the action is completed; effectiveness shall be assessed through subsequent evidence and re-measurement.
 
⸻
 
68. Canonical Evidence Rule
When evidence is insufficient, PD-02 shall record the limitation and defer, qualify, or withhold the performance conclusion rather than manufacture a result.
