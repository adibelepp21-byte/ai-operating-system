1. Purpose
C5 menetapkan Architecture Authority Governance sebagai constitutional mechanism untuk menentukan siapa yang memiliki hak sah untuk:
* melakukan;
* mendefinisikan;
* mereview;
* memutuskan;
* menyetujui;
* menolak;
* memberikan condition;
* melakukan override;
* mendelegasikan;
* mengubah;
* mencabut;
* atau mengeskalasikan
suatu Architecture matter dalam domain kewenangan PD-02.
C5 menjawab:
Siapa yang berhak melakukan apa terhadap suatu Architecture matter, berdasarkan authority apa, dalam scope apa, dengan batas apa, dan melalui precedence apa?
C5 melengkapi pemisahan:
C3
Delegation
    ↓
C4
Accountability
    ↓
C5
Authority
C5 tidak menggantikan C2 Decision Governance.
C2 menjawab:
Bagaimana decision dibuat dan dikelola?
C5 menjawab:
Siapa yang memiliki legal/organizational right untuk melakukan atau mengesahkan action tersebut?

3. Authority Model
PD-02 menggunakan AIOS Authority Model (AUM).
AUM membedakan tujuh authority classes:
1. Constitutional Authority
2. Governance Authority
3. Decision Authority
4. Approval Authority
5. Override Authority
6. Delegated Authority
7. Advisory Authority
Model tersebut digunakan untuk mencegah satu authority concept mengambil alih fungsi authority lain.
 
⸻
 
4. Constitutional Authority
Constitutional Authority merupakan authority yang berasal dari:
* AIOS Architecture Baseline;
* Platform Division definition;
* Architecture Charter;
* approved AIOS Authority Model.
Untuk PD-02, Constitutional Authority memberikan legitimacy terhadap:
* system structure;
* domain boundary;
* architecture consistency;
* reference architecture;
* canonical architecture;
* architecture review;
* canonical architectural artifacts.
Constitutional Authority PD-02 tidak meluas menjadi:
* enterprise strategic authority;
* general governance authority;
* operational authority;
* ownership atas Platform Division lain.
 
⸻
 
5. Domain Authority
Primary domain authority PD-02 adalah:
Architecture Authority
        │
        ├── System Structure
        ├── Domain Boundary
        ├── Architecture Consistency
        └── Reference Architecture
Domain Authority tersebut menjadi basis bagi architecture-related decisions dan actions.
Authority di luar Architecture domain harus menggunakan applicable authority owner.
 
⸻
 
6. Decision Authority
PD-02 memiliki Decision Authority terhadap material Architecture decisions yang berada dalam Architecture domain.
Decision Authority mencakup:
* architectural structure;
* architectural boundary;
* architectural consistency;
* reference architecture;
* canonical architecture;
* architecture standards;
* architecture decisions;
* architecture assessment outcomes;
* architectural deviation decisions dalam authorized scope.
Decision Authority harus memenuhi:
1. berada dalam Architecture domain;
2. memiliki architectural basis;
3. memiliki defined decision class;
4. memiliki applicable authority;
5. dapat ditelusuri terhadap evidence;
6. memiliki decision record apabila material.
 
⸻
 
7. Decision Authority Boundary
PD-02 dapat memutuskan architectural matters seperti:
* apakah architectural design aligned dengan Canonical Architecture;
* apakah architectural change membutuhkan Architecture Decision;
* apakah domain boundary acceptable secara arsitektural;
* apakah architecture artifact memenuhi architectural standard;
* apakah architecture deviation material;
* apakah architecture review menghasilkan acceptance, condition, rejection, atau escalation.
PD-02 tidak dapat secara sepihak memutuskan:
* enterprise strategic direction;
* general governance policy;
* enterprise resource allocation;
* operational execution;
* security policy;
* quality acceptance di luar architecture domain;
* implementation ownership Platform lain.
 
⸻
 
8. Approval Authority
PD-02 memiliki Approval Authority terhadap architectural artifacts dan decisions yang sepenuhnya berada dalam Architecture domain.
Approval dapat mencakup:
* Canonical Architecture;
* Architecture Blueprint;
* Architecture Standards;
* Architecture Decision Record;
* Reference Architecture;
* architectural baseline changes;
* architecture review outcomes.
Approval hanya sah apabila:
Decision Domain
        =
Authorized Architecture Domain
Jika decision membutuhkan authority dari domain lain, approval PD-02 tidak menggantikan authority tersebut.
 
⸻
 
9. Architecture Approval Boundary
Canonical rule:
Architecture approval confirms architectural validity; it does not automatically authorize implementation, funding, governance policy, or operational execution.
Relationship:
Architecture Approval
        ↓
Architectural Validity
        │
        ├── Governance Approval
        ├── Resource Approval
        └── Operational Authorization
              may still be required
Karena itu:
Architecture Approval
        ≠
Implementation Authorization
Approval PD-02 tidak memberikan hak kepada PD-02 untuk memerintahkan implementation domain lain.
 
⸻
 
10. Rejection Authority
Dalam domain Architecture, PD-02 dapat menetapkan:
* rejection;
* conditional acceptance;
* remediation requirement;
* escalation;
apabila hasil review menunjukkan bahwa architecture matter tidak memenuhi applicable architectural requirements.
Rejection hanya berlaku terhadap:
Architectural Validity
Rejection tersebut tidak otomatis:
* membatalkan enterprise strategy;
* membatalkan governance policy;
* membatalkan resource decision;
* memerintahkan operational shutdown;
* mengambil alih implementation ownership.
 
⸻
 
11. Veto Boundary
C5 tidak menciptakan Veto Authority sebagai authority class independen di luar AUM.
Apabila suatu Architecture action harus dihentikan atau tidak dapat disahkan, efek tersebut harus berasal dari authority yang sah, misalnya:
Decision Authority
        ↓
Reject

Approval Authority
        ↓
Withhold Approval

Override Authority
        ↓
Reverse / Supersede
Karena itu:
Veto effect must derive from legitimate authority; the label “veto” does not create authority by itself.
Tidak ada blanket Architecture veto terhadap:
* Executive Authority;
* Governance Authority;
* Security Authority;
* Quality Authority;
* operational execution;
* domain ownership Platform lain.
 
⸻
 
12. Override Authority
PD-02 memiliki limited Override Authority dalam domain Architecture.
Override dapat digunakan terhadap:
* architecture decision dalam authorized scope;
* architectural interpretation;
* architectural deviation;
* architectural approval;
* architectural inconsistency.
Override harus memiliki:
1. authority basis;
2. decision reference;
3. rationale;
4. affected scope;
5. affected parties;
6. effective state;
7. review condition;
8. traceable record.
 
⸻
 
13. Override Limitation
Architecture Override tidak boleh digunakan untuk:
* membatalkan Executive Authority;
* mengganti enterprise strategic direction;
* membatalkan Governance Authority;
* mengambil alih Security Authority;
* mengambil alih Quality Authority;
* memaksa operational execution;
* mengambil alih domain ownership Platform lain.
Canonical principle:
Architecture Override
        ≠
Enterprise Override
 
⸻
 
14. Delegated Authority
PD-02 dapat mendelegasikan authority secara formal apabila diperlukan.
Delegation harus menetapkan:
* Delegating Authority;
* Delegated Authority;
* authority class;
* decision class;
* scope;
* constraints;
* effective date;
* expiry/review date;
* record identifier.
Delegation tidak mengubah organizational ownership.
Delegation juga tidak secara otomatis menghapus accountability dari delegating authority.
 
⸻
 
15. Delegation Boundary
Delegated authority harus:
Within
Architecture Domain
        +
Within
Defined Scope
        +

Defined Time
Delegated authority tidak boleh:
* diperluas melalui asumsi;
* digunakan untuk domain lain;
* diteruskan tanpa authorization;
* tetap aktif setelah expiry;
* melebihi authority delegator.

18. Executive Authority Boundary

19. Cross-Platform Authority
PD-02 memiliki cross-platform architectural authority, bukan cross-platform operational authority.
PD-02 dapat:
* review architectural dependency;
* review cross-platform architecture;
* define architectural boundary;
* define architectural interface;
* identify architectural conflict;
* issue architectural decision dalam authorized Architecture domain;
* require architectural remediation dalam applicable scope;
* escalate unresolved cross-domain matters.
PD-02 tidak dapat:
* mengoperasikan Platform lain;
* mengatur staffing Platform lain;
* mengambil alih resource allocation;
* mengambil alih operational workflow;
* menjadi domain owner Platform lain.

24. Authority Conflict
Authority conflict terjadi apabila:
* dua authority mengklaim action yang sama;
* authority boundary tidak jelas;
* authority dan ownership bertentangan;
* decision membutuhkan multiple authority;
* delegated authority bertentangan dengan parent authority;
* architecture authority bertentangan dengan applicable governance authority.
PD-02 tidak boleh menyelesaikan conflict dengan asumsi:
Architecture selalu superior.
Conflict harus dipetakan berdasarkan:
1. domain;
2. authority type;
3. decision class;
4. applicable precedence;
5. escalation mechanism.
 
⸻
 
25. Authority Escalation
Escalation diperlukan apabila:
1. issue berada di luar Architecture Authority;
2. authority conflict tidak dapat diselesaikan;
3. terdapat cross-domain decision;
4. terdapat strategic implication;
5. terdapat governance implication;
6. terdapat material unresolved architecture risk;
7. terdapat authority ambiguity.
Canonical flow:
Architecture Issue
        ↓
Authority Classification
        ↓
Within PD-02 Authority?
   ┌────────┴────────┐
  YES                NO
   ↓                  ↓
DECIDE             ESCALATE
                      ↓
             Applicable Authority
Escalation tidak menciptakan authority baru.
 
⸻
 
26. Authority Constraints
PD-02 wajib mematuhi:
26.1 Domain Constraint
Authority hanya digunakan dalam authorized Architecture domain.
26.2 Constitutional Constraint
Tidak ada authority yang boleh diciptakan tanpa constitutional basis.
26.3 Evidence Constraint
Material authority action harus memiliki evidence yang memadai.
26.4 Scope Constraint
Authority tidak boleh digunakan melampaui defined scope.
26.5 Delegation Constraint
Delegated authority tidak boleh melebihi authority delegator.
26.6 Override Constraint
Override harus memiliki explicit authority basis dan documented rationale.
26.7 Governance Constraint
Architecture Authority tetap tunduk pada applicable governance.
26.8 Independence Constraint
Architecture review harus mempertahankan independence yang diperlukan oleh materiality dan risk.

27. Authority and Accountability Separation
C4 menetapkan accountability.
C5 menetapkan authority.
C4
Accountability
        ↓
Responsibility for Result

C5
Authority
        ↓
Right to Act / Decide
Seseorang dapat:
* memiliki authority tanpa menjadi final accountable owner;
* memiliki accountability tanpa menjalankan seluruh authority;
* menjalankan delegated authority tanpa memperoleh ownership.
 
⸻
 
28. Authority and Ownership Separation
Authority
    ≠
Ownership
PD-02 memiliki authority terhadap Architecture.
PD-02 juga memiliki ownership atas canonical architectural artifacts.
Namun PD-02 tidak memiliki ownership atas:
* runtime;
* AI engineering;
* infrastructure;
* security;
* quality;
* domain Platform lain.
Cross-platform architecture authority tidak memindahkan organizational ownership.
 
⸻

Architecture Authority
        ↓

        ↓
Implementation Domain Owner
        ↓

Jika implementation menyimpang:
Deviation
   ↓
Architecture Assessment
   ↓
Remediation / Reject / Condition / Escalate
PD-02 tidak otomatis menjadi executor.

36. Authority Revocation
Authority dapat dicabut apabila:
* authority tidak lagi valid;
* domain ownership berubah;
* charter berubah;
* delegated scope berakhir;
* authority model berubah;
* formal governance decision mengubah boundary.
Revocation harus terdokumentasi.

37. Emergency Authority
Dalam emergency architecture condition, PD-02 dapat melakukan immediate architectural assessment apabila terdapat material architecture risk.
Emergency condition tidak menciptakan unrestricted authority.
Emergency action harus:
1. memiliki context reference;
2. memiliki defined scope;
3. memiliki rationale;
4. dicatat;
5. ditinjau setelah kondisi emergency;
6. dieskalasikan jika berada di luar authority.
Emergency authority tidak dapat digunakan untuk mengambil alih enterprise strategy atau operational execution.

46. Authority and Independent Review
Material review harus mempertimbangkan independence.
Jika reviewer:
* membuat decision yang sedang direview;
* menjalankan implementation yang sedang direview;
* memiliki conflict of interest material;
maka review independence harus dinilai.
Jika independence required tetapi tidak tersedia:
ESCALATE — do not silently accept the conflict.

57. Canonical Authority Statement
PD-02 Architecture Office holds Architecture Authority for system structure, domain boundary, architecture consistency, reference architecture, and associated canonical architectural artifacts and decisions within its authorized domain. PD-02 may define, review, decide, approve, reject, condition, maintain, delegate, override within bounded authority, and escalate architectural matters within that scope, but shall not use Architecture Authority to assume enterprise strategy, governance policy, operational execution, or domain ownership belonging to other Platform Divisions.
Versi Bahasa Indonesia:
PD-02 Architecture Office memegang Architecture Authority atas system structure, domain boundary, architecture consistency, reference architecture, serta architectural artifacts dan decisions canonical yang berada dalam domain kewenangannya. PD-02 dapat mendefinisikan, mereview, memutuskan, menyetujui, menolak, memberikan condition, memelihara, mendelegasikan, melakukan override dalam batas kewenangan yang sah, dan melakukan eskalasi atas hal-hal arsitektural dalam scope tersebut, tetapi tidak boleh menggunakan Architecture Authority untuk mengambil alih enterprise strategy, governance policy, operational execution, atau domain ownership Platform Division lainnya.

58. Canonical Authority Principle
No authority without basis. No authority beyond scope. No delegation beyond granted authority. No approval beyond applicable domain. No override beyond legitimate authority. No architectural authority becomes operational ownership.
Versi Bahasa Indonesia:
Tidak ada authority tanpa basis. Tidak ada authority di luar scope. Tidak ada delegation yang melebihi authority yang diberikan. Tidak ada approval di luar domain yang berlaku. Tidak ada override di luar authority yang sah. Dan tidak ada Architecture Authority yang berubah menjadi operational ownership.
