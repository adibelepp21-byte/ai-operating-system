1. Purpose
C6 menetapkan Architecture Review Governance sebagai mekanisme formal untuk menggunakan Architecture Authority dalam mengevaluasi architectural matters.
C6 menjawab:
Bagaimana Architecture Review dilakukan, siapa yang melakukan review, apa yang dinilai, evidence apa yang diperlukan, bagaimana finding dan outcome ditetapkan, kapan approval atau condition dapat diberikan, dan bagaimana independence review dijaga?
C6 tidak menciptakan authority baru.
C6 menggunakan authority yang telah ditetapkan pada C5.

3. Review Philosophy
Architecture Review mengikuti prinsip:
ARP-01 — Review Before Evolution
Perubahan arsitektur yang material harus direview sebelum evolution tersebut dianggap architecturally accepted.
ARP-02 — Evidence-Based Review
Kesimpulan review harus berdasarkan evidence dan artefak yang dapat diverifikasi.
ARP-03 — Independent Validation
Review yang material harus memiliki validation yang independen secara proporsional terhadap materiality.
ARP-04 — Boundary Preservation
Review tidak boleh mengubah ownership domain hanya karena suatu matter masuk dalam review.
ARP-05 — Action-Oriented Review
Review harus menghasilkan outcome yang dapat digunakan untuk decision, remediation, escalation, monitoring, atau further review.
ARP-06 — Traceable Review
Setiap material conclusion harus dapat ditelusuri ke criteria, evidence, finding, dan authority basis.
Prinsip-prinsip tersebut merupakan penerapan domain Architecture dari baseline review principles AIOS.
 
⸻
 
4. Architecture Review Purpose
Architecture Review digunakan untuk memastikan bahwa:
* architecture tetap aligned dengan Architecture Baseline;
* domain boundary tetap coherent;
* architectural structure tetap konsisten;
* architectural dependency dapat dipahami;
* architectural evolution tidak menciptakan structural contradiction;
* architectural decision memiliki evidence yang memadai;
* canonical architecture tetap dapat dipertahankan.
Baseline mendefinisikan Architecture Review untuk menjaga compliance terhadap Architecture Baseline, platform boundary, organization architecture, capability evolution, dan structural consistency.
 
⸻
 
5. Review Scope
Architecture Review mencakup sekurang-kurangnya:
1. Architecture Baseline compliance;
2. Platform boundary compliance;
3. Organization architecture alignment;
4. Capability evolution;
5. Structural consistency.
Untuk PD-02, review juga dapat memeriksa:
* structural impact;
* domain boundary impact;
* dependency impact;
* architectural consistency;
* alignment terhadap Canonical Architecture.
 
⸻
 
6. Review Trigger
Architecture Review dapat dipicu oleh:
* material architectural change;
* proposed structural change;
* domain boundary change;
* capability evolution dengan architectural impact;
* cross-platform dependency;
* architectural inconsistency;
* architecture deviation;
* material Architecture Decision;
* change terhadap canonical architectural artifact;
* architecture compliance concern;
* formal Architecture Review request.
Tidak semua perubahan otomatis membutuhkan full Architecture Review.
Materiality menentukan depth dan level review.
 
⸻
 
7. Materiality
Materiality digunakan untuk menentukan tingkat review yang diperlukan.
Materiality mempertimbangkan:
* architectural scope;
* affected domain;
* cross-platform impact;
* structural impact;
* dependency impact;
* reversibility;
* risk;
* canonical artifact impact;
* strategic implication.
Model:
Low Materiality
      ↓
Local Architecture Review

Medium Materiality
      ↓
Formal Architecture Review

High Materiality
      ↓
Material Architecture Review
      ↓
Architecture Authority / Applicable Escalation
Materiality tidak mengubah ownership.
 
⸻
 
8. Review Levels
Architecture Review dapat dilaksanakan pada beberapa level.
Level 1 — Local Architecture Review
Untuk matter dengan:
* limited scope;
* limited dependency;
* low structural impact.
Level 2 — Formal Architecture Review
Untuk matter dengan:
* material architecture impact;
* cross-team impact;
* meaningful dependency;
* canonical artifact impact.
Level 3 — Cross-Platform Architecture Review
Untuk matter dengan:
* multiple Platform impact;
* domain boundary interaction;
* shared architectural dependency;
* architectural conflict.
Level 4 — Escalated Architecture Review
Untuk matter dengan:
* unresolved authority conflict;
* strategic implication;
* governance implication;
* material architectural risk;
* unresolved cross-domain conflict.
Level review menentukan review depth dan escalation context.
Level tidak menciptakan authority baru.

9. Review Ownership Model
Architecture Review menggunakan role separation berikut:
Role	Primary Responsibility
Architecture Owner	Memimpin Architecture Review
Architecture Reviewer	Melakukan independent validation / challenge
Domain Owner	Menyediakan evidence dan context domain
Architecture Authority	Menetapkan architectural outcome dalam authority scope
Custodian	Menjaga evidence dan review record
Executive Authority	Menentukan response apabila matter berada dalam executive authority boundary
Model Architecture Review ini mengikuti baseline review ownership structure.

10. Architecture Owner
Architecture Owner bertanggung jawab untuk:
* memastikan review dijalankan;
* menentukan review scope;
* memastikan criteria tersedia;
* memastikan evidence dikumpulkan;
* memastikan reviewer ditetapkan;
* memastikan finding terdokumentasi;
* memastikan outcome memiliki authority basis;
* memastikan follow-up ditetapkan.
Architecture Owner tidak boleh menghilangkan independent validation hanya karena review berada dalam domain Architecture.
 
⸻
 
11. Architecture Reviewer
Architecture Reviewer bertanggung jawab untuk:
* memeriksa evidence;
* menguji criteria;
* melakukan challenge;
* mengidentifikasi inconsistency;
* menguji architectural reasoning;
* memvalidasi conclusion;
* mengidentifikasi missing evidence;
* menyatakan limitation review.
Reviewer tidak boleh secara otomatis menjadi:
* implementation owner;
* decision owner;
* change executor;
untuk matter yang sama apabila hal tersebut menciptakan material conflict of interest.
 
⸻
 
12. Reviewer Independence
Independence bersifat proportional to materiality.
Semakin material review:
Higher Materiality
        ↓
Higher Independence Requirement
Reviewer harus cukup independen dari:
* implementation interest;
* direct delivery responsibility;
* outcome incentive;
* decision bias;
untuk dapat melakukan meaningful challenge.
Jika independence yang diperlukan tidak tersedia:
Independence Gap
        ↓
Document
        ↓
Escalate / Assign Alternative Reviewer
Review tidak boleh diam-diam dianggap independent apabila condition tersebut tidak terpenuhi.

Domain Owner tetap mempertahankan ownership atas domainnya.

17. Review Criteria

* Canonical Architecture;
* Architecture Standards;
* Architecture Blueprint;
* approved Architecture Decision;
* applicable Architecture Charter;
* approved architectural constraints;

Jika criteria tidak tersedia atau ambiguous:
Do not manufacture a review criterion.
Review harus:
* meminta clarification;
* identify limitation;
* atau escalate sesuai authority.

26. Conditional Acceptance

27. Architecture Approval
Architecture Review dapat menjadi input bagi Architecture Approval.
Approval hanya sah apabila berada dalam authority scope C5.
Canonical rule:
Architecture approval confirms architectural validity; it does not automatically authorize implementation, funding, governance policy, or operational execution.
Karena itu:
Architecture Approval
        ≠
Implementation Authorization
 
⸻
 
28. Rejection
Architecture Review dapat merekomendasikan rejection apabila evidence menunjukkan bahwa architectural matter tidak memenuhi applicable requirement dan tidak terdapat legitimate basis untuk acceptance.
Rejection harus:
* memiliki criteria;
* memiliki evidence;
* memiliki finding;
* memiliki authority basis;
* memiliki affected scope;
* memiliki record.
Rejection tidak boleh digunakan untuk mengambil alih execution domain.

31. Review Decision Boundary
Architecture Review dapat menghasilkan architectural decision apabila:
1. matter berada dalam Architecture Decision scope;
2. decision authority tersedia;
3. evidence cukup;
4. criteria jelas;
5. review selesai pada required depth.
Jika salah satu tidak terpenuhi:
Do Not Force Decision

* Further Evidence Required;
* Further Review Required;
* Awaiting Authority;
* Escalated.

* boundary;

        ↓

38. Cross-Platform Architecture Review
Cross-platform review digunakan ketika architectural matter memengaruhi lebih dari satu Platform.
Review harus mengidentifikasi:
* participating Platforms;
* ownership masing-masing;
* architectural dependency;
* decision authority;
* approval authority;
* implementation owner;
* unresolved conflict.
Canonical rule:
Cross-platform review does not create cross-platform ownership.

40. Architecture Review Independence Matrix
Situation	Independence Requirement
Low-materiality local review	Basic challenge
Material architecture review	Independent reviewer
Cross-platform material review	Reviewer independent dari affected implementation interests
High-risk architectural review	Enhanced independence
Authority conflict	Reviewer harus bebas dari conflicting authority interest

Independence harus dinilai berdasarkan materiality, bukan sekadar jabatan.

41. Conflict of Interest
Reviewer harus mengungkapkan material conflict apabila reviewer:
* memiliki direct implementation responsibility;
* memiliki material outcome incentive;
* merupakan sole decision-maker atas matter;
* memiliki ownership yang dapat memengaruhi conclusion.
Jika conflict material:
Conflict Identified
        ↓
Assess Materiality
        ↓
Mitigate / Replace Reviewer
        ↓
Document

69. Canonical Architecture Review Statement
PD-02 Architecture Office provides Architecture Review for material architectural changes, decisions, structures, boundaries, dependencies, and canonical architecture alignment within its authorized domain. Architecture Review is evidence-based, proportionate to materiality, independently validated where required, and traceable from criteria through evidence, assessment, findings, and authorized outcome. Architecture Review may produce assessment, finding, condition, recommendation, approval, rejection, or escalation within applicable authority, but shall not assume operational execution, implementation ownership, governance-policy ownership, enterprise strategic authority, or domain ownership belonging to another Platform Division.
Versi Bahasa Indonesia:
PD-02 Architecture Office menyediakan Architecture Review atas perubahan, keputusan, struktur, boundary, dependency, dan alignment terhadap canonical architecture yang memiliki material architectural impact dalam domain kewenangannya. Architecture Review dilaksanakan berbasis evidence, proporsional terhadap materiality, memperoleh validasi independen apabila diperlukan, dan dapat ditelusuri dari criteria melalui evidence, assessment, findings, hingga outcome yang sah. Architecture Review dapat menghasilkan assessment, finding, condition, recommendation, approval, rejection, atau escalation dalam authority yang berlaku, tetapi tidak boleh mengambil alih operational execution, implementation ownership, governance-policy ownership, enterprise strategic authority, maupun domain ownership Platform Division lainnya.
