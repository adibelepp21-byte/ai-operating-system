# 1. Purpose

D4 menetapkan model baku pengelolaan seluruh pekerjaan PD-02 Architecture Office.

D4 menjawab:

> **Bagaimana Architecture Office mengelola pekerjaan dari inisiasi sampai penutupan secara terstruktur, traceable, dan terkendali?**

D4 mencakup:

- work initiation;
- work classification;
- planning;
- prioritization;
- assignment;
- execution;
- tracking;
- review;
- state management;
- closure.

D4 menjadi Single Source of Truth untuk Work Management PD-02.

D4 tidak menggantikan Governance Architecture, Architecture Decision Governance, Performance Architecture, atau organizational lifecycle.

---

# 2. Work Management Boundary

Work Management PD-02 berlaku terhadap pekerjaan yang berada dalam Architecture domain.

Contoh:

- architecture modeling;
- architecture assessment;
- architecture review work;
- architecture decision preparation;
- Architecture Blueprint work;
- Architecture Decision Record work;
- Architecture Standards work;
- Reference Architecture work;
- domain-boundary analysis;
- architecture consistency analysis;
- architectural change work;
- cross-platform architecture work;
- architecture improvement work.

Work yang berada di domain Platform lain tetap menjadi ownership Platform tersebut.

---

# 3. Fundamental Work Management Principle

Prinsip utama:

> **Every material Architecture Office work item must have a defined purpose, owner, state, expected outcome, and closure condition.**

Work tidak boleh dianggap managed hanya karena telah dicatat.

Work harus memiliki lifecycle yang dapat ditelusuri.

---

# 4. Work Management Principles

## 4.1 Explicit Work

Setiap material work harus diidentifikasi secara eksplisit.

---

## 4.2 Clear Ownership

Setiap work item harus memiliki accountable work owner.

Work ownership tidak sama dengan organizational domain ownership.

---

## 4.3 Purpose Before Execution

Work harus memiliki tujuan atau expected outcome sebelum execution dimulai.

---

## 4.4 Evidence-Based Progress

Progress harus didasarkan pada evidence, bukan sekadar self-reported percentage.

---

## 4.5 Controlled State

Setiap work item harus memiliki state yang merepresentasikan kondisi aktualnya.

---

## 4.6 Proportional Management

Management intensity harus proporsional terhadap:

- materiality;
- complexity;
- risk;
- dependency;
- urgency.

---

5. Work Classification Model
Work PD-02 minimal diklasifikasikan berdasarkan:
Work
 ├── Initiative
 ├── Work Item
 ├── Task
 ├── Review
 ├── Decision Support
 └── Improvement Work
Klasifikasi digunakan untuk menentukan treatment yang sesuai.
 
⸻
 
6. Initiative
Initiative adalah bounded body of Architecture work yang:
* memiliki objective;
* memiliki scope;
* memiliki owner;
* memiliki expected outcome;
* memiliki time boundary atau completion condition.
Contoh:
* Architecture Blueprint initiative;
* Reference Architecture update;
* Architecture Standards development;
* major architecture assessment;
* architecture transformation work.
 
⸻
 
7. Work Item
Work Item adalah unit pekerjaan yang dapat dikelola secara individual.
Minimum:
* Work ID;
* title;
* purpose;
* owner;
* priority;
* state;
* expected outcome.
 
⸻
 
8. Task
Task adalah unit eksekusi yang lebih granular yang mendukung Work Item atau Initiative.
Task tidak boleh menjadi pengganti Work Item ketika pekerjaan memiliki:
* material dependency;
* material risk;
* material decision;
* independent outcome.
 
⸻
 
9. Review Work
Review Work adalah pekerjaan yang bertujuan menghasilkan:
* assessment;
* finding;
* condition;
* recommendation;
* review outcome.
Review Work tidak otomatis menghasilkan decision authority.
 
⸻
 
10. Decision Support Work
Decision Support Work menghasilkan material yang diperlukan untuk architecture decision, misalnya:
* option analysis;
* impact analysis;
* dependency analysis;
* architecture assessment;
* recommendation.
Decision Support Work tidak sama dengan Decision Authority.
 
⸻
 
11. Improvement Work
Improvement Work bertujuan meningkatkan:
* architecture quality;
* architecture process;
* architecture consistency;
* architecture tooling;
* architecture operating mechanism.
Improvement Work harus memiliki measurable atau observable expected outcome apabila material.
 
⸻

13. Portfolio Management

Contoh portfolio:
* Architecture Model;
* Domain Architecture;
* Reference Architecture;
* Architecture Standards;
* Architecture Decision;
* Architecture Governance Support;
* Cross-Platform Architecture;
* Architecture Improvement.
Portfolio tidak menciptakan organizational authority.

Portfolio digunakan untuk:
* visibility;
* prioritization;
* dependency management;
* resource awareness;
* work balancing;
* strategic alignment.
Portfolio bukan organizational hierarchy.
 
⸻
 
15. Portfolio Ownership
Setiap portfolio harus memiliki accountable owner apabila digunakan sebagai management structure.
Portfolio owner bertanggung jawab terhadap coherence dan visibility portfolio.
Portfolio ownership tidak menggantikan authority yang ditetapkan Part C.

19. Work Qualification
Sebelum work masuk execution, minimal harus diketahui:
* purpose;
* scope;
* owner;
* expected outcome;
* required dependency;
* priority;
* applicable authority/review requirement.
Work yang belum qualified tidak boleh diperlakukan sebagai committed execution tanpa basis yang sah.
 
⸻
 
20. Work Prioritization
Prioritization mempertimbangkan:
1. strategic relevance;
2. architectural materiality;
3. architectural risk;
4. dependency impact;
5. urgency;
6. decision dependency;
7. expected value;
8. available capacity.
Prioritization tidak boleh digunakan untuk bypass mandatory governance.
 
⸻
 
21. Priority Classes
PD-02 dapat menggunakan:
Critical
High
Medium
Low
Interpretasi:
Critical
Material architecture concern yang membutuhkan immediate management attention.
High
Significant architecture work yang memiliki material dependency atau impact.
Medium
Normal planned architecture work.
Low
Work yang dapat dilakukan setelah higher-priority work.
Priority class harus dapat direview.
 
⸻
 
22. Assignment Model
Setiap material work harus memiliki:
* work owner;
* executor atau responsible party;
* reviewer jika required;
* supporting participant jika required.
Assignment harus jelas.
 
⸻
 
23. Assignment Boundary
Assignment:
Assignment
   ≠
Authority Transfer
Penugasan pekerjaan tidak:
* memberikan decision authority;
* memberikan approval authority;
* mengubah organizational ownership;
* mengubah accountability yang ditetapkan governance.
Jika delegated authority diperlukan, gunakan mekanisme delegation yang berlaku.
 
⸻
 
24. Assignment Rules
Work tidak boleh assigned apabila:
* owner tidak jelas;
* scope tidak dapat dipahami;
* required authority belum tersedia;
* dependency critical belum diketahui;
* assignment menyebabkan material conflict yang belum diselesaikan.
Exception terhadap kondisi tersebut harus ditangani melalui applicable governance mechanism.

27. Proposed

⸻
 
28. Qualified
Work telah dinilai cukup jelas untuk masuk planning.
 
⸻
 
29. Planned
Work telah memiliki treatment yang direncanakan tetapi belum masuk execution.
 
⸻
 
30. Assigned
Work telah memiliki owner/executor yang ditetapkan.
 
⸻
 
31. Ready
Prerequisite untuk memulai execution telah terpenuhi.
 
⸻
 
32. In Progress
Execution sedang berlangsung.
Progress harus memiliki evidence yang sesuai.
 
⸻
 
33. Review
Work telah menghasilkan output yang membutuhkan validation/review sebelum completion.
Review dapat berupa:
* work review;
* architecture review;
* quality check;
* stakeholder validation.
Jenis review harus dibedakan.
 
⸻
 
34. Completed
Expected execution outcome telah dicapai dan completion evidence tersedia.
Completed belum selalu berarti formally closed.
 
⸻
 
35. Closed
Closure condition telah terpenuhi.
Closure harus memastikan:
* outcome recorded;
* required artifact updated;
* outstanding condition resolved atau explicitly accepted;
* evidence retained;
* follow-up identified jika required.
 
⸻
 
36. Blocked
Work Blocked apabila execution tidak dapat dilanjutkan karena dependency, decision, resource, authority, information, atau condition material.
Blocked bukan Completed.
 
⸻
 
37. Deferred
Work Deferred apabila execution secara eksplisit ditunda untuk alasan yang dapat ditelusuri.
Deferred harus memiliki:
* reason;
* owner;
* expected reconsideration point.
 
⸻
 
38. Cancelled
Work Cancelled apabila execution dihentikan sebelum closure karena keputusan yang sah.
Cancellation harus memiliki reason dan recorded authority/context apabila material.
 
⸻

46. Work Constraints
Work Management PD-02 tidak boleh:
1. membuat authority baru;
2. mengambil alih Platform ownership;
3. bypass governance;
4. mengubah architecture baseline tanpa applicable authority;
5. mengubah organizational structure;
6. menggantikan architecture review;
7. menggantikan performance review;
8. memaksa implementation domain lain;
9. menyembunyikan blocked work;
10. menyatakan completion tanpa evidence.

* become deferred;
