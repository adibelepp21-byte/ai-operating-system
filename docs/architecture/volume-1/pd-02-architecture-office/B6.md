# 1. Purpose

B6 menetapkan **Reporting Hierarchy PD-02 Architecture Office**.

B6 menjawab:

> **Bagaimana organizational structure PD-02 terhubung melalui reporting relationship sehingga accountability, information flow, dan organizational coordination memiliki jalur yang jelas?**

B6 menjadi structural relationship layer setelah:

```text
B1 — Organizational Model
        ↓
B2 — Architecture Sub Division Architecture
        ↓
B3 — Architecture Capability Architecture
        ↓
B4 — Architecture Team Architecture
        ↓
B5 — Architecture Role Group Architecture
        ↓
B6 — Reporting Hierarchy
B6 tidak mengubah struktur yang telah ditetapkan B1–B5.
B6 hanya menetapkan hubungan pelaporan dan accountability di antara organizational elements tersebut.
 
⸻
 
2. Reporting Hierarchy Principle
PD-02 menggunakan prinsip:
Every organizational unit must have a clear primary reporting relationship.
Reporting hierarchy harus memungkinkan:
* accountability;
* information flow;
* organizational coordination;
* status reporting;
* performance reporting;
* issue routing;
* escalation routing.
Reporting hierarchy harus tetap sederhana, dapat ditelusuri, dan tidak menciptakan authority baru.
 
⸻

Architecture Role Group

⸻
 
5. Primary Reporting Relationship
Primary reporting relationship adalah hubungan formal yang digunakan untuk menetapkan:
* accountable organizational superior;
* routine reporting destination;
* organizational status reporting;
* responsibility escalation;
* organizational performance context.
Setiap organizational unit hanya memiliki satu primary reporting destination pada satu waktu kecuali terdapat formal structural exception yang disahkan melalui applicable authority.
 
⸻

7. Reporting at Sub Division Level
Setiap Architecture Sub Division memiliki:
* defined organizational owner;
* primary reporting destination;
* responsibility boundary;
* Team population;
* accountability path.
Sub Division bertanggung jawab melaporkan:
* material responsibility status;
* capability condition;
* Team condition;
* material issue;
* material dependency;
* material deviation;
* relevant performance information.
Sub Division tidak memperoleh authority tambahan hanya karena menjadi reporting layer.
 
⸻
 
8. Reporting at Team Level
Setiap Team memiliki primary reporting relationship kepada organizational level yang ditetapkan oleh Sub Division Architecture.
Team reporting mencakup:
* Team responsibility status;
* capability execution condition;
* material workload condition;
* Team-level issue;
* dependency condition;
* output condition;
* improvement requirement.
Team reporting tidak mengubah ownership capability yang telah ditetapkan B3.
 
⸻
 
9. Reporting at Role Group Level
Role Group merupakan responsibility cluster di bawah Team.
Role Group reporting digunakan untuk:
* responsibility status;
* output status;
* issue identification;
* dependency reporting;
* evidence submission;
* accountability support.
Role Group tidak menjadi organizational owner terpisah dari Team apabila B5 tidak menetapkannya sebagai organizational container.
Dengan demikian:
Role Group
    ↓
Responsibility Accountability
    ↓
Team
    ↓
Organizational Accountability
 
⸻
 
10. Executive Reporting Interface
PD-02 memiliki reporting interface kepada applicable Executive authority.
Executive reporting digunakan untuk menyampaikan:
* material architecture status;
* material organizational condition;
* material architecture risk;
* material dependency;
* unresolved issue;
* significant deviation;
* organizational performance context;
* matter requiring executive attention.
Executive reporting tidak berarti Executive Office mengambil alih operational responsibility PD-02.
 
⸻
 
11. Accountability Flow
Accountability mengalir melalui hierarchy:

Material accountability issue dapat dinaikkan melalui hierarchy.
Namun escalation yang membutuhkan governance authority mengikuti Part C.
 
⸻
 
12. Information Flow
Reporting hierarchy menyediakan primary information path, tetapi bukan satu-satunya information path.
Information dapat mengalir melalui:
Primary Reporting Flow
        +
Functional Information Flow
        +
Cross-Team Interface
        +
Cross-Platform Interface
Functional atau cross-platform information flow tidak mengubah primary reporting relationship.
 
⸻
 
13. Functional Relationship
PD-02 dapat memiliki functional relationships yang tidak identik dengan primary reporting line.
Contoh:
Team A
   │
   ├── Primary Reporting → Sub Division A
   │
   └── Functional Interface → Team B
Functional relationship dapat digunakan untuk:
* coordination;
* information exchange;
* specialist consultation;
* dependency management;
* architectural review.
Functional relationship tidak menciptakan subordinate relationship.
 
⸻
 
14. Cross-Platform Relationship
PD-02 dapat berinteraksi dengan Platform Division lain melalui:
* architecture interface;
* dependency interface;
* review interface;
* decision interface;
* implementation interface.
Namun:
Cross-Platform Interface
        ≠
Reporting Relationship
Platform lain tidak menjadi subordinate kepada PD-02 hanya karena menerima architecture review, architecture requirement, atau architectural guidance.
Prinsip ini konsisten dengan A6:
Architectural interaction does not transfer organizational ownership, operational responsibility, or implementation authority.
 
⸻

19. Materiality-Based Reporting
Tidak seluruh informasi harus dinaikkan ke Executive level.
Reporting dapat menggunakan materiality.
Routine
Tetap pada operational/team level.
Material
Dinaikkan ke Sub Division atau Architecture Office.
Significant
Dapat diteruskan ke Executive interface.
Governance-Critical
Dirouting melalui applicable Governance Authority.
Model:
Information
    ↓
Materiality Assessment
    │
    ├── Routine → Local Reporting
    │
    ├── Material → Management Reporting
    │
    ├── Significant → Executive Reporting
    │
    └── Governance-Critical → Governance Route
Materiality tidak mengubah authority.
 
⸻
 
20. Reporting Integrity Rules
PD-02 harus mempertahankan:
Rule 01 — One Primary Reporting Owner
Setiap organizational unit memiliki primary reporting destination yang jelas.
Rule 02 — No Hidden Reporting Authority
Informal reporting relationship tidak boleh digunakan untuk menciptakan authority yang tidak tercatat.
Rule 03 — Reporting Does Not Transfer Ownership
Reporting tidak memindahkan domain ownership.
Rule 04 — Functional Relationship Must Be Explicit
Functional relationship yang material harus dapat diidentifikasi.
Rule 05 — Cross-Platform Reporting Is Not Subordination
Cross-platform reporting tidak membuat Platform lain subordinate.
Rule 06 — Material Reporting Must Be Traceable
Material report harus dapat ditelusuri kepada organizational source.
Rule 07 — Reporting Changes Must Be Explicit
Perubahan material pada hierarchy harus melalui formal organizational change mechanism.

27. Canonical Reporting Principle
PD-02 Reporting Hierarchy establishes a clear and traceable organizational accountability structure from Role Group through Team and Sub Division to the Architecture Office and its applicable Executive interface. Reporting relationships govern organizational accountability and information routing; they do not create, transfer, or redefine authority, domain ownership, or implementation responsibility.
Versi Bahasa Indonesia:
Reporting Hierarchy PD-02 menetapkan struktur accountability organisasi yang jelas dan dapat ditelusuri dari Role Group melalui Team dan Sub Division hingga Architecture Office dan Executive interface yang berlaku. Reporting relationship mengatur accountability organisasi dan routing informasi; reporting tidak menciptakan, memindahkan, atau mendefinisikan ulang authority, domain ownership, maupun implementation responsibility.

8. reporting tidak memindahkan ownership;

10. Executive reporting dapat dilakukan tanpa mengambil alih PD-02;
