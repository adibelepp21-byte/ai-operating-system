# 1. Purpose

D9 menetapkan **Operating Improvement Constitution** bagi PD-02 Architecture Office.

D9 menjawab pertanyaan:

> **Bagaimana operating model PD-02 dikembangkan secara sistematis berdasarkan evidence, learning, assessment, innovation, dan validated improvement?**

D9 memastikan bahwa operating model tidak hanya:

- berjalan;
- controlled;
- traceable;

tetapi juga:

- belajar;
- berkembang;
- semakin efektif;
- semakin efisien;
- semakin matang;
- semakin adaptif.

D9 merupakan **evolution layer** dari Part D.

3. Operating Improvement Constitution
Operating improvement PD-02 adalah proses terkontrol untuk mengubah operating model berdasarkan evidence dan learning.
Canonical principle:
Improvement must be evidence-based, authorized, traceable, implemented, and validated.
Dengan demikian:
Improvement
    ≠
Uncontrolled Change
dan:
Innovation
    ≠
Unauthorized Structural Change
 
⸻
 
4. Operating Improvement Purpose
Operating Improvement memiliki tujuan:
OI-01 — Increase Operating Effectiveness
Meningkatkan kemampuan operating model dalam menghasilkan intended architectural outcome.
 
⸻
 
OI-02 — Increase Operating Efficiency
Mengurangi:
* unnecessary friction;
* redundant activity;
* avoidable delay;
* unnecessary coordination overhead;
* repeated operational waste.

Memperbaiki kejelasan:

⸻

5. Operating Improvement Principles
OIP-01 — Evidence Before Improvement
Improvement harus memiliki evidence atau learning yang memadai.
Evidence dapat berasal dari:
* operational review;
* control result;
* recurring friction;
* work outcome;
* decision outcome;
* architecture review;
* stakeholder feedback;
* lessons learned;
* performance evidence.
Improvement tidak boleh hanya didasarkan pada preference individual.
 
⸻
 
OIP-02 — Improvement Before Structural Change
Tidak setiap operational problem membutuhkan perubahan structural.
Sebelum mengubah structure, assessment harus menentukan apakah masalah dapat diselesaikan melalui:
* workflow improvement;
* coordination improvement;
* information improvement;
* control improvement;
* tooling improvement;
* documentation improvement;
* capability improvement.
 
⸻
 
OIP-03 — Authorized Change
Material improvement yang mengubah controlled operating mechanism harus memiliki authority yang sesuai.
Improvement Opportunity
        ↓
Assessment
        ↓
Authorization
        ↓
Change
 
⸻
 
OIP-04 — Traceable Improvement
Setiap material improvement harus dapat ditelusuri dari:
Evidence
   ↓
Finding / Learning
   ↓
Opportunity
   ↓
Decision
   ↓
Change
   ↓
Validation
 
⸻
 
OIP-05 — Proportional Improvement
Besarnya improvement mechanism harus proporsional terhadap:
* materiality;
* impact;
* risk;
* dependency;
* cost;
* reversibility.
Improvement kecil tidak memerlukan mekanisme change sebesar perubahan constitutional.
 
⸻
 
OIP-06 — Preserve Authority Boundary
Operating improvement tidak boleh digunakan untuk:
* menciptakan authority baru;
* memperluas Architecture Authority secara informal;
* mengambil alih governance authority;
* mengubah Platform ownership;
* mengubah organizational structure tanpa authority.
 
⸻
 
OIP-07 — Learn Before Repeat
Recurring operational problem harus menghasilkan learning sebelum solusi yang sama diulang tanpa perubahan.
 
⸻
 
OIP-08 — Validate Before Institutionalize
Improvement tidak dianggap berhasil hanya karena telah diimplementasikan.
Improvement harus divalidasi sebelum menjadi operating practice yang dipertahankan.
 
⸻
 
OIP-09 — Improvement Without Drift
Improvement harus meningkatkan operating model tanpa menyebabkan constitutional drift.
 
⸻
 
OIP-10 — Continuous Learning
Operating model harus mampu mengubah experience menjadi organizational learning.

PD-02 Operating Improvement is successful when evidence and learning are systematically converted into authorized, traceable, validated, and sustainable operating improvements that increase effectiveness, efficiency, reliability, and adaptability while preserving governance boundaries, architecture integrity, organizational ownership, and Platform autonomy.

6. material change memiliki authorization yang sesuai;
7. implementation dapat ditelusuri;
8. improvement memiliki validation criteria;
9. validation menghasilkan outcome yang tercatat;
10. lessons learned dapat digunakan kembali;
11. innovation dapat diintegrasikan secara controlled;
12. recurring problem dapat menghasilkan systemic learning;
13. improvement tidak menciptakan authority baru;
14. improvement tidak mengubah ownership secara informal;
15. Platform autonomy tetap terjaga;
16. operating control tetap efektif;
17. performance evidence dapat digunakan sebagai input tanpa mengambil alih Part E;
18. structural change mengikuti applicable organizational mechanism;
19. lifecycle change mengikuti applicable lifecycle mechanism;
20. D10 memiliki input yang cukup untuk menilai operating success.
