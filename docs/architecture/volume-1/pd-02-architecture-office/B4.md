1. Purpose
B4 menetapkan Team Architecture PD-02 Architecture Office sebagai lapisan organisasi di bawah Sub Division dan di atas Role Group.
B4 menjawab:
Bagaimana 12 capability PD-02 diwujudkan menjadi Team yang memiliki responsibility cluster yang coherent, boundary yang jelas, ownership tunggal, dan dependency yang dapat dikelola?
B4 tidak menggunakan aturan:
1 Capability = 1 Team
Sebaliknya:
Capability
    ↓
Responsibility Coherence
    ↓
Workload Coherence
    ↓
Team Boundary
    ↓
Team
Team merupakan organizational execution container untuk satu atau lebih capability yang secara substantif dapat dijalankan bersama.
 
⸻
 
2. Design Position
Urutan organisasi PD-02:
PD-02 Architecture Office
        ↓
Sub Division
        ↓
Capability
        ↓

        ↓

B3 telah menetapkan 12 capability.
B4 tidak mengubah capability tersebut.
B4 hanya menentukan bagaimana capability tersebut dikelompokkan menjadi Team.
Canonical rule:
B4 may group capabilities into Teams, but may not silently redefine, merge, split, or transfer the canonical capability ownership established by B3.
Jika ditemukan bahwa capability B3 memang salah desain, perubahan harus kembali melalui controlled revision B3.

3. Team Design Principles
TA-01 — Capability-Driven
Team harus diturunkan dari capability yang sudah frozen.
Team tidak boleh dibangun hanya berdasarkan:
* jabatan;
* jumlah orang;
* technology preference;
* organizational convention;
* daftar aktivitas.
 
⸻
 
TA-02 — Responsibility Coherence
Capability hanya boleh digabung dalam satu Team apabila responsibility-nya memiliki hubungan substantif.
Indikator coherence:
* memiliki architectural concern yang sama;
* memiliki lifecycle yang berdekatan;
* memiliki primary consumers yang serupa;
* menggunakan evidence atau artifact yang saling berkaitan;
* menghasilkan outcome yang dapat dikelola sebagai satu responsibility cluster.
 
⸻
 
TA-03 — Workload Coherence
Team harus memiliki workload yang cukup coherent untuk dikelola sebagai satu organizational unit.
Workload tidak boleh menjadi dasar tunggal pembentukan Team.
Namun workload digunakan sebagai validation factor untuk mencegah:
* Team terlalu kecil;
* Team terlalu luas;
* Team dengan responsibility yang tidak seimbang;
* artificial fragmentation.
 
⸻
 
TA-04 — One Primary Team Owner
Setiap capability memiliki satu primary Team owner.
Supporting Team diperbolehkan.
Dual primary ownership tidak diperbolehkan.
 
⸻
 
TA-05 — Team Boundary Must Be Observable
Team boundary harus dapat dijelaskan berdasarkan:
* responsibility;
* capability;
* output;
* consumer;
* decision context;
* dependency.
Bukan berdasarkan:
“Team A mengerjakan sebagian dari pekerjaan Team B.”
 
⸻
 
TA-06 — Team Does Not Create Authority
Team menjalankan responsibility capability.
Team tidak memperoleh authority baru hanya karena capability ditempatkan di dalam Team.
Authority tetap mengikuti:
* A5 — Authority & Mandate;
* applicable Architecture Authority;
* delegation yang sah.
 
⸻
 
TA-07 — Team ≠ Role Group
Team adalah organizational container.
Role Group merupakan responsibility cluster workforce di dalam Team.
Team
    ↓
Role Group
Role Group baru ditetapkan pada section berikutnya.

Each Team follows:

   ↓

   ↓
Define Responsibility
   ↓
Operate
   ↓
Assess Workload
   ↓

   ↓

A Team may be changed only when evidence demonstrates that the existing responsibility structure is no longer coherent.
 
⸻
 
29. Team Creation Rule
A new Team may be proposed only when at least one condition exists:
1. capability workload becomes materially independent;
2. responsibility boundary becomes materially distinct;
3. decision/evidence control requires separation;
4. dependency complexity creates unacceptable coordination burden;
5. capability maturity requires dedicated organizational ownership;
6. a new frozen capability is introduced.
Team creation must not be used merely to increase organizational size.
 
⸻
 
30. Team Merge Rule
Two Teams may be merged when:
* responsibility becomes materially overlapping;
* workload is consistently insufficient for separate ownership;
* boundaries create unnecessary coordination;
* lifecycle becomes substantially shared;
* separation no longer provides control value.
Merge does not automatically merge the underlying capabilities.
 
⸻
 
31. Team Split Rule
A Team may be split when:
* workload becomes materially heterogeneous;
* responsibility domains diverge;
* authority boundary requires separation;
* risk profile becomes materially different;
* one Team becomes a coordination bottleneck;
* capability maturity requires distinct ownership.
Split must preserve capability ownership.
 
⸻
 
32. Team Retirement Rule
A Team may be retired when:
* its capability responsibility is removed;
* capability is transferred through approved organizational change;
* capability is merged into another Team;
* the underlying responsibility no longer exists.
Historical artifact and decision traceability must remain preserved.

   ↓

34. Team-to-Role Group Boundary
A Team may contain:
* one Role Group;
* multiple Role Groups;
* specialist Role Groups;
* shared enabling Role Groups.
Therefore:
1 Team ≠ 1 Role Group
The same anti-fragmentation principle used in B4 must continue into B5.

35. Team Architecture Success Criteria
B4 is successful when:
1. all B3 capabilities have primary Team ownership;
2. no capability has dual primary Team ownership;
3. Team boundaries are explicit;
4. Team responsibilities are coherent;
5. workload grouping is defensible;
6. no unnecessary Team fragmentation exists;
7. no Team combines materially unrelated capabilities;
8. cross-Team dependencies are explicit;
9. circular ownership does not exist;
10. Teams remain within PD-02 authority boundary;
11. Team architecture is ready for Role Group decomposition;
12. B3 capability definitions remain unchanged.
