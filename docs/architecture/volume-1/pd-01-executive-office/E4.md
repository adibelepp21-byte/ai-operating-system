1. Purpose

E4 menetapkan framework untuk menentukan indikator keberhasilan yang digunakan untuk mengukur pencapaian objective AIOS.

E4 menjawab:

“Apa indikator keberhasilan yang digunakan untuk mengetahui apakah objective tercapai?”

Canonical chain:

Objective
    ↓
Success Criterion
    ↓
Metric
    ↓
KPI / Indicator
    ↓
Target / Expected Condition
    ↓
Measurement
    ↓
Performance Evaluation

E4 tidak menciptakan objective baru.

⸻

2. Framework Principle

Every material objective should have a proportionate and traceable success measurement model.

Namun:

Not every objective requires a KPI.

Objective dapat menggunakan:

* KPI;
* supporting metric;
* diagnostic metric;
* qualitative criterion;
* milestone;
* compliance condition;
* outcome measure.

⸻

3. Objective-to-Metric Architecture

Objective
    │
    ▼
Success Criterion
    │
    ▼
Measurement Requirement
    │
    ▼
Metric
    │
    ├── KPI
    ├── Supporting Metric
    └── Diagnostic Metric
    │
    ▼
Target / Expected Condition
    │
    ▼
Measurement Result
    │
    ▼
Performance Evaluation

Objective = what we want.

Success Criterion = condition of success.

Metric = what is measured.

KPI = selected material indicator.

Target = expected condition.

Measurement = actual condition.

⸻

4. Success Metrics vs KPI

Success Metric adalah metric yang digunakan untuk menentukan apakah objective/criterion tercapai.

KPI adalah metric yang dipilih secara formal sebagai indikator utama untuk kondisi material.

Success Metrics
      │
      ├── KPI
      ├── Supporting Metrics
      └── Diagnostic Metrics

KPI dapat menjadi success metric, tetapi tidak setiap success metric harus menjadi KPI.

⸻

5. KPI Selection Principles

KPI harus:

* material;
* decision-relevant;
* traceable;
* measurable;
* sufficiently stable;
* interpretable;
* owned;
* evidence-backed.

Hindari KPI proliferation.

⸻

6. Strategic Objectives → Metrics

Strategic Objective
        ↓
Strategic Success Criterion
        ↓
Strategic Metric
        ↓
Strategic KPI
        ↓
Strategic Result

Strategic KPI terutama merepresentasikan:

* strategic outcome;
* strategic alignment;
* material strategic condition.

Activity counts tidak otomatis menjadi strategic KPI.

⸻

7. Operational Objectives → Metrics

Operational Objective
        ↓
Operational Success Criterion
        ↓
Operational Metric
        ↓
Operational KPI
        ↓
Operational Result

Categories:

* effectiveness;
* efficiency;
* quality;
* timeliness;
* reliability;
* service/workflow.

Performance Management mengukur operation; tidak mengambil execution ownership.

⸻

8. Governance Objectives → Metrics

Governance Objective
        ↓
Governance Success Criterion
        ↓
Governance Metric
        ↓
Governance KPI
        ↓
Governance Assessment

Possible categories:

* compliance;
* decision integrity;
* accountability;
* control effectiveness;
* escalation integrity.

⸻

9. Capability Objectives → Metrics

Capability measurement dapat mencakup:

* readiness;
* coverage;
* capability effectiveness;
* capability reliability;
* capability maturity;
* capability utilization.

Capability KPI tidak boleh disamakan dengan operational outcome.

⸻

10. KPI vs Performance Health

KPI result bukan Performance Health.

KPI Results
     ↓
Dimension Results
     ↓
Cross-Dimension Analysis
     ↓
Context
     ↓
Performance Health

⸻

11. KPI Framework Boundary

In Scope

* objective-to-metric mapping;
* success metrics;
* KPI selection;
* KPI classification;
* KPI ownership;
* KPI catalogue;
* metric coverage;
* leading/lagging mapping;
* target linkage;
* KPI lifecycle;
* KPI reporting;
* KPI governance.

Out of Scope

* strategic direction;
* strategic objectives;
* operational process ownership;
* governance authority;
* capability ownership;
* individual compensation;
* universal KPI numbers;
* universal targets;
* performance health conclusion.

⸻

12. Reference Implementation Pattern

Yang diwariskan:

* objective → criterion → metric → KPI;
* KPI selection principles;
* metric classification;
* ownership;
* lifecycle;
* catalogue schema;
* target/measurement separation;
* anti-gaming;
* coverage rules.

Yang domain-specific:

* objective;
* criterion;
* metric catalogue;
* KPI;
* target;
* threshold;
* evidence source;
* frequency;
* owner.

⸻

13. Success Criteria

E4 berhasil apabila:

1. material objective memiliki success measurement atau justified exception;
2. Strategic Objectives memiliki mapping;
3. Operational Objectives memiliki mapping;
4. Governance Objectives memiliki mapping;
5. Capability Objectives memiliki mapping;
6. KPI dapat dibedakan;
7. leading/lagging dapat dibedakan;
8. KPI memiliki owner dan evidence;
9. target terpisah dari actual;
10. definition traceable;
11. metric tidak menciptakan authority;
12. overlap/double counting terkendali;
13. KPI lifecycle dikelola;
14. reporting memiliki context;
15. framework dapat diwariskan.
