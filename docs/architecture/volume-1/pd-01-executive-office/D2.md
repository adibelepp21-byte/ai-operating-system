D2 — EXECUTIVE OPERATING CONSTITUTION

Section ID: D2
Section Name: Executive Operating Constitution
Platform ID: PD-01
Parent: D1
Status: FROZEN

1. Purpose

D2 defines how the Executive Office operates as the core executive
operating system of PD-01.

D2 does not redefine D1.

D1 establishes the organizational operating constitution.

D2 establishes how the Executive Office applies that constitution
in its daily and strategic operations.

2. Executive Operating Purpose

The Executive Office operates to:

* translate strategic direction into coordinated execution;
* maintain executive alignment;
* coordinate organizational activity;
* interface with Governance Architecture;
* coordinate Platform activity;
* review organizational execution;
* ensure operational issues reach the appropriate authority.

3. Executive Operating Principles

EO-01 — Strategic Alignment

Executive operations remain aligned with approved strategy.

EO-02 — Governance Alignment

Executive operations remain within Governance Constitution.

EO-03 — Architecture Alignment

Executive operations respect Architecture Baseline.

EO-04 — Explicit Coordination

Cross-functional and cross-platform activities use explicit
coordination mechanisms.

EO-05 — Evidence-Based Review

Executive review relies on available operational evidence.

EO-06 — Boundary Preservation

Executive coordination does not automatically transfer domain ownership.

4. Strategic Operations

Executive Office coordinates:

* strategic planning;
* strategic execution;
* organizational priorities;
* executive initiatives;
* strategic reviews;
* cross-platform alignment.

Strategic operations do not create authority that has not been granted
through Governance Architecture.

5. Governance Operations

Executive Office interfaces with governance through:

* decision preparation;
* governance review;
* escalation;
* compliance interface;
* executive review;
* governance implementation.

The Executive Office does not replace the Governance Architecture.

8. Executive Operating Interfaces

Primary interfaces include:

* Governance Architecture;
* Organization Architecture;
* Architecture Office;
* Platform Divisions;
* Performance Architecture;
* executive stakeholders;
* organizational work systems.

9. Executive Operating Constraints

D2 must not:

* redefine governance authority;
* replace organizational structure;
* own another Platform’s domain;
* create unauthorized decision rights;
* replace Performance Architecture;
* bypass Architecture Baseline.

10. Executive Operating Rhythm

D2 operates through:

* daily operational awareness;
* weekly coordination;
* monthly review;
* quarterly strategic review;
* annual operating assessment.

11. Executive Operating Review

Executive review assesses:

* strategic alignment;
* operational condition;
* material dependencies;
* exceptions;
* decision requirements;
* improvement requirements.

Review output must be routed to the appropriate authority.

12. Executive Operating Success Statement

D2 succeeds when the Executive Office can:

* translate direction into coordinated execution;
* maintain organizational alignment;
* surface material issues;
* coordinate Platform activity;
* route decisions correctly;
* maintain traceability;
* continuously improve operations.
