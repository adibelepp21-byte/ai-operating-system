1. Purpose

A9 menetapkan kondisi objektif yang harus dipenuhi agar PD-01 dapat dinyatakan
berhasil sebagai Platform Division.

A8 menjawab:

Apa yang ingin dicapai?

A9 menjawab:

Kapan kita dapat mengatakan bahwa PD-01 berhasil?

⸻

2. Success Evaluation Framework

PD-01 dievaluasi melalui enam dimensi:

Dimension	Focus
SD-01 Governance Effectiveness	efektivitas governance
SD-02 Strategic Alignment	alignment strategi
SD-03 Architecture Compliance	architecture integrity
SD-04 Cross-Platform Collaboration	coordination
SD-05 Decision Quality	kualitas keputusan
SD-06 Organizational Maturity	maturity

⸻

3. SD-01 — Governance Effectiveness

PD-01 berhasil apabila:

* governance process terdokumentasi;
* policy owner jelas;
* Executive Charter dijalankan;
* conflicts memiliki resolution;
* Architecture Change Control berjalan.

Evidence:

* Governance Review Report;
* Policy Register;
* Decision Log;
* Architecture Review Record.

⸻

4. SD-02 — Strategic Alignment

PD-01 berhasil apabila:

* Platform roadmap selaras dengan AIOS strategy;
* Strategic Objectives traceable ke Mission/Vision;
* enterprise priorities dipahami;
* tidak terdapat strategic initiatives yang conflicting tanpa resolution.

Evidence:

* Strategic Alignment Matrix;
* Platform Roadmap Review;
* Executive Strategy Review;
* Portfolio Alignment Report.

⸻

5. SD-03 — Architecture Compliance

PD-01 berhasil apabila:

* material changes mengikuti Architecture Change Control;
* tidak terdapat material implementation yang bertentangan dengan
    Architecture Baseline;
* Platform Encyclopedia konsisten;
* Architecture Review berjalan secara berkala.

Evidence:

* Architecture Compliance Report;
* Change Control Register;
* ADR;
* Baseline Conformance Assessment.

⸻

6. SD-04 — Cross-Platform Collaboration

PD-01 berhasil apabila:

* dependencies antar Platform terdokumentasi;
* cross-platform conflicts diselesaikan;
* collaboration memiliki ownership;
* program lintas Platform memiliki governance;
* autonomy masing-masing Platform tetap terjaga.

⸻

7. SD-05 — Decision Quality

PD-01 berhasil apabila material decisions:

* memiliki context;
* evidence;
* rationale;
* authority;
* Decision Owner;
* consequence;
* record.

⸻

8. SD-06 — Organizational Maturity

PD-01 menggunakan lima tingkat maturity:

Level	Classification	Description
1	Initial	governance masih dasar
2	Managed	governance terdokumentasi
3	Defined	governance terstandardisasi
4	Integrated	governance, strategy, architecture terintegrasi
5	Gold Standard	menjadi Reference Implementation

Target resmi PD-01:

Level 5 — Gold Standard

10. Continuous Improvement

Setiap evaluation cycle harus menghasilkan apabila diperlukan:

* improvement recommendation;
* capability roadmap update;
* organizational risk identification;
* governance improvement;
* decision quality improvement;
* maturity improvement.
