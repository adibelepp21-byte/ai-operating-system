D3 — PLATFORM COORDINATION CONSTITUTION

Section ID: D3
Section Name: Platform Coordination Constitution
Platform ID: PD-01
Parent: D2
Status: FROZEN

1. Purpose

D3 defines the operational mechanism for coordination between PD-01
Executive Office and PD-02 through PD-10.

D3 is operational.

C8 governs the relationship.

D3 executes the coordination mechanism.

2. Coordination Principles

PC-01 — Ownership Preservation

Coordination does not transfer ownership.

PC-02 — Explicit Interface

Cross-platform interaction must have an identifiable interface.

PC-03 — Dependency Transparency

Dependencies must be visible.

PC-04 — Coordinated Execution

Cross-platform work must have defined synchronization points.

PC-05 — Escalation When Required

Material coordination blockers must follow the applicable escalation
path.

3. Coordination Domains

Coordination may cover:

* strategic alignment;
* capability dependencies;
* architecture dependencies;
* work dependencies;
* information exchange;
* decision dependencies;
* operational risk;
* shared initiatives;
* cross-platform programs.

5. Coordination Workflow

1. Identify coordination need.
2. Identify affected Platform.
3. Identify domain owner.
4. Define required interaction.
5. Define expected output.
6. Establish timing.
7. Execute coordination.
8. Monitor dependency.
9. Resolve or escalate.
10. Close the coordination item.

6. Coordination Roles

Typical roles include:

* initiating owner;
* participating owner;
* coordination owner;
* decision owner where applicable;
* escalation authority where required.

Role assignment does not modify Part C authority.

7. Coordination Interfaces

Interfaces include:

* information;
* capability;
* work;
* decision;
* architecture;
* review;
* escalation.

8. Coordination Rhythm

Cross-platform coordination should align with:

* weekly coordination;
* monthly review;
* quarterly strategic review;
* event-driven coordination.

9. Dependency Management

Each material dependency should identify:

* source;
* destination;
* dependency type;
* owner;
* expected outcome;
* timing;
* status;
* blocker;
* escalation path.

10. Coordination Constraints

D3 must not:

* override Platform ownership;
* create governance authority;
* bypass compliance;
* redefine architecture;
* create unauthorized delegation.

11. Coordination Review

Coordination effectiveness is reviewed through:

* unresolved dependencies;
* missed commitments;
* communication failures;
* recurring coordination friction;
* escalation frequency;
* completion quality.

12. Coordination Success Statement

D3 succeeds when cross-platform operations are:

* visible;
* coordinated;
* traceable;
* ownership-preserving;
* timely;
* escalatable;
* reviewable.
