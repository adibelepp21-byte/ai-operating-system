1. Purpose

E5 mendefinisikan bagaimana hasil performance dievaluasi, direview, diinterpretasikan, dan diterjemahkan menjadi improvement signal.

E5 menjawab:

“Bagaimana performa dievaluasi?”

E5 mencakup:

1. Review Cycle;
2. Performance Assessment;
3. Executive Review;
4. Platform Review;
5. Improvement Decision.

⸻

2. Performance Review Principle

Performance review converts measured evidence into an evidence-based assessment of current performance and a controlled signal for what should happen next.

Review bukan sekadar:

* dashboard review;
* KPI inspection;
* reporting meeting;
* score aggregation.

Review menghasilkan:

* assessment;
* gap identification;
* interpretation;
* finding;
* review outcome;
* improvement signal;
* follow-up requirement.

4. Review Scope

Performance Review mencakup:

* Strategic Objectives;
* Success Criteria;
* Strategic Performance;
* Operational Performance;
* Governance Performance;
* Capability Performance;
* Organizational Effectiveness;
* Improvement Progress.

5. Review Cycle

Baseline review cycle:

Continuous
Monthly Executive
Quarterly Strategic
Annual Maturity
Event-Based Review

Cadence domain-specific dapat berbeda sesuai context.

⸻

6. Review Questions

Minimum questions:

Q-01 — Objective

Apakah objective dan scope jelas?

Q-02 — Evidence

Apakah evidence cukup?

Q-03 — Actual

Apa kondisi aktual?

Q-04 — Expected

Apa expected condition?

Q-05 — Variance

Apa gap/variance?

Q-06 — Materiality

Apakah finding material?

Q-07 — Authority

Apakah proposed outcome berada dalam authority yang tepat?

Q-08 — Traceability

Apakah conclusion dapat ditelusuri?

⸻

7. Review Failure States

Review dapat berhenti atau ditunda pada:

Insufficient Evidence
Measurement Invalid
Criteria Missing
Objective Ambiguous
Authority Unclear
Material Context Missing

Do not manufacture a performance conclusion.

11. Improvement Decision Boundary

Performance Management dapat menghasilkan recommendation/improvement signal.

Performance Management tidak otomatis menjadi decision authority.

Target adjustment tidak dilakukan secara unilateral.

13. Success Criteria

E5 berhasil apabila:

1. formal review memiliki objective/scope;
2. review cycle dapat dijalankan;
3. assessment memiliki evidence traceability;
4. actual vs expected dibedakan;
5. current vs trend dibedakan;
6. materiality dihitung;
7. gaps dapat diklasifikasikan;
8. causal claims tidak dibuat tanpa evidence;
9. Executive Review memiliki owner/reviewer/evidence/authority;
10. Platform Review integrated;
11. improvement decision traceable;
12. completion ≠ effectiveness;
13. target adjustment tidak unilateral;
14. boundaries terjaga;
15. critical findings dapat dieskalasi;
16. insufficient evidence dapat menghentikan conclusion;
17. improvement diverifikasi;
18. review history auditable;
19. framework inheritable.
