⸻

1. Purpose

C6 menetapkan bagaimana sistem governance, authority, accountability, architecture compliance, performance, dan strategic alignment ditinjau, divalidasi, dievaluasi, dan ditingkatkan.

⸻

2. Executive Review Constitution

Setiap review harus:

* memiliki objective;
* memiliki scope;
* memiliki owner;
* memiliki reviewer;
* memiliki criteria;
* memiliki evidence;
* menghasilkan assessment;
* menghasilkan finding;
* menghasilkan outcome/decision;
* memiliki improvement action;
* memiliki audit history.

Review adalah mekanisme pengendalian evolusi organisasi.

⸻

3. Executive Review Objectives

ER-01 — Governance Effectiveness

Menilai efektivitas governance.

ER-02 — Architecture Alignment

Memastikan alignment dengan Architecture Baseline.

ER-03 — Performance Visibility

Memastikan performance dapat dievaluasi.

ER-04 — Strategic Alignment

Memastikan arah strategis tetap terjaga.

ER-05 — Continuous Improvement

Menghasilkan improvement action.

⸻

4. Review Types

Review Type	Primary Owner	Final Authority
Governance Review	Governance Owner	Executive Authority
Architecture Review	Architecture Owner	Architecture Authority
Performance Review	Performance Owner	Executive Authority
Strategic Review	Strategic Owner	Executive Authority

Tabel di atas adalah authoritative source untuk Review Type, Primary Owner,
dan Final Authority. E5 §9 dan E6 §6 mengacu ke tabel ini dan tidak
menduplikasinya.

C6 tidak membuat authority hierarchy baru.

6. Executive Review Roles

Role	Responsibility
Review Owner	Menyiapkan dan mengelola review
Domain Owner	Menyediakan evidence/context
Executive Leadership	Menilai hasil dan response
Reviewer	Independent validation/challenge
Custodian	Menjaga review record

⸻

7. Executive Review Authority Boundary

Review Owner dapat:

* mengelola review;
* menyusun assessment;
* mengidentifikasi gap;
* menghasilkan finding;
* menghasilkan improvement signal.

Review Owner tidak otomatis dapat:

* mengubah strategic direction;
* mengubah governance policy;
* mengubah architecture;
* mengubah approved target;
* memerintahkan execution di luar delegated authority.

⸻

8. Platform Review

Platform Review mengevaluasi:

* Strategic Performance;
* Operational Performance;
* Governance Performance;
* Capability Performance;
* Improvement Performance.

Output:

Platform
   ↓
Integrated Assessment
   ↓
Platform Finding
   ↓
Executive Review

Platform Review tidak hanya menjumlahkan KPI.

10. Review Escalation

Review harus dieskalasikan apabila ditemukan:

* governance violation;
* architecture deviation;
* unresolved accountability conflict;
* strategic misalignment;
* critical performance gap.

Eskalasi mengikuti C3, C4, C5, dan C7.

⸻

11. Review Documentation

Setiap review mempertahankan:

* Review Identifier;
* Review Type;
* Review Owner;
* Scope;
* Criteria;
* Evidence;
* Assessment;
* Findings;
* Decision / Outcome;
* Improvement Actions;
* Follow-up;
* Completion Status;
* Review History.

⸻

12. Review Success

Executive Review berhasil apabila:

* review berjalan sesuai siklus;
* owner jelas;
* evidence tersedia;
* conclusion dapat ditelusuri;
* architecture compliance dapat diverifikasi;
* performance dapat dievaluasi;
* strategic alignment terjaga;
* improvement ditindaklanjuti.

⸻

13. Canonical Status
