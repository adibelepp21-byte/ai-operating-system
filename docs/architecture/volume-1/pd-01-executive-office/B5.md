1. Purpose

B5 defines Role Groups as the final organizational structure layer before workforce roles.

The organization chain is:

B1 — Organizational Model
        ↓
B2 — Executive Sub Division Architecture
        ↓
B3 — Capability Architecture
        ↓
B4 — Team Architecture
        ↓
B5 — Role Group Architecture
        ↓
Workforce Role

B5 answers:

“What responsibility clusters are required within each Team to implement the organizational capability?”

Role Group does not define an individual person.

It defines a responsibility cluster.

⸻

2. Role Group Design Principles

RG-01 — Responsibility-Based Design

Role Groups are based on responsibility domains, not traditional job titles.

Capability
    ↓
Team
    ↓
Role Group
    ↓
Role

⸻

RG-02 — Separation of Concern

Each Role Group must have:

* primary responsibility;
* boundary;
* outputs;
* consumers.

⸻

RG-03 — Human and AI Workforce Compatibility

Role Groups must be capable of supporting:

* human roles;
* AI agent roles;
* hybrid workforce roles.

⸻

RG-04 — Stable Organizational Pattern

The reusable pattern is:

* responsibility model;
* ownership model;
* interaction pattern.

Specific role names are not automatically inherited.

6. Workforce Boundary

Role Groups are not individual positions.

They provide a stable responsibility architecture from which individual workforce roles may later be defined.

Therefore B5 does not define:

* individual persons;
* employment status;
* compensation;
* hiring;
* individual skill profiles;
* individual performance contracts.

Those concerns belong to later workforce and operating architecture.

⸻

10. Part B Architectural Invariants

The following invariants apply across B1–B5.

INV-B01 — Mandate Before Organization

Organizational structure must derive from mandate.

⸻

INV-B02 — Ownership Before Responsibility

Every domain must have a primary owner before detailed responsibility is assigned.

⸻

INV-B03 — Capability Before Team

Teams must implement capabilities.

Teams must not become the source of capability definition.

⸻

INV-B04 — Team Before Role Group

Role Groups must derive from Team responsibility.

⸻

INV-B05 — Role Group Before Individual Role

Individual workforce roles must be derived from stable responsibility clusters.

⸻

INV-B06 — Governance Boundary Preservation

Organization architecture must not transfer authority that has not been granted through the governance model.

⸻

INV-B07 — Platform Boundary Preservation

PD-01 may coordinate and govern enterprise concerns without becoming the execution owner of other Platform domains.

⸻

INV-B08 — Single Primary Ownership

No domain should have two competing primary owners.

⸻

INV-B09 — Reusable Pattern

The structural pattern may be inherited by PD-02 through PD-10.

Domain content must remain platform-specific.

⸻

11. Part B Success Criteria

Part B is successful when:

* the complete PD-01 organizational structure is explicit;
* every major organizational domain has an owner;
* every ESD has a capability;
* every capability can be decomposed into Teams;
* every Team can be decomposed into Role Groups;
* workforce implementation can later be defined without redesigning the organizational architecture;
* Platform boundaries remain intact;
* governance authority is not silently transferred;
* the architecture can serve as a Reference Implementation.
