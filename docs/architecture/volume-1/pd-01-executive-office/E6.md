1. Purpose

E6 menetapkan bagaimana performance governance dikelola, dimiliki, direview, dieskalasikan, dan dipertanggungjawabkan.

E6 menjawab:

“Siapa bertanggung jawab terhadap kualitas performance?”

E6 mencakup:

1. Performance Ownership;
2. Performance Authority;
3. Review Authority;
4. Escalation;
5. Accountability.

⸻

2. Performance Governance Principle

Performance governance establishes ownership and accountability for the performance management capability while preserving the authority and ownership of the domains being measured.

Performance Governance tidak mengambil alih:

* strategic authority;
* governance authority;
* architecture authority;
* operational ownership;
* domain capability ownership;
* decision ownership.

4. Performance Ownership

ESD-08 / Performance Management Office memiliki ownership atas performance management capability.

Domain Owner mempertahankan ownership atas domain yang diukur.

Performance ownership ≠ domain ownership.

⸻

5. Performance Authority

Performance authority adalah delegated authority untuk menjalankan fungsi performance governance/review.

Performance authority bukan authority yang berdiri di atas Governance Authority.

Final decision authority tetap mengikuti approved governance model.

⸻

6. Executive Review Integration

Review Type	Primary Owner	Final Authority
Governance Review	Governance Owner	Executive Authority
Architecture Review	Architecture Owner	Architecture Authority
Performance Review	Performance Owner	Executive Authority
Strategic Review	Strategic Owner	Executive Authority

⸻

7. Accountability

Performance failure tidak otomatis memindahkan accountability.

Accountability harus tetap mengikuti ownership dan authority yang sah.

⸻

8. Escalation

Material findings harus memiliki escalation route.

Escalation diarahkan kepada authority yang dapat menyelesaikan condition.

Escalation tidak sama dengan transfer accountability.

⸻

9. Governance Failure vs Performance Failure

Governance failure harus dapat dibedakan dari:

* performance failure;
* measurement failure;
* execution failure;
* capability gap.

Premature attribution harus dihindari.

⸻

10. Decision vs Recommendation

Performance Finding
       ↓
Recommendation
       ↓
Authorized Decision
       ↓
Authorized Action

Recommendation bukan decision.

Performance Management tidak boleh menyatakan recommendation sebagai authorized decision.

⸻

11. Governance Feedback Loop

Performance Result
        ↓
Performance Review
        ↓
Governance Assessment
        ↓
Accountability / Authority Check
        ↓
Escalation if Required
        ↓
Authorized Response
        ↓
Improvement
        ↓
Re-measurement

13. Success Criteria

E6 berhasil apabila:

1. performance capability memiliki owner;
2. ESD-08 memiliki ownership;
3. domain owner mempertahankan domain ownership;
4. performance authority dibedakan dari decision authority;
5. review ownership dibedakan dari review authority;
6. final authority mengikuti approved governance;
7. accountability tidak berpindah hanya karena failure;
8. measurement accountability traceable;
9. material findings memiliki escalation;
10. escalation menuju authority tepat;
11. governance failure dapat dibedakan;
12. measurement failure dapat dibedakan;
13. recommendation ≠ decision;
14. improvement action memiliki accountable owner;
15. effectiveness dapat diverifikasi;
16. performance tidak override strategy/governance/architecture/operation;
17. authority conflicts dapat dieskalasi;
18. records auditable;
19. governance changes follow Architecture Change Control;
20. model inheritable.
