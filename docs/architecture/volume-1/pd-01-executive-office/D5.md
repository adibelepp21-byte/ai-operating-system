Section Name: Operational Decision Constitution
Platform ID: PD-01
Parent: D4
Status: FROZEN

1. Purpose

D5 establishes how decisions move through operational execution.

C2 defines decision authority.

D5 defines operational decision flow.

2. Decision Flow Principles

OD-01 — Correct Routing

A decision must reach the appropriate authority.

OD-02 — Context Before Action

Operational decisions must have sufficient context.

OD-03 — Traceability

Decision movement must be traceable.

OD-04 — Execution Clarity

Approved decisions must have clear implementation ownership.

OD-05 — Reviewability

Material decisions must be reviewable.

3. Decision Trigger Model

Operational decisions may be triggered by:

* work initiation;
* exception;
* dependency;
* risk;
* operational change;
* review finding;
* performance signal;
* strategic requirement.

4. Decision Intake

Decision intake records:

* decision question;
* context;
* source;
* affected domain;
* urgency;
* impact;
* required authority;
* supporting evidence.

5. Decision Analysis

Analysis identifies:

* problem;
* options;
* consequences;
* dependencies;
* risks;
* constraints;
* recommended path.

Analysis does not itself constitute authorization.

6. Decision Routing

Routing determines the correct decision authority based on
the applicable Governance and Authority Architecture.

Decision Need
↓
Classification
↓
Authority Identification
↓
Routing
↓
Decision

7. Decision Implementation

Once authorized:

* implementation owner is identified;
* required actions are defined;
* dependencies are assigned;
* timing is established;
* evidence is retained.

8. Decision Communication

Material decisions are communicated to affected participants.

Communication should include:

* decision;
* rationale where appropriate;
* owner;
* effective timing;
* affected scope;
* required action.

9. Decision Monitoring

Implementation is monitored for:

* completion;
* deviation;
* unintended consequence;
* dependency failure;
* exception.

10. Decision Review Cycle

Material decisions may be reviewed when:

* assumptions change;
* impact changes;
* implementation fails;
* new evidence appears;
* governance requires review.

12. Operational Decision Success Statement

D5 succeeds when operational decisions are:

* correctly routed;
* adequately informed;
* properly implemented;
* communicated;
* monitored;
* traceable;
* reviewable.
