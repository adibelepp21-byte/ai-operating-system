⸻

1. Purpose

C8 menetapkan bagaimana seluruh Platform Division AIOS dapat berinteraksi, berkolaborasi, dan menjaga alignment tanpa menghilangkan ownership masing-masing Platform.

PD-01:

* mengoordinasikan governance antar Platform;
* menjaga enterprise consistency;
* memastikan architecture alignment;
* menyediakan dependency resolution mechanism;
* memfasilitasi collaboration.

PD-01 tidak mengambil ownership domain Platform lain.

⸻

2. Cross Platform Governance Constitution

PD-01 Executive Office
        ↓
PD-02
        ↓
PD-03
        ↓
...
PD-10

Setiap Platform:

* memiliki domain ownership;
* bertanggung jawab terhadap capability domain;
* berkolaborasi melalui governance mechanism;
* mengelola dependency;
* mengikuti escalation path.

⸻

3. Governance Position of PD-01

3.1 Governance Coordinator

PD-01:

* memastikan alignment;
* menjaga governance consistency;
* memfasilitasi coordination;
* memastikan keputusan lintas Platform terdokumentasi.

PD-01 tidak:

* mengelola operasi Platform lain;
* menggantikan Platform Owner;
* menentukan detail implementasi domain lain.

3.2 Architecture Alignment Authority

PD-01 menjaga alignment dengan:

* Architecture Baseline v1.0;
* Platform Organization Specification;
* AIOS Governance Model;
* Platform Encyclopedia Standard.

3.3 Cross Platform Coordination Facilitator

PD-01 memfasilitasi:

* dependency management;
* shared initiatives;
* cross-platform review;
* conflict resolution.

3.4 Governance Consistency Guardian

PD-01 menjaga agar governance relationship tetap konsisten.

⸻

4. Cross Platform Governance Principles

1. Ownership Remains Local.
2. Dependency Must Be Explicit.
3. Authority Must Be Explicit.
4. Collaboration Does Not Transfer Ownership.
5. Governance Does Not Equal Execution.
6. Cross Platform Decisions Must Be Traceable.
7. Conflicts Must Have Escalation Paths.
8. Architecture Consistency Must Be Preserved.

⸻

5. Cross Platform Governance Scope

C8 mencakup:

* strategic alignment;
* architecture alignment;
* dependency governance;
* cross-platform decision governance;
* shared initiative governance;
* collaboration governance;
* conflict resolution;
* governance forums.

7. Cross Platform Interaction Types

7.1 Alignment Interaction

Tujuan:

Menjaga alignment enterprise.

7.2 Dependency Interaction

Tujuan:

Mengelola kebutuhan antar Platform.

Provider tetap memiliki capability ownership.

Consumer tetap memiliki requirement ownership.

7.3 Collaboration Interaction

Tujuan:

Membangun shared outcome.

Ownership tetap terpisah.

13. Boundary Protection

C8 tidak memberikan authority kepada PD-01 untuk:

* mengambil keputusan domain Platform lain;
* mengubah ownership;
* mengelola capability Platform lain;
* menggantikan Platform Leadership.

PD-01 hanya memastikan:

* alignment;
* governance;
* coordination;
* consistency.
