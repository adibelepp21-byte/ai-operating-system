1. Purpose

C10 menetapkan bagaimana AIOS menentukan bahwa governance telah berjalan secara efektif, matang, dan memberikan nilai organisasi.

C10 menjadi penutup resmi Part C — Governance Architecture.

⸻

2. Governance Success Constitution

Governance dianggap berhasil apabila:

* governance berjalan konsisten;
* keputusan dibuat dengan authority yang tepat;
* ownership jelas;
* konflik dapat diselesaikan;
* compliance dapat dipertahankan;
* Platform dapat berkolaborasi tanpa kehilangan ownership;
* governance mampu berkembang mengikuti kebutuhan organisasi.

⸻

3. Governance Success Position of PD-01

3.1 Governance Success Evaluator

PD-01:

* mengevaluasi governance;
* menilai effectiveness;
* mengidentifikasi improvement;
* memastikan outcome dapat diukur.

PD-01 tidak menggantikan domain success criteria Platform lain.

3.2 Governance Maturity Steward

PD-01:

* memantau governance maturity;
* mengidentifikasi maturity gap;
* mengarahkan improvement;
* menjaga governance evolution.

3.3 Organizational Improvement Coordinator

PD-01 mengoordinasikan improvement berdasarkan:

* governance review;
* compliance assessment;
* escalation learning;
* architecture feedback.

3.4 Governance Excellence Guardian

PD-01 menjaga:

* consistency;
* effectiveness;
* transparency;
* accountability;
* continuous improvement.

6. Governance Success Criteria

GSC-01 — Governance Effectiveness

Governance efektif apabila:

* framework diterapkan;
* proses berjalan konsisten;
* keputusan mengikuti mekanisme;
* ownership jelas.

GSC-02 — Strategic Alignment

Governance berhasil apabila:

* Platform bergerak sesuai strategic direction;
* keputusan mendukung objectives;
* architecture direction terjaga.

GSC-03 — Decision Quality

Keberhasilan terlihat melalui:

* decision owner jelas;
* authority sesuai;
* reasoning terdokumentasi;
* keputusan traceable.

GSC-04 — Accountability Maturity

Governance berhasil apabila:

* responsibility tidak ambigu;
* ownership jelas;
* accountability dapat dipertanggungjawabkan.

GSC-05 — Authority Integrity

Governance berhasil apabila:

* authority digunakan sesuai batas;
* delegation terkendali;
* approval mengikuti authority model.

GSC-06 — Cross Platform Collaboration

Governance berhasil apabila:

* Platform dapat bekerja bersama;
* dependency dikelola;
* conflict diselesaikan melalui governance mechanism.

GSC-07 — Governance Compliance Maturity

Governance berhasil apabila:

* requirements dipenuhi;
* evidence tersedia;
* findings ditindaklanjuti;
* improvement berjalan.

GSC-08 — Continuous Governance Evolution

Governance matang apabila mampu:

* menerima feedback;
* memperbaiki kelemahan;
* berkembang mengikuti perubahan AIOS.

8. Governance Maturity Levels

Governance maturity dapat dinilai secara bertahap:

Initial
   ↓
Defined
   ↓
Managed
   ↓
Optimized
   ↓
Adaptive

Maturity tidak boleh ditentukan hanya dari satu score.

Assessment harus menggunakan evidence terhadap:

* governance structure;
* decision quality;
* accountability;
* authority;
* compliance;
* cross-platform governance;
* improvement capability.

⸻

9. Governance Success Review Cycle

Governance Success dievaluasi melalui:

Periodic Review

Evaluasi berkala governance maturity.

Event-Based Review

Evaluasi setelah:

* major change;
* architecture evolution;
* governance issue.

Continuous Improvement Review

Evaluasi berdasarkan:

* compliance result;
* escalation learning;
* organizational feedback.

12. Boundary Protection

Governance Success Constitution tidak memberikan authority kepada PD-01 untuk:

* menentukan seluruh success metric Platform lain;
* mengambil ownership outcome domain lain;
* menggantikan Platform leadership.

PD-01 hanya:

* mengevaluasi governance effectiveness;
* menjaga maturity;
* mengoordinasikan improvement.

⸻

13. Governance Success Statement

Governance Success Constitution berhasil apabila:

* governance structure jelas;
* governance mechanism efektif;
* decision quality terjaga;
* accountability maturity meningkat;
* authority integrity terjaga;
* cross-platform alignment efektif;
* compliance dapat diverifikasi;
* continuous improvement berjalan.
