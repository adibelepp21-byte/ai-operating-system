# PART B — ORGANIZATION ARCHITECTURE
Part B defines how the strategic and constitutional foundation established in Part A is translated into organizational structure.
The architectural chain is:
```text
Part A — Strategic Foundation
        ↓
B1 — Organizational Model
        ↓
B2 — Executive Sub Division Architecture
        ↓
B3 — Executive Sub Division Capability Architecture
        ↓
B4 — Executive Team Architecture
        ↓
B5 — Executive Role Group Architecture
```

The fundamental design chain is:

Strategic Mandate
        ↓
Organization
        ↓
Sub Division
        ↓
Capability
        ↓
Team
        ↓
Role Group
        ↓
Workforce Role

Part B does not define individual people.

It defines the organizational architecture required to execute the mandate of PD-01.

1. Purpose

B1 defines the organizational model of PD-01 Executive Office.

If Part A defines:

* identity;
* purpose;
* mission;
* charter;
* authority;
* boundary;
* principles;
* objectives;
* success criteria;
* relationship to the AIOS Architecture Baseline;

then B1 answers:

“Bagaimana PD-01 harus diorganisasikan agar mandat tersebut dapat dijalankan secara efektif tanpa mengambil alih domain Platform lain?”

B1 therefore establishes the organizational layers from Executive Leadership through organizational enablement and executive support.

⸻

2. Organizational Design Principles

OD-01 — Organization Follows Mandate

The organization must be derived from the mandate of PD-01.

Organizational structures must not be created merely because a conventional enterprise organization normally contains such functions.

⸻

OD-02 — Ownership Before Assignment

Every organizational domain must have a clear owner before responsibility is assigned.

There must be no:

* function without owner;
* decision without authority;
* capability without responsibility.

⸻

OD-03 — Strategic Layer Separation

PD-01 separates organizational responsibility into distinct layers:

Strategic Leadership Layer
        ↓
Governance & Management Layer
        ↓
Enterprise Alignment Layer
        ↓
Organizational Enablement Layer
        ↓
Executive Support Layer

Each layer has a distinct responsibility.

⸻

OD-04 — Platform Boundary Preservation

PD-01 is the enterprise Executive Office.

It is responsible for:

* strategic direction;
* enterprise governance;
* decision authority;
* organizational alignment;
* cross-platform coordination.

PD-01 does not become the operator of every Platform.

It does not own:

* daily operations of other Platforms;
* technical implementation of other Platforms;
* domain execution belonging to another Platform;
* operational decisions delegated to Platform Owners.

⸻

OD-05 — Clear Domain Ownership

Each organizational domain should have one primary owner.

Collaboration does not create duplicate ownership.

⸻

OD-06 — Capability-Based Organization

The organization must eventually be decomposable into:

Organization
    ↓
Sub Division
    ↓
Capability
    ↓
Team
    ↓
Role Group

The structure must not become activity-driven or person-driven.

⸻

OD-07 — Reference Pattern

The organizational model must be reusable by PD-02 through PD-10.

The pattern is inherited.

The domain content is not.

⸻

⸻

9. Organizational Contract

The Executive Office commits to:

* lead according to the Architecture Baseline;
* make decisions transparently and accountably;
* preserve enterprise governance consistency;
* respect Platform domain boundaries;
* support sustainable organizational growth;
* ensure strategic decisions create value for AIOS as a whole.

Each Platform Division is expected to:

* comply with the Architecture Baseline;
* implement applicable governance;
* participate in cross-platform coordination;
* preserve its own domain integrity;
* report strategic issues affecting the wider organization.

⸻

10. Executive Authority Boundary

The Executive Office shall not:

* execute the daily operations of another Platform;
* take over another Platform’s domain ownership without legitimate governance authority;
* unilaterally alter the Architecture Baseline outside Architecture Change Control;
* interfere with operational decisions belonging to a Platform Owner unless there is a legitimate enterprise-level impact, governance violation, or authorized escalation.

⸻
