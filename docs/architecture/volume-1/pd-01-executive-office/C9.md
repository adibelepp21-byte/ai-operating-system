1. Purpose

C9 menetapkan bagaimana seluruh governance system diperiksa, dinilai, diverifikasi, diaudit, dan ditingkatkan secara berkelanjutan.

Governance Compliance memastikan governance:

* diterapkan;
* dipatuhi;
* dapat diverifikasi;
* dapat diaudit;
* dapat ditingkatkan.

⸻

2. Governance Compliance Constitution

Konstitusi ini menetapkan:

* setiap Platform bertanggung jawab terhadap compliance domain;
* PD-01 menyediakan compliance framework;
* assessment menggunakan standar yang konsisten;
* evidence terdokumentasi;
* finding memiliki corrective action;
* improvement dikembalikan ke governance system.

⸻

3. Governance Compliance Position of PD-01

3.1 Governance Assurance Coordinator

PD-01:

* menjaga consistency assessment;
* memastikan review sesuai standard;
* mengoordinasikan assurance cycle;
* mengonsolidasikan assessment.

3.2 Compliance Framework Owner

PD-01:

* mendefinisikan compliance model;
* menetapkan assessment standard;
* menjaga governance requirement;
* memelihara methodology.

3.3 Assessment Facilitator

PD-01 memfasilitasi:

* assessment planning;
* evidence coordination;
* review facilitation;
* finding management.

3.4 Governance Compliance Guardian

PD-01 menjaga governance assurance tetap konsisten dengan architecture dan governance baseline.

⸻

4. Compliance Principles

1. Evidence Before Conclusion.
2. Requirement Before Assessment.
3. Ownership Remains with Domain Owner.
4. Compliance Does Not Equal Execution.
5. Findings Must Be Traceable.
6. Corrective Action Must Have Owner.
7. Closure Must Be Validated.
8. Material Issues Must Be Escalated.
9. Compliance Results Feed Governance Improvement.

⸻

5. Compliance Scope

C9 mencakup:

* governance requirements;
* governance assessment;
* compliance evidence;
* auditability;
* finding management;
* corrective action;
* validation;
* closure;
* cross-platform compliance;
* governance improvement.

7. Compliance Assessment

Assessment harus membedakan:

* compliant;
* partially compliant;
* non-compliant;
* insufficient evidence;
* not applicable.

Tidak boleh membuat conclusion tanpa evidence yang cukup.

⸻

8. Finding Management

Setiap finding memiliki:

Attribute	Description
Finding ID	Identitas temuan
Description	Deskripsi masalah
Impact	Dampak governance
Owner	Pemilik penyelesaian
Action Plan	Rencana perbaikan
Due Date	Target
Validation Status	Status verifikasi

⸻

9. Governance Compliance Documentation

Seluruh compliance activity memiliki:

* assessment record;
* audit evidence;
* review result;
* finding register;
* corrective action;
* closure approval.

Dokumentasi menjadi bagian Governance Repository AIOS.

⸻

10. Compliance Review

Compliance dapat direview apabila:

* requirement berubah;
* architecture berubah;
* governance berubah;
* material finding muncul;
* corrective action gagal;
* evidence berubah.

⸻

11. Cross Platform Compliance Governance

PD-01:

* menetapkan framework;
* menyediakan assessment standard;
* memfasilitasi review;
* menjaga enterprise consistency.

PD-01 tidak:

* mengambil compliance ownership Platform lain;
* mengoperasikan compliance domain lain;
* menggantikan Platform governance owner.
