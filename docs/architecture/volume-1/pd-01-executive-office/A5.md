⸻

1. Purpose

A5 menjawab:

Siapa berhak memutuskan apa?

A5 menerjemahkan Executive Charter menjadi authority dan decision model.

⸻

2. Authority Categories

PD-01 menggunakan kategori:

1. Constitutional Authority
2. Governance Authority
3. Decision Authority
4. Approval Authority
5. Override Authority
6. Delegated Authority
7. Advisory Authority

⸻

3. Decision Authority

Decision authority mencakup keputusan:

* strategic direction;
* enterprise priorities;
* cross-platform matters;
* material organizational changes;
* enterprise policy;
* strategic portfolio;
* architecture-related enterprise matters;
* unresolved cross-platform conflicts.

⸻

4. Approval Authority

Approval diperlukan untuk material matters yang:

* mengubah enterprise direction;
* berdampak lintas Platform;
* mengubah governance;
* mengubah material architecture;
* mengubah organizational boundary;
* memerlukan enterprise-level commitment.

⸻

5. Delegated Authority

Delegation harus menentukan:

Element	Requirement
Authority Owner	wajib
Delegate	wajib
Scope	wajib
Duration	wajib
Constraints	wajib
Accountability	wajib
Record	wajib

Delegation:

Authority Delegation
        ≠
Ownership Transfer

⸻

6. Escalation

Escalation digunakan apabila:

* authority conflict;
* ownership conflict;
* unresolved cross-platform issue;
* material strategic risk;
* architecture/governance conflict;
* decision berada di luar delegated scope.

⸻

7. Decision Lifecycle

Issue
  ↓
Context
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Options
  ↓
Assessment
  ↓
Decision Authority
  ↓
Decision
  ↓
Record
  ↓
Implementation
  ↓
Review

⸻

8. Decision Quality Requirements

Material decision harus memiliki:

* context;
* evidence;
* options;
* rationale;
* authority;
* consequence;
* owner;
* record.

⸻

9. Relationship to Other Platforms

PD-01 memiliki enterprise decision authority pada matters yang memang
enterprise-level.

Platform lain tetap memiliki decision authority dalam domain masing-masing.

Prinsip:

Enterprise Decision Authority
        ≠
Universal Operational Decision Authority

⸻

10. Success Statement

A5 berhasil apabila:

* authority jelas;
* decision owner jelas;
* delegation terdokumentasi;
* escalation tersedia;
* approval boundary jelas;
* keputusan dapat diaudit;
* Platform autonomy tetap dipertahankan.
