1. Purpose

E8 mendefinisikan bagaimana hasil performance digunakan untuk memperbaiki, mengoreksi, mengoptimalkan, dan mengembangkan Platform secara berkelanjutan.

E8 menjawab:

“Bagaimana hasil performance digunakan untuk berkembang?”

E8 mencakup:

1. Performance Gap Identification;
2. Improvement Initiative;
3. Corrective Action;
4. Optimization Cycle.

⸻

2. Performance Improvement Principle

Performance improvement is an evidence-driven feedback process that converts validated performance gaps and findings into authorized changes, verifies their effectiveness through re-measurement, and feeds the resulting learning back into the next performance cycle.

Gap
 ≠
Action

dan:

Action Completed
 ≠
Improvement Effective

⸻

5. Gap Identification

Material gap harus:

* evidence-based;
* traceable;
* classified;
* materiality-assessed.

Gap harus dibedakan dari ordinary variance.

Source of gap tidak boleh ditentukan tanpa evidence.

⸻

6. Improvement Initiative

Material improvement harus memiliki:

* objective;
* expected result;
* owner;
* authority requirement;
* success measurement;
* implementation state;
* effectiveness verification.

⸻

7. Corrective Action

Corrective Action menangani identified condition yang membutuhkan correction.

Corrective Action ≠ general improvement.

⸻

8. Optimization

Optimization bertujuan meningkatkan effectiveness/efficiency setelah material baseline conditions terjaga.

Optimization tidak boleh mengorbankan material performance dimensions lain.

Optimization ≠ uncontrolled change.

⸻

9. Verification

Action
   ↓
Implementation
   ↓
Re-measurement
   ↓
Effectiveness Assessment

Completion tidak membuktikan effectiveness.

⸻

10. Improvement States

Possible states:

* Identified;
* Proposed;
* Authorized;
* In Progress;
* Blocked;
* Implemented;
* Under Verification;
* Effective;
* Ineffective;
* Re-diagnosis Required;
* Closed.

Closed tidak boleh digunakan untuk menyembunyikan unresolved material condition.

⸻

11. Canonical Boundary

PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
        │
        ├── identifies gap
        ├── evaluates need
        ├── defines improvement measurement
        ├── tracks improvement
        ├── verifies effectiveness
        └── generates learning
                │
                ▼
        AUTHORIZED OWNER
                │
                ├── decides where authorized
                ├── executes change
                └── owns implementation
                │
                ▼
          NEW PERFORMANCE
                │
                ▼
          RE-MEASUREMENT

Performance improvement is not automatic authority.

Action completion is not effectiveness.

Corrective action is not optimization.

Optimization is not uncontrolled change.

Improvement cannot hide regression.

Target manipulation is not improvement.

Metric gaming is not improvement.

Performance Management verifies outcome; action owner remains accountable for execution.

13. Success Criteria

E8 berhasil apabila:

1. material gap dapat diidentifikasi;
2. gap dibedakan dari ordinary variance;
3. source of gap tidak premature;
4. improvement signal memiliki evidence;
5. initiative memiliki objective/expected result;
6. corrective action dibedakan;
7. accountable owner ada;
8. authority dapat diidentifikasi;
9. implementation ≠ effectiveness;
10. effectiveness diverifikasi;
11. residual gap terlihat;
12. ineffective improvement memicu re-diagnosis;
13. optimization dibedakan dari correction;
14. optimization tidak merusak dimension lain;
15. blocked/escalated states tersedia;
16. history auditable;
17. regression dapat dideteksi;
18. learning masuk future cycles;
19. reporting terintegrasi E7;
20. governance mengikuti E6;
21. strategy/governance/architecture tetap pada authority domain;
22. framework inheritable.
