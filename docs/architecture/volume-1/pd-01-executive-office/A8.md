1. Purpose

A8 menerjemahkan Strategic Purpose dan constitutional foundation PD-01 menjadi
Strategic Objectives.

Setiap objective mengikuti:

Strategic Theme
      ↓
Strategic Objective
      ↓
Strategic Initiative
      ↓
Success Metrics
      ↓
Target Outcome

⸻

2. Strategic Theme ST-01 — Enterprise Leadership

Strategic Objective

Memastikan AIOS memiliki arah strategis enterprise yang jelas dan konsisten.

Strategic Initiatives

* strategic planning;
* enterprise priority setting;
* strategic portfolio management;
* long-term direction;
* strategic review.

Success Metrics

* strategic objectives memiliki owner;
* strategic priorities terdokumentasi;
* Platform roadmap memiliki strategic alignment.

Target Outcome

AIOS memiliki arah strategis enterprise yang jelas dan dapat diterjemahkan
ke Platform.

⸻

3. Strategic Theme ST-02 — Enterprise Governance

Strategic Objective

Memastikan AIOS memiliki governance yang konsisten, transparan, accountable,
dan dapat diaudit.

Strategic Initiatives

* governance framework;
* policy management;
* governance review;
* accountability model;
* governance change control.

Success Metrics

* governance decisions terdokumentasi;
* policy ownership jelas;
* governance conflicts dapat diselesaikan.

Target Outcome

AIOS memiliki governance enterprise yang coherent dan accountable.

⸻

4. Strategic Theme ST-03 — Architecture Integrity

Strategic Objective

Menjaga integritas Architecture Baseline sebagai sumber kebenaran arsitektur
organisasi.

Strategic Initiatives

* Architecture Compliance Review;
* Architecture Change Control;
* baseline stewardship;
* Platform Encyclopedia consistency;
* architecture alignment.

Success Metrics

* material architecture changes terdokumentasi;
* architectural deviations teridentifikasi;
* Architecture Baseline tetap konsisten.

Target Outcome

Architecture Baseline tetap stabil, coherent, canonical, dan menjadi acuan
seluruh Platform Division.

⸻

5. Strategic Theme ST-04 — Cross-Platform Coordination

Strategic Objective

Meningkatkan efektivitas koordinasi dan kolaborasi antar Platform Division.

Strategic Initiatives

* cross-platform forums;
* strategic issue management;
* dependency alignment;
* conflict resolution;
* cross-platform program coordination.

Success Metrics

* cross-platform conflicts dapat diselesaikan;
* dependencies terdokumentasi;
* program lintas Platform memiliki governance.

Target Outcome

Seluruh Platform Division dapat bekerja sebagai satu sistem organisasi
terintegrasi.

⸻

6. Strategic Theme ST-05 — Organizational Excellence

Strategic Objective

Mendorong peningkatan kualitas organisasi secara berkelanjutan melalui
evaluasi, pembelajaran, capability improvement, dan controlled innovation.

Strategic Initiatives

* Organizational Maturity Assessment;
* capability improvement roadmap;
* organizational review;
* continuous improvement;
* innovation governance.

Success Metrics

* maturity meningkat;
* improvement actions ditindaklanjuti;
* organizational findings ditutup secara sistematis.

Target Outcome

AIOS berkembang secara berkelanjutan tanpa kehilangan stabilitas
architecture dan governance.

⸻

8. Strategic Alignment Rules

Setiap objective harus:

1. traceable terhadap Strategic Purpose;
2. sesuai organizational boundary;
3. memiliki initiative;
4. memiliki success metric;
5. memiliki target outcome;
6. dapat diterjemahkan ke Performance Architecture;
7. tidak mengambil domain ownership Platform lain.

⸻

9. Transition to Performance

A8 menjadi input bagi:

* KPI;
* Organizational Performance Dashboard;
* Executive Performance Review;
* Strategic Portfolio Review;
* Governance Performance Assessment;
* Organizational Maturity Assessment.

⸻

10. Success Statement

A8 berhasil apabila seluruh strategic themes memiliki objectives yang jelas,
distinct, measurable, traceable, dan dapat diturunkan ke execution serta
performance management.
