1. Purpose

E7 menetapkan bagaimana informasi performance dikemas, dikomunikasikan, didistribusikan, dan digunakan untuk mendukung review serta decision-making.

E7 menjawab:

“Bagaimana informasi performa dikomunikasikan?”

E7 mencakup:

1. Reporting Hierarchy;
2. Executive Reporting;
3. Platform Reporting;
4. Transparency Model;
5. Decision Support.

⸻

2. Reporting Principle

Performance reporting translates validated performance evidence and approved evaluation into the right level of information for the right consumer at the right time, with sufficient context to support accurate interpretation.

Reporting harus:

* accurate;
* traceable;
* contextual;
* proportionate;
* audience-appropriate;
* timely;
* transparent within legitimate access boundaries;
* decision-useful.

4. Reporting Boundary

E7 memiliki ownership atas reporting capability.

E7 tidak:

* mengubah measurement;
* menciptakan evaluation baru tanpa evidence;
* menciptakan governance authority;
* mengambil decision authority.

⸻

5. Reporting Hierarchy

Reporting dapat memiliki:

* Domain / Operational Reporting;
* Platform Reporting;
* Executive Reporting.

Hierarchy reporting bukan hierarchy authority.

Level reporting menentukan:

* audience;
* aggregation;
* context;
* decision-support need.

⸻

6. Executive Reporting

Executive reporting harus:

* prioritize materiality;
* provide decision-relevant context;
* preserve evidence lineage;
* distinguish actual, target, variance, trend, and evaluation;
* expose material exceptions.

Executive summary tidak boleh menghilangkan material weakness.

⸻

7. Platform Reporting

Platform reporting mempertahankan lima performance dimensions:

* Strategic;
* Operational;
* Governance;
* Capability;
* Improvement.

Platform reporting harus memberikan integrated performance context.

⸻

8. Transparency Model

Transparency tidak berarti unrestricted disclosure.

Information harus tersedia kepada legitimate consumer dengan:

* provenance;
* context;
* confidence;
* limitation;
* access boundary.

Restricted information tetap membutuhkan traceability.

⸻

10. Reporting Integrity

Reporting harus membedakan:

* actual;
* target;
* variance;
* trend;
* evaluation;
* confidence;
* limitation;
* recommendation;
* decision.

Dashboard bukan performance reality.

Aggregation bukan permission to hide material exceptions.

12. Success Criteria

E7 berhasil apabila:

1. material performance information memiliki consumer;
2. hierarchy membedakan evidence/domain/platform/executive context;
3. executive reporting materiality-driven;
4. platform reporting mempertahankan five dimensions;
5. reporting traceable;
6. actual/target/variance/trend/evaluation dibedakan;
7. material exceptions tidak tersembunyi;
8. transparency berada dalam access boundaries;
9. restricted information traceable;
10. recommendation ≠ decision;
11. decision authority identifiable;
12. reporting tidak menciptakan authority;
13. reports memiliki version/history;
14. corrections memiliki audit trail;
15. confidence/limitation dapat dikomunikasikan;
16. event-based reporting tersedia;
17. dashboard memiliki context;
18. decision-support usable;
19. reporting dapat memicu escalation tanpa mengambil authority;
20. framework inheritable.
