1. Purpose

D7 defines how people, Teams, Sub Divisions, Executive Office, and
Platforms collaborate to execute organizational work.

D3 coordinates Platform relationships.

D7 defines broader organizational collaboration.

2. Collaboration Principles

CL-01 — Purposeful Collaboration

Collaboration exists to achieve a defined outcome.

CL-02 — Ownership Preservation

Collaboration does not erase ownership.

CL-03 — Clear Participation

Participants and responsibilities must be understood.

CL-04 — Transparent Communication

Material information must reach affected participants.

CL-05 — Respect for Authority

Collaboration does not create unauthorized decision rights.

3. Collaboration Participants

Participants may include:

* Executive Office;
* Sub Division;
* Team;
* Role Group;
* Platform Division;
* domain owner;
* initiative owner;
* specialist contributor.

4. Collaboration Levels

Level 1 — Inform

Provide relevant information.

Level 2 — Consult

Seek expertise or context.

Level 3 — Coordinate

Synchronize dependent activity.

Level 4 — Collaborate

Jointly execute toward an outcome.

Level 5 — Integrate

Operate through sustained cross-domain interaction.

7. Collaboration Communication Model

Communication should define:

* audience;
* purpose;
* information;
* timing;
* channel;
* owner;
* expected response.

8. Collaboration Responsibilities

Participants are responsible for:

* fulfilling agreed contribution;
* communicating material changes;
* surfacing blockers;
* respecting ownership;
* preserving required evidence.

9. Collaboration Constraints

Collaboration must not:

* override authority;
* transfer ownership implicitly;
* bypass governance;
* bypass compliance;
* obscure accountability.

10. Collaboration Review

Collaboration should be reviewed through:

* outcome quality;
* coordination efficiency;
* unresolved friction;
* communication quality;
* dependency resolution.

11. Collaboration Success Statement

D7 succeeds when organizational collaboration is:

* purposeful;
* clear;
* ownership-preserving;
* timely;
* transparent;
* outcome-oriented;
* reviewable.
