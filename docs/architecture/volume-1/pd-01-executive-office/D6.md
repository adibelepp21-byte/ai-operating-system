D6 — OPERATING RHYTHM CONSTITUTION

Section ID: D6
Section Name: Operating Rhythm Constitution
Platform ID: PD-01
Parent: D5
Status: RECOVERY CANDIDATE

1. Purpose

D6 establishes the temporal architecture of operations.

D6 answers:

When and at what rhythm is organizational operation performed?

D6 is not a meeting calendar alone.

2. Operating Rhythm Principles

OR-01 — Purposeful Cadence

Every recurring rhythm must have a purpose.

OR-02 — Appropriate Frequency

Frequency must match operational need.

OR-03 — Synchronization

Different operating cycles must remain synchronized.

OR-04 — Review Integration

Review must occur at appropriate rhythm boundaries.

OR-05 — Avoid Ritualism

Meetings and recurring activities must not exist solely because they
are recurring.

3. Daily Operating Cycle

Daily operations focus on:

* active work;
* immediate blockers;
* urgent decisions;
* critical dependencies;
* operational awareness.

4. Weekly Operating Cycle

Weekly coordination focuses on:

* work synchronization;
* cross-functional dependency;
* cross-platform coordination;
* priority confirmation;
* emerging exceptions.

5. Monthly Operating Cycle

Monthly review focuses on:

* operational condition;
* initiative progress;
* recurring issues;
* resource pressure;
* improvement opportunities.

6. Quarterly Operating Cycle

Quarterly review focuses on:

* strategic alignment;
* operating effectiveness;
* major dependencies;
* architecture/governance implications;
* improvement priorities.

7. Annual Operating Cycle

Annual assessment focuses on:

* Operating Model effectiveness;
* organizational operating maturity;
* structural operating lessons;
* strategic alignment;
* next-cycle improvement priorities.

8. Rhythm Synchronization

Daily
  ↓
Weekly
  ↓
Monthly
  ↓
Quarterly
  ↓
Annual

Information and decisions should flow between levels.

9. Governance Interface

Operating rhythm must align with applicable:

* governance review;
* decision cycles;
* compliance cycles;
* executive review;
* escalation mechanisms.

10. Rhythm Constraints

D6 must avoid:

* unnecessary meeting load;
* duplicate reviews;
* conflicting cadence;
* review without evidence;
* operational delay caused by ritual.

11. Rhythm Review

Cadence is reviewed when:

* organizational scale changes;
* work characteristics change;
* recurring reviews become ineffective;
* dependencies change;
* governance requirements change.

12. Operating Rhythm Success Statement

D6 succeeds when the organization has a predictable but adaptable
operating cadence that synchronizes execution, coordination,
review, and improvement.
