# D1 — OPERATING MODEL CONSTITUTION

> Section ID: D1
> Section Name: Operating Model Constitution
> Platform ID: PD-01
> Official Name: Executive Office
> Part: D — Operating Architecture
> Status: RECOVERY CANDIDATE
> Classification: Operating Constitution

## 1. Purpose

D1 establishes the constitutional operating model of PD-01 Executive
Office.

D1 answers:

> How does the organization operate as a coherent system?

D1 is the parent constitution for D2–D10.

No operational mechanism may intentionally operate outside the
Operating Constitution established by D1.

D1 does not define detailed governance authority, organizational
structure, performance measurement, or platform-specific implementation.

Those concerns remain in their respective architectural domains.

## 2. Operating Model Objectives

### OM-01 — Operational Consistency

Ensure organizational activities are performed consistently across
the Executive Office.

### OM-02 — Operational Alignment

Ensure operations remain aligned with:

- strategic direction;
- Governance Constitution;
- Architecture Baseline;
- approved organizational boundaries.

### OM-03 — Operational Efficiency

Ensure organizational capabilities are executed effectively and
efficiently.

### OM-04 — Operational Transparency

Ensure operational processes are documented, understandable, and
visible to appropriate participants.

### OM-05 — Operational Traceability

Ensure operational activities can be traced throughout their
lifecycle.

### OM-06 — Continuous Operational Improvement

Ensure operations continuously learn, improve, and mature.

## 3. Operating Philosophy

### OP-01 — Strategy Drives Operations

Operations implement approved organizational direction.

Operations do not redefine strategy.

### OP-02 — Governance Enables Operations

Governance establishes rules, authority, control, and accountability
within which operations execute.

Governance does not replace operational execution.

### OP-03 — Architecture Before Execution

Operations must remain consistent with the applicable Architecture
Baseline.

### OP-04 — Process Before Activity

Operational work should follow defined processes rather than
undocumented individual habits.

### OP-05 — Improvement by Design

Operating mechanisms must permit review, learning, and improvement.

## 4. Operating Principles

### OPR-01 — Explicit Ownership

Every operational activity must have an identifiable owner.

### OPR-02 — Standardized Execution

Recurring operational processes should use documented standards.

### OPR-03 — Cross-Platform Collaboration

Cross-platform operations use defined coordination mechanisms.

### OPR-04 — Controlled Flexibility

Operational flexibility is permitted only within established
governance, architecture, authority, and ownership boundaries.

### OPR-05 — Measurable Operations

Operational activities should generate evidence suitable for
monitoring and evaluation.

### OPR-06 — Continuous Review

Operations are periodically reviewed for alignment and effectiveness.

## 6. Operating System Relationship

The operating model follows:

Strategy
→ Governance
→ Architecture
→ Planning
→ Execution
→ Coordination
→ Monitoring
→ Review
→ Improvement
→ Standardization

No operating activity should intentionally bypass applicable
governance or architecture constraints.

## 7. Operating Rhythm

The organization operates through multiple temporal cycles:

| Rhythm | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Daily Operations | Execute ongoing operational work |
| Weekly Coordination | Synchronize work and dependencies |
| Monthly Review | Review operational condition |
| Quarterly Strategic Review | Assess alignment with strategy |
| Annual Operating Assessment | Assess Operating Model effectiveness |

These rhythms are elaborated by D6.

## 8. Governance Interface

D1 interfaces with Governance Architecture through:

- Governance Constitution;
- Decision Constitution;
- Delegation Constitution;
- Accountability Constitution;
- Authority Constitution;
- Executive Review Constitution;
- Escalation Constitution;
- Cross Platform Governance Constitution;
- Governance Compliance Constitution;

Governance controls the legitimacy and authority of operations.

Operations execute within those boundaries.

## 9. Operating Constraints

Operations must not:

- violate Architecture Baseline;
- cross established domain boundaries without authorization;
- exceed delegated authority;
- alter organizational ownership without authorized decision;
- bypass required governance;
- bypass required review;
- conceal material operational exceptions.

11. Operating Success Statement

The Operating Model succeeds when:

* operations are aligned;
* ownership is explicit;
* execution is repeatable;
* coordination is effective;
* decisions are traceable;
* operational control is maintained;
* improvement is continuous;
* operational evidence is available;
