D8 — OPERATIONAL CONTROL CONSTITUTION

Section ID: D8
Section Name: Operational Control Constitution
Platform ID: PD-01
Parent: D7
Status: RECOVERY CANDIDATE

1. Purpose

D8 ensures operations remain:

* aligned;
* traceable;
* measurable;
* controlled.

D8 is not governance compliance.

D8 is not performance management.

Governance asks whether the organization is operating within
authorized rules.

Operational Control asks whether operations remain under control
while execution is occurring.

2. Operational Control Principles

OC-01 — Alignment Control

Operations remain aligned with approved objectives.

OC-02 — Traceability Control

Material activities remain traceable.

OC-03 — Evidence Control

Control relies on observable evidence.

OC-04 — Exception Visibility

Material deviations must become visible.

OC-05 — Corrective Response

Material control failures require appropriate response.

3. Alignment Control Model

Control checks alignment against:

* strategy;
* governance;
* architecture;
* organizational boundary;
* approved work.

4. Traceability Model

Operational traceability connects:

Objective
↓
Work
↓
Owner
↓
Action
↓
Evidence
↓
Review
↓
Outcome

5. Measurement Model

Operational measurement may include:

* completion;
* timeliness;
* workload;
* dependency condition;
* exception frequency;
* process adherence;
* operational stability.

D8 does not replace Part E performance measurement.

6. Operational Control Points

Control points may occur at:

* work initiation;
* planning;
* authorization;
* execution;
* review;
* closure;
* exception;
* change.

7. Operational Monitoring

Monitoring identifies:

* deviation;
* blocker;
* delay;
* dependency failure;
* control failure;
* unexpected consequence.

8. Operational Exception Control

Exceptions should be:

1. detected;
2. classified;
3. recorded;
4. owned;
5. assessed;
6. resolved or escalated;
7. reviewed.

9. Operational Reporting

Reporting should distinguish:

* normal condition;
* at-risk condition;
* blocked condition;
* exception;
* resolved condition.

10. Operational Review

Review determines:

* whether control remains effective;
* whether corrective action is required;
* whether escalation is required;
* whether improvement is appropriate.

11. Continuous Control Improvement

Control mechanisms themselves may be improved when:

* recurring exceptions occur;
* control points are ineffective;
* evidence is insufficient;
* processes create unnecessary friction.

12. Operational Control Success Statement

D8 succeeds when operations remain:

* aligned;
* observable;
* traceable;
* measurable;
* controlled;
* responsive to deviation.
