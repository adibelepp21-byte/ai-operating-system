Platform ID: PD-01
Parent: D8
Status: FROZEN

1. Purpose

D9 establishes the constitutional mechanism for continuous
improvement of the Operating Model.

D8 keeps operations under control.

D9 makes operations better.

2. Operating Improvement Principles

OI-01 — Evidence-Based Improvement

Improvement should be based on evidence.

OI-02 — Root-Cause Orientation

Improvement should address underlying causes where practical.

OI-03 — Controlled Change

Improvement must respect governance and architecture boundaries.

OI-04 — Measured Effectiveness

Completed action is not automatically effective improvement.

OI-05 — Continuous Learning

Lessons from operations should improve future operations.

4. Operational Assessment Model

Assessment considers:

* effectiveness;
* efficiency;
* stability;
* adaptability;
* recurring friction;
* maturity;
* stakeholder impact.

5. Improvement Opportunity Management

Each material opportunity should identify:

* observed condition;
* evidence;
* impact;
* root cause where known;
* proposed improvement;
* owner;
* priority;
* expected outcome.

6. Innovation Integration

Innovation may be introduced when it improves:

* operational capability;
* efficiency;
* adaptability;
* quality;
* scalability.

Innovation must not bypass architecture or governance.

7. Lessons Learned Management

Lessons learned should capture:

* context;
* observation;
* consequence;
* lesson;
* recommended change;
* applicability.

8. Improvement Prioritization

Prioritization considers:

* impact;
* urgency;
* strategic alignment;
* cost;
* complexity;
* risk;
* reversibility;
* expected benefit.

10. Improvement Validation

Validation distinguishes:

Action Completed

from:

Improvement Effective

An improvement is effective only when evidence indicates that
the intended condition improved.

11. Continuous Learning Mechanism

Learning feeds:

* operating standards;
* work practices;
* coordination;
* control;
* future planning;
* capability development.

12. Operating Improvement Success Statement

D9 succeeds when the Operating Model systematically learns,
improves, adapts, and standardizes effective improvements.
