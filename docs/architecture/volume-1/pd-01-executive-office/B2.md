1. Purpose

B2 translates the B1 organizational model into explicit Executive Sub Divisions.

B2 answers:

“What organizational Sub Divisions are required inside PD-01 to own and coordinate the Executive Office mandate?”

The design target is clear ownership without unnecessary fragmentation.

⸻

2. Sub Division Design Principles

ESD-01 — One Primary Domain Per Sub Division

Each Sub Division has a primary responsibility domain.

⸻

ESD-02 — Ownership Clarity

Every Sub Division must have:

* purpose;
* domain ownership;
* responsibility boundary;
* outputs;
* relationship to other Sub Divisions.

⸻

ESD-03 — No Cross-Platform Execution Ownership

An Executive Sub Division may coordinate or govern a Platform domain without becoming the execution owner of that domain.

⸻

ESD-04 — Separation of Concern

Related functions may collaborate but must not be merged merely for convenience when their authority, responsibility, or outputs differ materially.

⸻

ESD-05 — Reusable Pattern

The structural pattern may be inherited by PD-02 through PD-10.

The actual domain names and capabilities are platform-specific.

⸻

5. Boundary Rules

Rule 01 — Executive Office Leads, Not Operates

Executive Office establishes direction, governance, and alignment.

It does not become the operator of every Platform.

⸻

Rule 02 — Coordination Does Not Own Domain

ESD-06 coordinates Platforms.

It does not own their domain capabilities.

⸻

Rule 03 — Measurement Does Not Define Strategy

ESD-08 measures and evaluates performance.

It does not independently define enterprise strategy.

⸻

Rule 04 — Support Does Not Create Authority

ESD-10 provides support.

Support activity does not create decision authority.

⸻

Rule 05 — Governance Does Not Become Implementation

Governance Sub Divisions establish or assure governance.

They do not absorb the execution responsibilities of Platform Owners.

⸻

7. Cross-Platform Boundary

PD-01 may:

* establish enterprise direction;
* define enterprise governance;
* coordinate Platforms;
* review architecture;
* govern decisions;
* evaluate enterprise performance;
* coordinate risk and compliance.

PD-01 may not:

* become the technical owner of another Platform;
* replace Platform capability ownership;
* execute another Platform’s daily operation;
* redefine another Platform’s domain without legitimate governance authority.

9. Success Criteria

B2 succeeds when:

* all major Executive Office functions have an owner;
* no Sub Division has contradictory primary responsibilities;
* domain boundaries are clear;
* the model can be decomposed into capabilities;
* the model can support Team and Role Group architecture;
* the model can be reused without transferring PD-01-specific domain content to other Platforms.
