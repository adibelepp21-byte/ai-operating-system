Section A6 — Organizational Boundary

Section ID: A6

Platform ID: PD-01
Official Name: Executive Office
Volume: Volume 1
Status: FROZEN

⸻

1. Purpose

A6 menetapkan:

* domain PD-01;
* out-of-scope domain;
* shared responsibility;
* ownership;
* cross-platform relationship;
* escalation boundary.

⸻

2. Boundary Principles

1. Single Domain Ownership
2. Governance Before Intervention
3. Platform Autonomy
4. Explicit Responsibility
5. Cross-Platform Collaboration
6. Escalation Before Override

⸻

3. In Scope

PD-01 memiliki primary ownership atas:

Domain	Description
Enterprise Strategy	visi, arah, sasaran strategis
Enterprise Governance	governance enterprise
Executive Decision Management	keputusan strategis
Organizational Leadership	leadership AIOS
Enterprise Policy	policy lintas Platform
Architecture Stewardship	stewardship Architecture Baseline
Cross-Platform Coordination	koordinasi strategis
Enterprise Portfolio Oversight	strategic portfolio
Organizational Performance Oversight	strategic performance
Architecture Change Governance	enterprise architecture change

⸻

4. Out of Scope

PD-01 tidak memiliki operational ownership terhadap:

* Software Engineering;
* Product Development;
* Data Engineering;
* AI Engineering;
* Cybersecurity Operations;
* Cloud Operations;
* Infrastructure Operations;
* Network Operations;
* IT Operations;
* Business Operations;
* Customer Operations;
* Marketing Operations;
* Finance Operations;
* HR Operations.

Domain tersebut tetap berada pada Platform/domain owner yang sah.

⸻

5. Shared Responsibility

Shared responsibility dapat terjadi pada:

* Architecture;
* Governance;
* Risk;
* Performance;
* Security;
* Quality;
* Cross-platform programs.

Shared responsibility harus selalu menetapkan primary owner.

⸻

6. Ownership Rule

Each Platform Owns Its Domain.

Relationship:

Relationship	PD-01	Other Platform
Strategy Alignment	Governance Owner	Contributor
Domain Execution	Oversight	Primary Owner
Cross-Platform Program	Coordinator	Participant
Architecture Compliance	Governance Authority	Compliance Owner
Domain Capability	No Direct Ownership	Capability Owner

Baris Architecture Compliance mengikuti C8 §8 — Shared Responsibility Model.
Governance Compliance berada pada Governance Authority; Architecture
Consistency tetap berada pada Architecture Authority. Tabel ini tidak
menjadikan PD-01 sebagai Architecture Authority.

⸻

7. Dependency Boundary

Dependency ≠ Ownership
Collaboration ≠ Control
Governance ≠ Execution

⸻

8. Boundary Conflict

Jika terjadi conflict:

Boundary Issue
      ↓
Identify Domain
      ↓
Identify Owner
      ↓
Assess Authority
      ↓
Resolve
      ↓
Escalate if unresolved

⸻

9. Boundary Change

Perubahan boundary harus melalui:

Boundary Change Proposal
        ↓
Impact Assessment
        ↓
Affected Platform Review
        ↓
Authority Decision
        ↓
Boundary Record Update

⸻

10. Success Statement

A6 berhasil apabila seluruh domain memiliki ownership yang jelas, tidak
terdapat dual-primary ownership material, dan autonomy Platform tetap
dipertahankan.
