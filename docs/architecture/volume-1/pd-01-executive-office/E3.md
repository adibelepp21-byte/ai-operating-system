1. Purpose

E3 mendefinisikan bagaimana performance diukur secara:

* consistent;
* evidence-based;
* traceable;
* repeatable;
* auditable.

E3 menjawab:

“Bagaimana performance diukur?”

        ↓
E3
"How is it measured?"
        ↓
Measurement Result
        ↓

⸻

2. Measurement Philosophy

Measurement mengubah kondisi nyata menjadi observable, comparable, traceable evidence.

Reality
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Measurement Definition
   ↓
Measurement Method
   ↓
Measured Result
   ↓
Traceability

Measurement bukan sekadar collection of numbers.

⸻

3. Measurement Hierarchy

Performance Dimension
        ↓
Measurement Objective
        ↓
Metric
        ↓
Indicator
        ↓
KPI
        ↓
Measurement Result

Metric adalah measurable representation.

Indicator memberikan interpretive signal.

KPI adalah selected material indicator.

⸻

4. Metric vs Indicator vs KPI

Metric
  ↓
Observable measurable condition
Indicator
  ↓
Signal used to interpret a condition
KPI
  ↓
Formally selected material indicator

KPI merupakan subset dari metric environment.

⸻

5. Leading and Lagging Indicators

Leading Indicator

Memberikan signal tentang kondisi yang dapat mempengaruhi future performance.

Lagging Indicator

Menggambarkan result yang telah terjadi.

Keduanya harus dapat dibedakan.

⸻

6. Measurement Specification

Material measurement minimal memiliki:

* metric definition;
* formula/method;
* source;
* owner;
* frequency;
* validity;
* version;
* evidence traceability;
* limitations.

8. Measurement vs Evaluation

Measurement:

What happened?

Evaluation:

What does it mean?

Actual
   +
Expected
   ↓
Evaluation

E3 tidak menciptakan target secara sepihak.

⸻

9. Measurement Coverage

Organisasi harus dapat mengetahui:

What is measured?
        ↓
What is not measured?
        ↓
Why?
        ↓
Is the gap material?

Absence of measurement tidak otomatis berarti absence of performance.

Possible status:

* Not Measured;
* Measurement Gap;
* Insufficient Evidence;
* Measurement Pending.

⸻

10. Measurement Confidence

Possible classifications:

High Confidence
Medium Confidence
Low Confidence
Insufficient Evidence

Confidence mempertimbangkan:

* data quality;
* evidence completeness;
* source reliability;
* calculation stability;
* measurement limitations.

Confidence tidak mengubah measurement value.

⸻

11. Measurement Anti-Gaming Controls

Minimum controls:

1. metric definition dikunci;
2. formula terdokumentasi;
3. source dapat ditelusuri;
4. historical result tidak diubah tanpa controlled revision;
5. metric owner dan data owner dipisahkan apabila diperlukan;
6. material anomaly dapat ditinjau;
7. perubahan metric memiliki rationale;
8. KPI bukan satu-satunya representation of performance.

⸻

12. Measurement Governance

Governance
    ↓
Approves measurement framework / material definitions
    ↓
Performance Management
    ↓
Maintains measurement architecture
    ↓
Domain / Operational Owner
    ↓
Produces / provides evidence
    ↓
Measurement
    ↓
Measurement Result

Performance Management memiliki ownership atas measurement capability.

Domain owner tetap memiliki ownership atas domain dan evidence.

⸻

14. Success Criteria

E3 berhasil apabila:

1. material measurements memiliki reference;
2. metric memiliki definition reproducible;
3. KPI dapat dibedakan;
4. leading/lagging dapat dibedakan;
5. measurement memiliki source traceability;
6. quality dapat dinilai;
7. lifecycle lengkap;
8. changes controlled;
9. measurement tidak mengambil evaluation;
10. measurement tidak menciptakan authority;
11. measurement gap dapat dinyatakan tanpa fake score;
12. framework dapat diwariskan.
