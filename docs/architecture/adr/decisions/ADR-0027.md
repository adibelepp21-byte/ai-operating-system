## 1. Result

`ACT-CC-P6-054` established that a concrete consumer works outside the core.
`ACT-CC-P6-055` asked whether that is *authorized*. It is not — and the question
was **already recorded as Founder-reserved**, in a persisted repository artifact,
before this Act asked it.

## 2. §7.2 / §7.4 — the gate is not new — [E]

[E] `docs/program/AIOS_P6_039_E01_CONSTRUCTION_BLOCKER_v1.0.md` §5, verbatim:

> **"[D] Where does a concrete execution consumer live?** Three options, **none
> selectable by this office**"

| | Option | Recorded cost |
|---|---|---|
| i | Relax Agent-boundary dependency assertions | *"Changes assertions that `DEC-P6-029` issuance 3, `DEC-P6-032` and `DEC-P6-033` expressly protect"* |
| **ii** | **Authorize a consumer location outside the eleven boundaries** | *"**Structural addition touching Blueprint §31 and Freeze §4**"* |
| iii | Confirm E-01 is deliberately trivial | *"leaves failure, rejection and Trace semantics to a later consumer phase"* |

[A] **Option ii is the fourth-region question `ACT-CC-P6-054` arrived at
independently.** `ACT-CC-P6-055` §7.4 asked whether region allocation is
delegated, already authorized, Founder-reserved, or undefined. The persisted
record answers it: **Founder-reserved**, *"not selectable by this office"*, with
its cost already priced against Blueprint §31 and Freeze §4.

[E] **§7.2 — `ACT-CC-P6-039` revoked nothing.** It recorded *"Construction
performed NONE"*, *"Conformance suite UNMODIFIED"*, *"native_core production code
UNCHANGED"*, and returned a decision. [A] It **conditioned** E-01 construction on
a Founder selection among i/ii/iii; it did not cancel it. [E] **No persisted
record shows that selection was ever made.**

[A] So `ADR-0026`'s finding and `ACT-CC-P6-039` §5 converge on one question from
two directions — and `P6-039` got there first. My reading in `ADR-0026` that the
allocation gate was newly discovered was **incomplete**: it was already on the
record, priced, and reserved.

## 3. §7.1 — a material evidence gap — [E]

[E] `ACT-CC-P6-055` §7.1 requires establishing `DEC-P6-034`'s exact scope,
conditionality, expiry and supersession **from canonical source**. That cannot be
done: **`DEC-P6-034` has no persisted record in this repository.**

[E] Repository-wide, exactly two Founder Decisions of this series exist as
records — `AIOS_DEC_P6_032_…` and `AIOS_DEC_P6_033_…`. Checked individually:

```
DEC-P6-034 … DEC-P6-041   record file: ABSENT (all eight)
```

[E] The only two references to `DEC-P6-034` anywhere are
`AIOS_P6_039_E01_CONSTRUCTION_BLOCKER_v1.0.md` (citing it as its authorization)
and `ADR-0026` (mine, last Act). **Both are lower-order artifacts citing a
higher-order instrument whose body is not in the record.**

[A] `ACT-CC-P6-055` §10 is explicit: *"Lower-order artifacts SHALL NOT be used to
silently manufacture higher-order authority."* And the program's standing rule
bars reconstructing a canonical body from summaries, citations, or memory — **no
"best effort" reconstruction is permitted.** I therefore **cannot** certify that
`DEC-P6-034` is live, nor state its scope. This is recorded as a **RECOVERY
GAP**, not as a finding that no authorization was given.

[I] The Decisions were issued — this office has acted under them throughout the
program. [A] But conversational issuance is not canonical persistence, and the
distinction is exactly what §10 protects. A practice of persisting them existed
and lapsed after `DEC-P6-033`.

[A] **I have not closed the gap.** Writing those eight records would mean
reconstructing Founder instruments from my own recollection — the precise act the
program forbids. Closing it requires the Founder to supply the bodies, or to
authorize persistence of the issued texts.

## 6. Findings

1. **[E]** The fourth-region question is **already Founder-reserved** by
   `P6-039` §5 option ii — *"none selectable by this office"*.
2. **[E]** `P6-039` conditioned rather than revoked; the i/ii/iii selection was
   never recorded as made.
3. **[E] RECOVERY GAP:** `DEC-P6-034`–`DEC-P6-041` have no persisted records.
   Their scope cannot be verified, and this office must not reconstruct them.
4. **[A]** `ADR-0026` §5 is **corrected**: the gate was not newly discovered,
   only re-derived. Its technical findings stand unchanged.
5. **[R]** Option **iii** from `P6-039` §5 — *"E-01 is deliberately trivial"* —
   remains available and unpriced by any structural change. `P6-039` called it
   *"honest, and not a defeat"*, and `ADR-0026` reached the same place from the
   other side. It is the only one of the three that needs no new region and no
   assertion change.

## 7. The decision

> **FOUNDER DECISION REQUIRED**
>
> **Question:** Activate the E-01 consumer phase and authorize a fourth
> top-level product/application region as the canonical home for consumer
> implementations?
>
> **A — AUTHORIZE.** Consumer phase activated; fourth region allocated.
> `native_core/` and C1–C4 remain untouched. Cost, per `P6-039` §5 ii: a
> structural addition touching **Blueprint §31** and **Freeze §4**.
>
> **C — RESERVE.** Consumer phase stays reserved until a real consumer
> requirement exists. Costs nothing; the contract boundary is complete and
> correct as it stands.
>
> Separately, and independently of A/C: **the `DEC-P6-034`–`041` recovery gap
> (§3)** — supply the bodies, or authorize persistence of the issued texts.
> This office will not reconstruct them.

[R] **C**, on current evidence — nothing downstream consumes E-01 today, and a
structural addition touching Freeze §4 is a large price for an artifact with no
consumer. [R] If E-01 must be demonstrably realizable now, **iii** achieves that
without any structural change. [A] Neither is a selection; both are
recommendations, and `ACT-CC-P6-055` §13.7 is explicit that recommendation is not
authorization.
