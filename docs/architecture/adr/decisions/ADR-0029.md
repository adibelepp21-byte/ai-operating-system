`E-64` confirms the entity identity independently of `ADR-0010`: the Master

Execution→Runtime"* is the Domain Model's *"Organization owns Platform Division;
Platform Division owns Capability."* **Same chain, same position, two names.**

### What is not settled — `G-09`

Two canonical sources populate that one entity type differently:

| Source | Members | Count |
|---|---|---|
| `Master Program Volume VII §3` (Phase 10) | Executive Office · Engineering · Finance · Research · Marketing · Content | **6** |

**Overlap: one name — `Executive Office`.** Whether that is one unit or two

### Why this is load-bearing rather than cosmetic

`E-67`. `AIOS_ARCHITECTURE_FREEZE_v1.0.md §5` layer 4 (**Capability**) takes

Department-owned unit of ability"* owned by *"exactly one Department (INV-1)."*

**Until this is decided, `INV-1` cannot be evaluated for any Capability**,
because *"exactly one Department"* does not identify a member of a determinate
set. A frozen invariant that cannot be evaluated is not enforceable.

## The conflict this ADR records

Two Founder-issued instruments dated 2026-09-09 assert a position:

| Instrument | Text |
|---|---|
| `ACT-CC-AIOS-FULL-SYSTEM-RESOLUTION…-v1.0 §20` | `PD ≠ Phase` · **`PD ≠ Department`** · **`Department ≠ PD`** · `Phase ≠ PD` |
| `AIOS_CANONICAL_ARCHITECTURE` reconstituted candidate **v2.0 §17** | **`Department ≠ PD`**, plus *"A Department may consume capabilities from multiple Platform Divisions. A Platform Division may support multiple Departments."* |

**The candidate's §17 states a many-to-many relation.** A many-to-many relation
between Department and Platform Division is coherent **only if they are distinct
entity types**. `ADR-0010` holds they are **one entity with two names**.

**These cannot both stand.**

### Why the later instruments do not simply supersede the earlier ones

**By the authorizing Act's own authority stack (`§2`)**, `FOUNDER DECISIONS /
CANONICAL AUTHORITY` sits **above** `VALID CONSTRUCTION ACTS` and above `THIS
ACT`. The Act therefore ranks itself **below** `FD-6`.

`ACT §2` further states the Act does not *"revoke Founder authority"*, does not
*"silently ratify a disputed canonical source"*, and does not *"authorize
constitutional changes"*. `ACT §7(6)` reserves *"cancelling or overriding a
valid Founder Decision"* to the Founder, and `§7(10)` reserves *"unresolved
higher-order authority conflict."*

**And the constitutional route is specific:** the Canonical Domain Model is
amended *"only through an Architecture Decision Record approved under Section
3.4"* (`Constitution §5`). **An Act is not that mechanism.** This ADR is.

### What this ADR explicitly does not do

It does not adopt either reading. It does not rename `PD-01`. It does not add
Finance, Marketing, Content or Research as Platform Divisions, or drop any of
the ten. It does not assert that either list supersedes the other. It does not
treat the Act's `§20` as an error, and it does not treat it as an amendment.

## Options

### Option A — Department and Platform Division remain ONE entity; the Phase 10 list is historical

`ADR-0010` stands unchanged. `Master Program Volume VII §3`'s six-member list is
classified **historical** and superseded as a *population* by `PD-01…PD-10`.

- **Consequence:** `INV-1` becomes evaluable immediately against a determinate
  ten-member set. `Department ≠ PD` in the Act `§20` and candidate `§17` is then
  a **terminology slip** to be corrected at revision, and the many-to-many
  language in `§17` must be withdrawn.
- **Cost:** the Master Program's Phase 10 domain names (Finance, Marketing,
  Content, Research) lose their organizational-unit standing and would need a
  different home if they are still wanted.

### Option B — They are TWO distinct entity types with a many-to-many relation

The Act `§20` and candidate `§17` are adopted as the intended architecture.

- **Consequence:** this is a **Canonical Domain Model amendment** — a new entity
  type is introduced alongside Platform Division. `ADR-0010` is superseded in
  part. `Freeze §4`/`§5` must be re-examined, because *"Department-owned"* in
  `INV-1` would need to say which of the two types it means.
- **Cost:** amends a **frozen** baseline (`Freeze §2` freezes the twelve
  entities and *"No new entity"*), which `ACT §7(9)` reserves to the Founder.

### Option C — One entity; the Phase 10 list is a FUNCTIONAL grouping, not an organizational unit

The six Phase 10 names denote **business functions** that Departments serve, not
members of the organizational-unit entity.

- **Consequence:** `ADR-0010` stands; `INV-1` becomes evaluable against the ten;
  the Phase 10 names survive as a **function taxonomy** rather than as units.
  `Volume VII §1.2`'s definition — a Department is *"kumpulan workflow
  multi-agent yang terorganisir di sekitar satu **fungsi bisnis**"* — reads
  naturally under this option.
- **Cost:** requires stating where the function taxonomy lives, since it is not
  currently an entity anywhere.

---

## Recommendation

**Option C**, with **Option A** as the fallback.

**Why.** `Volume VII §1.2` already describes a Department as organized *around a
business function* — it defines the unit **by** its function rather than
equating the two. Option C is the only reading under which every instrument in
the conflict keeps a true statement: `ADR-0010`'s one-entity identity holds, the
six Phase 10 names keep a meaning, and the candidate's *"a Department may
consume capabilities from multiple Platform Divisions"* becomes true of
**functions drawing on divisions** without introducing a second entity type.

**This is a recommendation, not a decision** (`RECOMMENDED ≠ DECIDED`). Option B
is a live option and is the one the two Founder-issued instruments read most
naturally as intending; it is not recommended **only** because it amends a
frozen baseline, which is reserved.

---

## Exact decision required

> **Does the canonical organizational-unit entity enumerate the ten Platform
> Divisions, and if so what is the standing of the six Phase 10 names —
> historical (A), or a functional taxonomy that is not an entity (C)? Or is a
> second entity type intended (B), amending the frozen twelve?**

**One answer — A, B, or C — closes `G-09` and makes `INV-1` evaluable.**

## What continues in parallel regardless of the answer

The Cross-PD Interface Registry, the Phase–PD Capability & Dependency Map, the
candidate v2 anchoring map, and all verification work — **none depends on this
answer**, and all are proceeding.

## Addendum — 2026-09-10 · new evidence from `ACT-CC-P10-AUTHORIZATION`

**Status unchanged: Proposed.** This addendum records evidence that did not
exist when the ADR was drafted; it does not decide anything.

`ACT-CC-P10-AUTHORIZATION §5` establishes a **direction** between the two
surfaces:

```text
PD-01 … PD-10 → ORGANIZATIONAL SOURCE → P10 → ORGANIZATIONAL
OPERATIONALIZATION → DEPARTMENT ECOSYSTEM
```

**The Platform Divisions are the organizational *source* for the Department
Ecosystem.** That bears on this ADR: under **Option B** (two distinct entity
types with a many-to-many relation) it is unclear what "source" would mean, since
neither type would derive from the other. Under **Option A** and **Option C** the
direction reads naturally — one organizational definition surface, realized
operationally.

**Three reasons this is not a decision:**

1. `ACT §5`'s stated inequality is **`PD ≠ P10`** — Platform Division versus
   **Phase**, which was never in dispute. `ADR-0010`'s question is Platform
   Division versus **Department**.
2. It does not address the **six-name `Volume VII §3` list**, which is the
   population half of `G-09`.
3. `ACT §11` forbids inventing a decision, and `ACT §7` routes reserved
   questions to escalation rather than autonomous resolution.

**`NARROWED ≠ RESOLVED`.** Recorded as narrowing, and the question in
*Exact decision required* stands unchanged.

**Newly measured consequence** (`P10-DEPARTMENT-ECOSYSTEM-BASELINE.md`): the
Department mechanism is **implemented and conformance-verified** — 520 lines,
58 tests — and its **population is empty**, with `OwnershipGraph` never
constructed outside tests. **This ADR is the only thing standing between a
verified mechanism and an operational organization.**

## Addendum 2 — 2026-09-10 · `ACT-CC-P10-004 §5` frontier review

**Status unchanged: Proposed.** This addendum records evidence found by a
frontier search. **It decides nothing**, adopts no option, and does not convert
this ADR to Approved. `ACT-CC-P10-004 §5` forbids that conversion by inference
and it is not performed.

### Supersession search — complete, and negative

`ADR-0029` is the **highest-numbered ADR in the corpus**. There is no subsequent
ADR, so **no ADR supersedes it**. That is settled by enumeration, not assumed.

### A Founder Decision has supplied the population half

`FD-P10-003` — Founder Decision, **DECIDED** — states verbatim:

> *"Where `ADR-0029` does not itself establish sufficient authority for
> population, this Founder Decision supplies the required Founder-level authority
> to resolve the population boundary."*

and fixes the rule `CANONICAL DEPARTMENT = EXPLICITLY ESTABLISHED ORGANIZATIONAL
UNIT`, directing that *"P10 shall use an explicit Department population rather
than treating the entire PD-01–PD-10 Platform Organization as automatically
equivalent to the Department Ecosystem."*

**This resolves the population question for P10 and only for P10.** A Founder
Decision ranks above an ADR in the authority hierarchy, so the P10 Department
Ecosystem's membership no longer waits on this ADR. **`G-09`'s enumeration
question — what the canonical organizational-unit entity enumerates in
general — is untouched and remains open.**

### An ISSUED Architect Decision already holds the one-entity reading

`ADE-P10-G04`, **ISSUED** 2026-09-05 under `APT-CD1.1-AA-001`, states:

> *"`Department` and `Platform Division` are not two constructs. They are one
> entity under two names."*

quoting the Canonical Domain Model §2's own *"Historical alias: Department."*

**Consequence for the options.** `Option B` — two distinct entity types with a
many-to-many relation — is contradicted by **four** instruments: `FD-6`
(`GDR-0020`), `ADR-0010` (Approved), `ADE-P10-G04` (Issued), and the Domain
Model's own text. **It is not thereby eliminated** — the two Founder-issued
instruments of 2026-09-09 read most naturally as intending it, and only the
Founder can say whether `Department ≠ PD` there meant an entity-type distinction
or a population/scope statement. **That is the live question, and it is
narrower than the one this ADR opened.**

### Correction to this ADR's own *Consequences* section

The closing section states that leaving this open means *"`INV-1` stays
unevaluable; `P10` population stays undetermined."* **Both clauses were true
when written and are now false.** They were written while the Department
population was empty and the only candidate sets were the `Volume VII §3` six
and `PD-01…PD-10`. `FD-P10-003` has since supplied an explicit population, and
`INV-1` is **measured** against it: 3 Capabilities, 0 unowned, 0 record/nesting
disagreements, reproducible via `python3 tools/organization_catalog.py`.

**The original text is left standing above rather than edited**, because it is
the record of what was believed when the ADR was raised. This addendum is the
correction.

### What this ADR still blocks, and what it does not

**Still blocked:** adoption of candidate architecture v2 `§17` as written; any
general statement about what the organizational-unit entity enumerates outside
P10; closure of `G-09`.

**Not blocked:** **P10 completion.** The population is Founder-determined,
`INV-1` is evaluable and evaluated, and the two resident Departments are
established by Approved ADRs (`ADR-0003`, `ADR-0008`) that never reference
Platform Divisions at all. **Under every option A, B and C the two remain
validly established**, so no option's adoption would unmake them.

**Residual to disclose:** if the Founder later adopts `Option A` *and* intends
`PD-01…PD-10` to be the exhaustive population of the entity, then `Engineering`
and `Platform` — which are **not** among those ten — would need reconciling
against that list. `FD-P10-003`'s explicit-population authority governs P10 in
the meantime. **This is a future reconciliation, not a present block**, and it
is recorded here so that it is not discovered later as a surprise.
