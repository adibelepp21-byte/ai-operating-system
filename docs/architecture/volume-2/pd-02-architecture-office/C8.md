1. Purpose
C8 menetapkan Cross-Platform Architecture Governance sebagai constitutional mechanism untuk mengatur hubungan arsitektural antara PD-02 Architecture Office dan Platform Division lain dalam AIOS.
C8 menjawab:
Bagaimana architectural relationship antar Platform dikelola sehingga alignment, dependency, interface, shared responsibility, dan architectural decision dapat berjalan tanpa menghapus ownership masing-masing Platform?
C8 secara khusus mengatur:
* cross-platform architecture alignment;
* architectural dependency;
* architectural interface;
* shared architectural responsibility;
* cross-platform architecture decision;
* architectural consistency;
* architectural change impact;
* cross-platform architecture collaboration;
* cross-platform architecture conflict sebelum escalation;
* traceability hubungan arsitektural.
C8 tidak mengambil organizational ownership atas Platform lain.

67. Canonical Cross-Platform Architecture Statement
PD-02 Architecture Office governs cross-platform architectural relationships through alignment, dependency management, interface definition, shared responsibility, architectural review, and decision traceability. Cross-platform architectural governance may establish requirements, review material impact, and resolve architectural matters within applicable authority, but shall not transfer organizational ownership, operational responsibility, implementation authority, or resource authority from the Platform Division that owns the affected domain.
Versi Bahasa Indonesia:
PD-02 Architecture Office mengatur hubungan arsitektural lintas Platform melalui alignment, pengelolaan dependency, definisi interface, shared responsibility, architecture review, dan decision traceability. Cross-platform architectural governance dapat menetapkan requirements arsitektural, menilai dampak material, dan menyelesaikan architectural matter dalam authority yang berlaku, tetapi tidak boleh memindahkan organizational ownership, operational responsibility, implementation authority, atau resource authority dari Platform Division yang memiliki domain terdampak.
