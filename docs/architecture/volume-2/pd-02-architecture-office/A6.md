1. Purpose
A6 menetapkan organizational boundary PD-02 Architecture Office dalam AIOS Platform Organization.
A6 menjawab:
Domain apa yang menjadi ownership PD-02, domain apa yang berada di luar ownership PD-02, area mana yang membutuhkan shared responsibility, dan bagaimana boundary tersebut berinteraksi dengan Platform Division lain?
A6 bertujuan memastikan:
* single ownership per domain;
* explicit responsibility;
* clear organizational boundary;
* preservation of Platform autonomy;
* controlled cross-platform interaction;
* separation antara architecture authority dan operational ownership.
A6 tidak mendefinisikan:
* detailed authority matrix;
* internal hierarchy;
* staffing;
* team structure;
* operating workflow;
* performance model;
* implementation architecture.
Authority telah ditetapkan pada A5.

3. Fundamental Boundary Principles
Organizational Boundary PD-02 dibangun berdasarkan prinsip berikut.
3.1 Single Domain Ownership
Setiap organizational domain memiliki satu primary owner.
PD-02 memiliki primary ownership atas domain Architecture.
 
⸻
 
3.2 Architecture Boundary ≠ Domain Takeover
PD-02 dapat memiliki architectural concern terhadap Platform lain tanpa mengambil ownership domain tersebut.
Architectural Dependency
        ≠
Organizational Ownership
 
⸻
 
3.3 Platform Autonomy
Setiap Platform Division mempertahankan autonomy dalam domain yang menjadi ownership-nya selama tetap memenuhi applicable AIOS architecture, governance, security, quality, dan other applicable controls.
Baseline secara eksplisit mempertahankan autonomy Platform Division pada domain masing-masing.
 
⸻
 
3.4 Explicit Responsibility
Setiap material responsibility harus memiliki owner yang dapat diidentifikasi.
Tidak boleh terdapat:
* implicit ownership;
* dual primary ownership;
* ownership tanpa accountability;
* authority tanpa boundary.
 
⸻
 
3.5 Collaboration Does Not Transfer Ownership
Kolaborasi antar Platform tidak memindahkan ownership.
Collaboration
    ≠
Ownership Transfer
 
⸻
 
3.6 Architecture Influence Does Not Transfer Execution Ownership
PD-02 dapat mempengaruhi architectural direction dalam domain authority-nya.
Hal tersebut tidak menjadikan PD-02 sebagai implementation owner.
Architecture Authority
        ≠
Implementation Ownership
 
⸻

22. Architecture Boundary vs Organizational Boundary
Keduanya harus dibedakan.
Architecture Boundary
Menjawab:
Di mana architectural concern berlaku?
Organizational Boundary
Menjawab:
Siapa yang memiliki domain tersebut?
Contoh:
Architecture Boundary
        │
        ├── Runtime
        ├── Security
        ├── Infrastructure
        └── AI Engineering
Namun:
Organizational Ownership
        │
        ├── Runtime → Runtime Owner
        ├── Security → Security Owner
        ├── Infrastructure → Infrastructure Owner
        └── AI Engineering → AI Engineering Owner
PD-02 menjaga architectural relationship tanpa mengambil organizational ownership.

27. Escalation Boundary
PD-02 melakukan escalation ketika:
1. concern berada di luar Architecture domain;
2. terdapat conflict antar organizational owner;
3. terdapat cross-domain decision;
4. terdapat strategic consequence;
5. terdapat governance consequence;
6. terdapat unresolved ownership;
7. authority boundary tidak jelas.
Canonical escalation:
Boundary Issue
      ↓
Identify Domain
      ↓
Identify Owner
      ↓
Within PD-02?
   ┌──┴──┐
  YES    NO
   ↓      ↓
Handle   Escalate
Escalation digunakan untuk mengembalikan concern kepada authority/owner yang tepat.
 
⸻
 
28. Ownership Conflict
Jika dua Platform mengklaim ownership terhadap concern yang sama:
1. identifikasi domain;
2. identifikasi canonical responsibility;
3. periksa current boundary;
4. tentukan primary owner;
5. dokumentasikan conflict;
6. escalate jika tidak dapat diselesaikan dalam existing authority.
PD-02 tidak boleh mengklaim ownership hanya karena concern tersebut memiliki architectural dimension.
 
⸻
 
29. Boundary Ambiguity
Boundary dianggap ambiguous apabila:
* tidak jelas siapa primary owner;
* dua Platform memiliki overlapping primary ownership;
* responsibility tidak memiliki owner;
* authority dan ownership bertentangan;
* architecture concern disalahartikan sebagai operational ownership.
Ambiguity harus diselesaikan sebelum material decision dibuat apabila ambiguity tersebut mempengaruhi accountability.
 
⸻

41. Organizational Boundary Success Conditions
A6 dianggap berhasil apabila:
1. PD-02 memiliki satu primary organizational domain yang jelas;
2. Architecture domain memiliki single primary owner;
3. in-scope responsibilities terdokumentasi;
4. out-of-scope responsibilities terdokumentasi;
5. shared responsibilities memiliki owner masing-masing;
6. cross-platform architecture relationship jelas;
7. architecture authority tidak disamakan dengan operational ownership;
8. collaboration tidak memindahkan ownership;
9. delegation tidak memindahkan ownership;
10. architecture approval tidak memindahkan implementation ownership;
11. boundary conflicts memiliki escalation path;
12. boundary changes memiliki formal mechanism;
13. Platform autonomy tetap dipertahankan;
14. tidak terdapat material overlap dengan Platform Division lain.

42. Canonical Boundary Statement
PD-02 Architecture Office owns the Architecture domain within AIOS, including system architecture, architecture model, domain boundary, architecture consistency, architecture decisions, reference architecture, and canonical architectural artifacts. PD-02 may interact with, review, advise, and establish architectural requirements affecting other Platform Divisions, but such architectural interaction does not transfer organizational ownership, operational responsibility, or implementation authority from those Platform Divisions to PD-02.
Versi Bahasa Indonesia:
PD-02 Architecture Office memiliki ownership atas domain Architecture dalam AIOS, termasuk system architecture, architecture model, domain boundary, architecture consistency, architecture decisions, reference architecture, dan canonical architectural artifacts. PD-02 dapat berinteraksi, mereview, memberikan advisory, dan menetapkan architectural requirements yang berdampak pada Platform Division lain dalam authority yang sah, tetapi interaksi arsitektural tersebut tidak memindahkan organizational ownership, operational responsibility, atau implementation authority dari Platform Division tersebut kepada PD-02.
