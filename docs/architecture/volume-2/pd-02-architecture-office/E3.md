4. Core Measurement Principles
E3 PD-02 menggunakan prinsip berikut:
1. Purposeful — setiap formal measurement harus memiliki legitimate purpose.
2. Relevant — measurement harus relevan dengan condition yang ingin dipahami.
3. Defined — metric harus memiliki definisi eksplisit.
4. Traceable — result harus dapat ditelusuri ke evidence.
5. Repeatable — metode harus dapat digunakan kembali secara konsisten.
6. Reproducible — result harus dapat dihitung ulang apabila diperlukan.
7. Valid — measurement harus mengukur condition yang dimaksud.
8. Complete — evidence harus mencukupi untuk scope measurement.
9. Timely — measurement harus tersedia pada waktu yang relevan.
10. Controlled — perubahan terhadap measurement harus dikendalikan.
11. Contextual — result tidak boleh dipisahkan dari context material.
12. Non-manipulative — measurement tidak boleh direkayasa untuk menghasilkan desired appearance.

12. Metric vs KPI
Canonical rule:
Every KPI is a metric, but not every metric is a KPI.
Supporting metrics dapat digunakan untuk:

* diagnosis;
* analysis;
* validation.
KPI digunakan untuk:
* formal success monitoring;
* material performance visibility;
* structured reporting;
* review support.

13. Leading Indicator

* dependency risk signals;
* capability readiness indicators.

14. Lagging Indicator

* improvement effectiveness.

16. Leading Indicator Boundary
Leading indicator tidak boleh:

Leading indicator adalah signal.

17. Lagging Indicator Boundary
Lagging indicator tidak boleh:
* digunakan sebagai satu-satunya early-warning mechanism;
* digunakan untuk menjelaskan root cause tanpa diagnostic evidence;
* digunakan tanpa mempertimbangkan measurement latency.

28. Accuracy
Accuracy menilai apakah measurement merepresentasikan kondisi yang benar sesuai definisi.

⸻
 
29. Completeness

30. Consistency

⸻
 
31. Timeliness

Measurement yang benar tetapi terlalu terlambat dapat kehilangan decision utility.

41. Phase 3 — Approve

Approval harus memastikan:

* clear ownership;
* clear definition;

* no architecture conflict;

* frequency;
* source;

⸻
 
50. Phase 12 — Retire / Replace
Metric dapat retired apabila:

* metric tidak lagi material;
* source tidak tersedia;
* superior metric menggantikan;
* metric misleading;
* cost tidak justified;
* architecture berubah secara sah.
Retirement harus didokumentasikan.
Historical result tidak boleh dihapus hanya karena metric retired.

3. memiliki version;

6. melalui applicable approval.

53. Measurement Anti-Gaming
Minimum controls:
1. metric definition dikunci;
2. formula terdokumentasi;
3. source dapat ditelusuri;
4. historical result tidak diubah tanpa controlled revision;
5. metric owner dan evidence owner dipisahkan apabila diperlukan;
6. material anomaly dapat direview;
7. metric change memiliki rationale;
8. KPI tidak menjadi satu-satunya representation of performance.

* selective data reporting;
* unusual denominator changes;
* unexplained source changes;
* metric discontinuity;
* abnormal result improvement;
* suppression of negative evidence.
Signal bukan otomatis proof of misconduct.
Harus dilakukan validation.

101. Canonical Measurement Rule
Every formal PD-02 performance measurement shall have a defined purpose, metric definition, applicable performance dimension, evidence source, ownership, measurement method, validation discipline, and traceability sufficient to reproduce or substantiate the reported result.
 
⸻
 
102. Canonical Measurement–Evaluation Rule
E3 shall produce an evidence-based measurement result; interpretation of whether that result represents acceptable, deficient, improving, deteriorating, or otherwise material performance shall remain within the applicable evaluation and review architecture.
 
⸻
 
103. Canonical Measurement Gap Rule
When a condition cannot be measured reliably, PD-02 shall declare the applicable measurement limitation rather than manufacture a performance result.
 
⸻
 
104. Canonical Measurement Change Rule
Material changes to metric definition, formula, source, unit, frequency, classification, or measurement logic shall be controlled, versioned, justified, and traceable without silently rewriting historical performance.
 
⸻
 
105. Canonical Evidence Rule
Every material measurement result shall be traceable to the evidence and method from which it was produced, with evidence ownership remaining with the responsible domain owner.
 
⸻
 
106. Canonical Domain Boundary Rule
Performance measurement ownership does not transfer ownership of the domain being measured.
