# 1. Purpose

D10 menetapkan **Operating Success Constitution** bagi PD-02 Architecture Office.

D10 menjawab pertanyaan:

> **Kapan Operating Model PD-02 dianggap berhasil?**

D10 menetapkan standar konstitusional untuk menilai apakah operating model PD-02:

- menjalankan mandate secara efektif;
- menghasilkan operating outcome yang diharapkan;
- bekerja secara efisien;
- mempertahankan operating integrity;
- mampu beradaptasi;
- mampu berkembang melalui continuous improvement;
- mencapai tingkat kematangan operasional yang sesuai.

D10 menjadi **terminal success layer** bagi Part D.

3. Operating Success Constitution
Operating Model PD-02 dianggap berhasil apabila operating system mampu:
1. menjalankan Architecture Office mandate;
2. menerjemahkan mandate menjadi architecture work;
3. mengelola work secara konsisten;
4. menghasilkan decision dan artifact yang traceable;
5. menjaga architecture alignment;
6. mempertahankan governance boundary;
7. menjaga organizational ownership;
8. menjaga Platform autonomy;
9. mempertahankan operational control;
10. menggunakan evidence untuk improvement;
11. menghasilkan outcome yang berguna;
12. beradaptasi terhadap perubahan yang relevan.
Canonical principle:
Operating success is demonstrated through effective, efficient, controlled, traceable, adaptable, and continuously improving execution of the Platform’s operating mandate.
 
⸻
 
4. Operating Success Purpose
OSP-01 — Validate Operating Effectiveness
Menentukan apakah operating model benar-benar menjalankan intended operating purpose.
 
⸻
 
OSP-02 — Validate Operating Efficiency
Menentukan apakah operating model menggunakan effort, coordination, information, dan time secara proporsional terhadap outcome yang dihasilkan.
 
⸻
 
OSP-03 — Validate Operating Integrity
Memastikan keberhasilan tidak dicapai dengan:
* bypass governance;
* authority misuse;
* ownership transfer;
* loss of traceability;
* uncontrolled change.
 
⸻
 
OSP-04 — Validate Operating Adaptability
Menilai apakah operating model mampu menyesuaikan diri terhadap perubahan context tanpa kehilangan constitutional integrity.
 
⸻
 
OSP-05 — Validate Operating Maturity
Menentukan tingkat kematangan operating capability PD-02 berdasarkan evidence.
 
⸻
 
OSP-06 — Enable Operating Improvement
Menggunakan success assessment sebagai input untuk improvement cycle D9.
 
⸻
 
5. Operating Success Principles
OSP-PR-01 — Outcome Over Activity
Operating success tidak ditentukan oleh banyaknya aktivitas.
Yang dinilai adalah outcome yang dihasilkan oleh operating mechanism.
 
⸻
 
OSP-PR-02 — Evidence Before Success Claim
Tidak boleh menyatakan operating model berhasil hanya berdasarkan assertion.
Material success claim harus memiliki evidence yang relevan.
 
⸻
 
OSP-PR-03 — Success Within Boundary
Operating model tidak dianggap successful apabila outcome diperoleh dengan melanggar:
* governance boundary;
* authority boundary;
* organizational boundary;
* Platform ownership;
* required control.
 
⸻
 
OSP-PR-04 — Proportionality
Success assessment harus proporsional terhadap:
* materiality;
* scope;
* impact;
* dependency;
* operational risk.
 
⸻
 
OSP-PR-05 — Continuous Validation
Operating success bukan status permanen.
Success harus dapat divalidasi kembali ketika context berubah.
 
⸻
 
OSP-PR-06 — Learning-Oriented Assessment
Assessment tidak hanya mencari failure.
Assessment juga mencari:
* successful practice;
* improvement opportunity;
* recurring pattern;
* maturity signal;
* adaptation requirement.
 
⸻
 
OSP-PR-07 — No Authority Creation
Success assessment tidak menciptakan authority baru.
 
⸻
 
OSP-PR-08 — No Ownership Transfer
Success assessment tidak mengubah organizational atau Platform ownership.
 
⸻
 
OSP-PR-09 — Improvement Feedback
Assessment result harus dapat menjadi input D9 apabila improvement diperlukan.
 
⸻

7. Operating Effectiveness
Operating effectiveness menjawab:
Apakah operating model menghasilkan intended operating outcome?
Assessment mencakup:
* mandate execution;
* work completion;
* decision enablement;
* architecture alignment;
* artifact production;
* review effectiveness;
* coordination outcome;
* intended operating result.
 
⸻
 
8. Operating Effectiveness Indicators
Indikator dapat mencakup:
* percentage of material work reaching intended outcome;
* completion of required operating activities;
* successful architecture decision preparation;
* successful review preparation;
* architecture artifact completeness;
* alignment outcome;
* reduction of unresolved operational blockage;
* stakeholder usability of operating output.
Indikator aktual harus disesuaikan dengan domain PD-02.
 
⸻
 
9. Operating Efficiency
Operating efficiency menjawab:
Apakah outcome dapat dicapai dengan penggunaan effort dan coordination yang proporsional?
Assessment mencakup:
* unnecessary work;
* duplicated activity;
* coordination friction;
* avoidable delay;
* rework;
* information retrieval friction;
* review overhead;
* workflow complexity.
Efficiency tidak boleh dicapai dengan menghapus required governance atau control.
 
⸻
 
10. Operating Efficiency Indicators
Indikator dapat mencakup:
* cycle time;
* rework rate;
* unnecessary handoff;
* coordination overhead;
* work aging;
* decision preparation lead time;
* artifact reusability;
* effort-to-outcome ratio.
Metric definition tetap mengikuti applicable Performance Architecture.
 
⸻
 
11. Operating Integrity
Operating integrity menjawab:
Apakah operasi mencapai outcome tanpa merusak constitutional boundary?
Operating integrity dipertahankan apabila:
* authority tetap valid;
* ownership tetap jelas;
* work tetap traceable;
* decision tetap recorded;
* artifact tetap canonical;
* change tetap controlled;
* governance interface tidak dibypass.
 
⸻
 
12. Operating Reliability
Operating reliability menjawab:
Apakah operating mechanism menghasilkan outcome secara konsisten?
Reliability ditunjukkan melalui:
* repeatability;
* predictable workflow;
* stable ownership;
* consistent artifact quality;
* consistent decision preparation;
* recurring control effectiveness.
 
⸻
 
13. Operating Adaptability
Operating adaptability menjawab:
Apakah operating model mampu menyesuaikan diri terhadap perubahan context secara controlled?
Adaptability mencakup kemampuan untuk:
* mengakomodasi architecture change;
* menyesuaikan workflow;
* merespons dependency change;
* menyesuaikan coordination pattern;
* menyerap lessons learned;
* mengintegrasikan validated innovation.
Adaptability tidak berarti unrestricted flexibility.
 
⸻
 
14. Operating Adaptability Indicators
Indikator dapat mencakup:
* response time terhadap material context change;
* successful adaptation rate;
* time to incorporate validated learning;
* ability to adjust workflow;
* reduction of recurring friction after adaptation;
* successful integration of validated improvement.
 
⸻
 
15. Operating Maturity
Operating maturity menunjukkan tingkat kemampuan operating system.
Canonical direction:
Ad Hoc
   ↓
Defined
   ↓
Managed
   ↓
Optimized
   ↓
Adaptive
D10 menggunakan maturity untuk menilai operating capability, bukan untuk memberikan ranking terhadap individu.
 
⸻
 
16. Maturity Level — Ad Hoc
Operating model berada pada level Ad Hoc apabila:
* operating activity masih bergantung pada individual practice;
* workflow belum konsisten;
* ownership dapat ambigu;
* evidence belum sistematis;
* improvement masih reactive.
 
⸻
 
17. Maturity Level — Defined
Operating model berada pada level Defined apabila:
* operating mechanism terdokumentasi;
* ownership lebih jelas;
* workflow telah didefinisikan;
* required artifacts diketahui;
* review mechanism tersedia;
* operating evidence mulai konsisten.
 
⸻
 
18. Maturity Level — Managed
Operating model berada pada level Managed apabila:
* operating mechanism diterapkan konsisten;
* work dapat dipantau;
* ownership jelas;
* decision traceability tersedia;
* control berjalan;
* operating outcome dapat dievaluasi;
* improvement memiliki lifecycle.
 
⸻
 
19. Maturity Level — Optimized
Operating model berada pada level Optimized apabila:
* operating performance dianalisis;
* recurring friction dapat diidentifikasi;
* improvement diprioritaskan berdasarkan evidence;
* workflow terus dioptimalkan;
* rework berkurang;
* coordination efficiency meningkat;
* learning digunakan secara sistematis.
 
⸻
 
20. Maturity Level — Adaptive
Operating model berada pada level Adaptive apabila:
* operating model mampu merespons context change;
* improvement menjadi continuous capability;
* learning terintegrasi dengan operating cycle;
* innovation dapat diintegrasikan secara controlled;
* operating mechanism dapat berkembang tanpa constitutional drift.
Tidak semua Platform harus mencapai Adaptive pada waktu yang sama.
Maturity level harus mencerminkan observed capability.
 
⸻
 
21. Operating Success Criteria
Operating Model PD-02 dinyatakan successful apabila:
OSC-01 — Mandate Execution
Architecture Office mampu menjalankan mandate melalui operating mechanism yang konsisten.
OSC-02 — Work Effectiveness
Material Architecture Work menghasilkan intended outcome.
OSC-03 — Decision Enablement
Operating model menyediakan evidence, analysis, review, dan preparation yang diperlukan untuk decision yang valid.
OSC-04 — Artifact Integrity
Material architectural artifact:
* memiliki owner;
* memiliki status;
* memiliki version;
* memiliki source;
* memiliki relationship;
* memiliki lifecycle state.
OSC-05 — Traceability
Material operating activity dapat ditelusuri.
OSC-06 — Coordination
Cross-team dan cross-platform operating interface dapat berjalan tanpa menghilangkan ownership.
OSC-07 — Control
Operating activity tetap berada dalam required control boundary.
OSC-08 — Improvement
Operating friction dapat menjadi input improvement cycle.
OSC-09 — Adaptability
Operating model dapat merespons perubahan material.
OSC-10 — Sustainable Operation
Operating success dapat dipertahankan tanpa ketergantungan pada uncontrolled individual intervention.

35. Operating Success Failure Conditions
Operating model dianggap materially degraded apabila terdapat pola seperti:
* recurring failure to execute mandate;
* persistent work ownership ambiguity;
* systematic loss of traceability;
* recurring decision preparation failure;
* repeated uncontrolled operating change;
* persistent coordination breakdown;
* repeated control failure;
* improvement cycle yang tidak menghasilkan learning;
* inability to adapt to material context change.
Satu isolated operational issue tidak otomatis berarti operating model gagal.

43. Maturity Bottleneck Principle
Overall maturity tidak boleh menyembunyikan material weakness pada critical dimension.
Jika:
Effectiveness = Optimized
Efficiency    = Optimized
Adaptability  = Defined
Integrity     = Managed
maka overall maturity tidak boleh secara otomatis dinyatakan Adaptive.
Assessment harus mengungkap bottleneck yang relevan.
