3. Architecture Governance Definition
Dalam PD-02:
Architecture Governance adalah mekanisme terstruktur untuk memastikan bahwa architecture decisions, architectural artifacts, architectural boundaries, reviews, standards, dan architectural changes dijalankan secara sah, konsisten, terdokumentasi, dan tetap berada dalam Architecture domain.
Architecture Governance memastikan bahwa Architecture Office tidak hanya menghasilkan architecture, tetapi juga menjaga:
Integrity
   ↓
Authority
   ↓
Decision
   ↓
Accountability
   ↓
Review
   ↓
Traceability
   ↓
Controlled Evolution

5. Relationship to Governance Authority
PD-02 tidak memiliki Governance Authority secara umum.
Canonical relationship:
Architecture Authority
        │
        │ architectural concern
        ▼
Architecture Governance
        │
        │ governance interface
        ▼
Applicable Governance Authority
Jika suatu concern menyentuh governance policy, control, compliance, atau accountability enterprise, concern tersebut harus menggunakan applicable Governance Authority.
PD-02 dapat memberikan architectural input terhadap concern tersebut.
PD-02 tidak menjadi owner governance policy hanya karena policy tersebut memiliki architectural implication.
 
⸻
 
6. Governance Mission
Architecture Governance dibangun untuk memastikan bahwa:
1. architecture decisions memiliki authority basis;
2. architectural artifacts memiliki ownership yang jelas;
3. architectural review dilakukan secara konsisten;
4. architectural changes dikendalikan;
5. domain boundary tetap jelas;
6. architectural evidence dapat ditelusuri;
7. architecture deviation dapat dikendalikan;
8. cross-platform architectural relationship tetap coherent;
9. architecture authority tidak berubah menjadi operational authority;
10. architecture dapat berevolusi secara terkendali.

8. Governance Principles
AGP-01 — Authority Before Governance Action
Setiap governance action harus memiliki authority basis.
Authority
    ↓
Governance Action
Tidak diperbolehkan governance mechanism menghasilkan authority baru secara implisit.
 
⸻
 
AGP-02 — Architecture Governance Stays in Architecture
Governance mechanism PD-02 harus tetap berada dalam Architecture domain.
Tidak boleh digunakan untuk mengambil alih:
* enterprise strategy;
* governance policy;
* runtime operation;
* AI engineering execution;
* infrastructure;
* security;
* quality;
* domain ownership Platform lain.
 
⸻
 
AGP-03 — Governance Does Not Transfer Ownership
Governance participation tidak mengubah ownership.
Governance Participation
        ≠
Domain Ownership
 
⸻
 
AGP-04 — Decision Must Be Traceable
Setiap material architecture decision harus memiliki:
* context;
* authority basis;
* decision owner;
* decision;
* rationale;
* consequence;
* record;
* review state.
 
⸻
 
AGP-05 — Evidence Before Assessment
Material architecture assessment harus berbasis evidence yang relevan.
Preference tidak boleh diperlakukan sebagai architectural evidence.
 
⸻
 
AGP-06 — Separation of Concerns
Architecture governance harus mempertahankan pemisahan antara:
Decision
Approval
Review
Accountability
Execution
 
⸻
 
AGP-07 — Canonicality
Canonical architecture artifacts harus memiliki satu authoritative state.
Tidak diperbolehkan terdapat competing canonical states tanpa controlled transition.
 
⸻
 
AGP-08 — Controlled Change
Material architectural change harus mengikuti approved change mechanism.
 
⸻
 
AGP-09 — Explicit Escalation
Jika concern berada di luar Architecture Authority, concern harus dieskalasikan kepada applicable authority.
PD-02 tidak boleh mengisi authority gap dengan asumsi.
 
⸻
 
AGP-10 — Domain Autonomy Preservation
Architecture Governance menjaga coherence lintas Platform tanpa menghapus autonomy domain masing-masing Platform.

41. Governance Escalation Principle
Escalation wajib dilakukan apabila:
* authority tidak cukup;
* domain ownership conflict;
* governance policy diperlukan;
* strategic implication muncul;
* unresolved material risk;
* cross-domain decision diperlukan;
* authority precedence tidak dapat ditentukan;
* architecture concern melampaui PD-02 mandate.
Escalation tidak berarti transfer ownership.

* implementation preference;
* local optimization;
* individual interest;
* undocumented pressure;
