# 1. Purpose

E7 menetapkan bagaimana informasi performance PD-02 Architecture Office:

- dikemas;
- dikontekstualisasikan;
- dikomunikasikan;
- didistribusikan;
- ditelusuri;
- digunakan untuk mendukung review dan decision-making.

E7 menjawab:

> **"Bagaimana informasi performance PD-02 dikomunikasikan kepada consumer yang berwenang?"**

E7 mencakup:

1. Reporting Hierarchy;
2. Executive Reporting;
3. Platform Reporting;
4. Transparency Model;

E7 merupakan communication layer setelah measurement dan performance review.

E7 tidak menggantikan:

- measurement;
- evaluation;
- review;
- governance;
- Architecture Authority;
- decision authority;
- operational execution.

3. Core Reporting Principle
Performance reporting translates validated performance evidence and approved evaluation into the appropriate information for the appropriate consumer, at the appropriate level of aggregation and context, without altering the underlying evidence or decision authority.
Reporting PD-02 harus:
* accurate;
* traceable;
* contextual;
* proportionate;
* audience-appropriate;
* timely;
* transparent within legitimate access boundaries;
* decision-useful.
Reporting tidak boleh menjadi:
* data dump;
* KPI display tanpa context;
* dashboard decoration;
* executive summary tanpa evidence;
* score publication tanpa evaluation basis;
* mekanisme untuk menyembunyikan material exception.
 
⸻
 
4. PD-02 Reporting Boundary
PD-02 memiliki responsibility untuk menyediakan dan menjaga kualitas informasi performance yang berasal dari Architecture domain.
PD-02 menyediakan:
* Architecture performance evidence;
* Architecture performance result;
* Architecture performance context;
* Architecture-related finding;
* Architecture dependency;
* Architecture limitation;
* Architecture improvement status;
* Architecture decision relevance.
Namun:
PD-02 tidak memiliki primary organizational ownership atas Performance Management capability.
Performance Management tetap memiliki ownership terhadap performance reporting capability secara umum.
Dengan demikian:
Performance Management
        │
        └── owns performance reporting capability
sementara:
PD-02
        │
        └── owns Architecture-domain evidence,
            result, context, and response
Boundary ini mengikuti E6 PD-02 dan organizational boundary PD-02. Performance management sendiri berada di luar primary organizational ownership PD-02.

20. Transparency Model

Informasi yang hanya dapat diberikan kepada authorized consumers sesuai applicable security/governance controls.

59. Architecture Reporting Integrity Principle
Architecture performance reporting shall preserve the distinction between performance information and Architecture authority.
 
⸻
 
60. Transparency Principle
Transparency means sufficient visibility for legitimate consumers to understand performance and its implications; it does not mean unrestricted disclosure of all information.
 
⸻
 
61. Decision-Support Principle
Performance reporting shall support informed decisions without becoming the decision itself.
 
⸻
 
62. Authority Preservation Principle
No reporting level, report author, dashboard, aggregation layer, or consumer acquires decision authority merely by receiving or presenting performance information.
 
⸻
 
63. Cross-Platform Principle
Cross-platform reporting may expose dependency and shared impact, but it shall not transfer domain ownership between Platforms.
 
⸻
 
64. Architecture Escalation Principle
When performance reporting identifies an architectural concern, the matter shall enter the applicable Architecture Governance mechanism rather than being resolved through reporting itself.
 
⸻

1. setiap material Architecture performance information memiliki defined consumer;
2. reporting hierarchy dapat membedakan evidence, Architecture domain, Platform, dan Executive context;
3. executive reporting memprioritaskan materiality dan decision relevance;
4. PD-02 Platform Reporting mempertahankan lima performance dimensions;
5. Architecture reporting dapat ditelusuri kembali ke measurement/evidence;
6. actual, target, variance, trend, evaluation, finding, recommendation, dan decision dapat dibedakan;
7. material Architecture exceptions tidak tersembunyi oleh aggregation;
8. transparency tersedia dalam legitimate access boundary;
9. restricted Architecture information tetap memiliki traceability;
10. recommendation dapat dibedakan dari decision;
11. applicable decision authority dapat ditunjukkan;
12. reporting tidak menciptakan Architecture, Strategy, Governance, atau Operational authority baru;
13. PD-02 tetap mempertahankan Architecture ownership;
14. Performance Management tetap mempertahankan reporting capability ownership;
15. report memiliki version dan historical integrity;
16. material corrections memiliki audit trail;
17. confidence dan limitation dapat dikomunikasikan;
18. event-based reporting tersedia untuk material Architecture conditions;
19. dashboard dan report memiliki sufficient context;
20. decision-support package dapat digunakan oleh authorized decision-maker;
21. reporting dapat memicu Architecture Review tanpa menggantikan Architecture Review;
22. cross-platform reporting mempertahankan domain ownership masing-masing Platform;
23. reporting dapat mendukung E8 improvement;
24. reporting dapat menyediakan evidence bagi E9 maturity assessment;
25. reporting dapat menyediakan evidence bagi E10 success assessment;
26. framework dapat diwariskan ke Platform lain melalui domain adaptation tanpa memaksakan Architecture-specific format.
