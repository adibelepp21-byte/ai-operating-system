# 1. Purpose

E2 mendefinisikan bagaimana performance sebuah Platform dipahami, diklasifikasikan, diinterpretasikan, dan dinilai dalam AIOS.

E2 menjawab:

> **Bagaimana performa sebuah Platform dipahami?**

E2 menerjemahkan constitutional principles dari E1 menjadi model performance multidimensional untuk PD-02.

Performance PD-02 tidak boleh direduksi menjadi:

- satu KPI;
- satu metric;
- satu score;
- satu hasil operational;
- satu review;
- satu dimension.

Performance harus dipahami sebagai kondisi multidimensional yang memerlukan interpretation terhadap context, evidence, trend, materiality, dan relationship antar-dimension.

3. Performance Model Principle
Prinsip utama E2:
Platform performance is multidimensional; no single performance dimension is sufficient to represent the overall health or effectiveness of a Platform.
Sebuah Platform dapat:
* mencapai strategic objective tetapi memiliki governance weakness;
* memiliki operational performance yang baik tetapi capability yang belum memadai;
* memiliki governance yang kuat tetapi operational outcome yang lemah;
* memiliki capability tinggi tetapi belum menghasilkan outcome yang memadai;
* memiliki current performance yang baik tetapi improvement effectiveness yang rendah.
Karena itu, performance harus dilihat melalui beberapa dimension secara terintegrasi.
 
⸻

5. Performance Dimensions
PD-02 menggunakan lima performance dimensions:
1. Strategic Performance;
2. Operational Performance;
3. Governance Performance;
4. Capability Performance;
5. Improvement Performance.
Kelima dimension berlaku sebagai canonical interpretation model.
Domain-specific metrics, KPI, target, threshold, frequency, dan evidence source tidak ditetapkan oleh E2.
 
⸻
 
6. Dimension 1 — Strategic Performance
6.1 Definition
Strategic Performance mengukur dan mengevaluasi sejauh mana Platform memberikan kontribusi terhadap strategic objectives dan intended strategic outcomes yang secara sah berlaku bagi Platform tersebut.
Untuk PD-02, Strategic Performance terutama melihat kontribusi Architecture Office terhadap strategic architecture outcomes dan strategic objectives yang telah ditetapkan oleh authority yang relevan.
 
⸻
 
6.2 Strategic Performance Question
Pertanyaan utama:
Apakah PD-02 memberikan intended strategic contribution melalui architectural responsibility yang dimandatkan kepadanya?
 
⸻
 
6.3 Strategic Performance Scope
Dapat mencakup:
* strategic objective contribution;
* strategic alignment;
* strategic outcome;
* strategic architecture enablement;
* strategic value realization;
* strategic risk contribution;
* long-term architecture alignment.
 
⸻
 
6.4 Strategic Performance Boundary
Strategic Performance tidak:
* menetapkan strategy;
* mengubah strategic objective;
* mengambil alih strategic authority;
* menetapkan strategic direction;
* menggantikan Strategic Review.
Performance hanya menilai kondisi terhadap strategic reference condition yang telah ditetapkan.
 
⸻
 
7. Dimension 2 — Operational Performance
7.1 Definition
Operational Performance mengukur dan mengevaluasi seberapa efektif dan reliable operating mechanisms Platform menghasilkan intended operational outcomes.
Untuk PD-02, fokusnya mencakup operation dari Architecture Office sebagaimana ditetapkan dalam Part D.
 
⸻
 
7.2 Operational Performance Question
Apakah operating model PD-02 menghasilkan intended operational outcomes secara effective, reliable, dan sesuai boundary?
 
⸻
 
7.3 Operational Performance Scope
Dapat mencakup:
* operational effectiveness;
* workflow effectiveness;
* timeliness;
* quality;
* reliability;
* service effectiveness;
* decision cycle;
* coordination effectiveness;
* operational control.
 
⸻
 
7.4 Operational Performance Boundary
Operational Performance tidak:
* mengambil alih operational execution;
* mengubah operating ownership;
* menjadi operational command authority;
* menggantikan Part D;
* menciptakan operational decision rights baru.
 
⸻
 
8. Dimension 3 — Governance Performance
8.1 Definition
Governance Performance mengukur dan mengevaluasi apakah governance mechanisms yang berlaku menghasilkan governance conditions yang intended.
Untuk PD-02, Governance Performance dapat melihat effectiveness dari architecture governance yang berada dalam scope PD-02.
 
⸻
 
8.2 Governance Performance Question
Apakah governance mechanisms menghasilkan authority clarity, decision integrity, accountability, compliance, dan control yang intended?
 
⸻
 
8.3 Governance Performance Scope
Dapat mencakup:
* governance compliance;
* decision integrity;
* authority clarity;
* accountability clarity;
* review effectiveness;
* delegation integrity;
* escalation effectiveness;
* governance control effectiveness.
 
⸻
 
8.4 Governance Performance Boundary
Governance Performance tidak:
* menciptakan governance authority;
* mengubah governance policy;
* mengubah authority model secara unilateral;
* menggantikan Governance Architecture;
* menjadi final decision authority hanya karena menghasilkan performance finding.
 
⸻
 
9. Dimension 4 — Capability Performance
9.1 Definition
Capability Performance mengukur dan mengevaluasi apakah capability yang diperlukan Platform tersedia dan mampu menghasilkan intended performance.
Untuk PD-02, capability performance harus selaras dengan Capability Architecture yang telah ditetapkan dalam Part B.
 
⸻
 
9.2 Capability Performance Question
Apakah capability PD-02 mampu menjalankan responsibility dan menghasilkan intended outcome pada tingkat yang diperlukan?
 
⸻
 
9.3 Capability Performance Scope
Dapat mencakup:
* capability effectiveness;
* capability readiness;
* capability sufficiency;
* capability utilization;
* capability sustainability;
* capability dependency;
* capability degradation;
* capability improvement.
 
⸻
 
9.4 Capability Performance Boundary
Capability Performance tidak:
* mengambil alih capability ownership;
* menetapkan organization structure;
* membuat team;
* mengubah role;
* melakukan personnel evaluation;
* melakukan individual compensation assessment.
Capability Performance mengevaluasi capability condition.
 
⸻
 
10. Dimension 5 — Improvement Performance
10.1 Definition
Improvement Performance mengukur apakah improvement effort menghasilkan perubahan yang intended dan sustainable.
Improvement Performance tidak mengukur keberhasilan hanya berdasarkan completion.
 
⸻
 
10.2 Improvement Performance Question
Apakah improvement yang dilakukan benar-benar memperbaiki performance condition yang menjadi target improvement?
 
⸻
 
10.3 Improvement Performance Scope
Dapat mencakup:
* gap reduction;
* improvement effectiveness;
* recurring issue reduction;
* corrective action effectiveness;
* optimization outcome;
* improvement sustainability;
* improvement learning;
* re-measurement result.
 
⸻
 
10.4 Improvement Performance Boundary
Prinsip utama:
Action Completed
       ≠
Improvement Effective
Improvement effectiveness harus dibuktikan melalui evidence dan re-measurement yang sesuai.
Improvement Performance tidak mengambil alih improvement decision authority.
 
⸻
 
11. Dimension Relationship
Kelima dimension saling berhubungan tetapi tidak identik.
Strategic
   ↓
Defines intended strategic contribution
   ↓
Operational
   ↓
Produces operational outcome
   ↓
Governance
   ↓
Maintains authorized decision and control conditions
   ↓
Capability
   ↓
Provides ability to sustain responsibility
   ↓
Improvement
   ↓
Changes performance condition
Relationship tersebut tidak berarti setiap dimension harus selalu memiliki causal dependency langsung.
 
⸻
 
12. Dimension Independence
Setiap dimension harus tetap dapat dianalisis secara independen.
Contoh:
Strategic Performance = Strong
Operational Performance = Strong
Governance Performance = Weak
Capability Performance = Moderate
Improvement Performance = Weak
Result tersebut tidak boleh otomatis diratakan menjadi:
“Strong overall.”
Material weakness pada Governance atau dimension critical lainnya harus tetap terlihat.
 
⸻
 
13. Dimension Interaction
Cross-dimension analysis digunakan untuk memahami relationship antar kondisi.
Contoh:
Capability Performance ↓
          ↓
Operational Performance ↓
          ↓
Strategic Contribution ↓
Atau:
Governance Performance ↑
          +
Capability Performance ↑
          ↓
Operational Reliability ↑
Relationship tersebut adalah analytical interpretation, bukan automatic causal proof.
Causal conclusion memerlukan evidence yang memadai.
 
⸻
 
14. Dimension Overlap Control
Overlap antar-dimension harus dikendalikan.
Satu condition dapat memiliki relevance terhadap lebih dari satu dimension, tetapi:
Primary classification harus jelas.
Contoh:
Architecture Decision Cycle Time
Primary:
Operational Performance

Secondary Context:
Capability Performance
Metric atau evidence yang sama tidak boleh dihitung berulang kali hanya untuk meningkatkan overall performance result.
 
⸻
 
15. Primary vs Secondary Dimension
Setiap material performance condition dapat memiliki:
* Primary Dimension;
* Secondary Dimension;
* Cross-Dimension Relevance.
Model:
Performance Condition
       ↓
Primary Dimension
       ↓
Secondary Dimension(s)
       ↓
Integrated Interpretation
Primary classification menentukan lokasi utama condition dalam performance model.
 
⸻
 
16. Performance Dimension Mapping
Setiap formal measurement harus dapat dipetakan ke:

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

   ↓

17. Performance Score
Performance Score adalah nilai hasil measurement atau evaluation terhadap metric, criterion, atau defined assessment basis tertentu.
Contoh:
Metric Result
      ↓
Target / Criterion
      ↓
Score / Result
Score hanya merepresentasikan kondisi tertentu.
Score bukan keseluruhan Platform performance.
 
⸻
 
18. Performance Health
Performance Health adalah interpretasi multidimensional terhadap kondisi Platform.
Model:
Metrics
   ↓
Dimension Results
   ↓
Cross-Dimension Analysis
   ↓
Context
   ↓
Materiality
   ↓
Trend
   ↓
Evidence Quality
   ↓
Performance Health
Performance Health tidak boleh ditentukan hanya dari satu score.
 
⸻
 
19. Performance Health vs Performance Score
Canonical distinction:
Score adalah hasil pengukuran atau assessment terhadap kondisi tertentu.
Health adalah interpretasi multidimensional terhadap kondisi Platform.
Karena itu:
High KPI Score
      ≠
Automatically Healthy Platform
Dan:
Low KPI Score
      ≠
Automatically Failed Platform
Context dan materiality tetap diperlukan.
 
⸻
 
20. Performance Trend
Performance harus dapat dipahami secara temporal.
Minimum model:
Previous Period
       ↓
Current Period
       ↓
Trend
       ↓
Expected Direction
       ↓
Evaluation
Trend dapat diklasifikasikan sebagai:
* Improving;
* Stable;
* Declining;
* Volatile;
* Insufficient Evidence.
 
⸻
 
21. Trend Interpretation
Trend tidak menggantikan current-state assessment.
Contoh:
Current Performance: High
Trend: Declining
dapat menunjukkan emerging risk.
Sebaliknya:
Current Performance: Low
Trend: Improving
dapat menunjukkan effective improvement trajectory.
Keduanya harus dipertahankan dalam interpretation.
 
⸻
 
22. Performance Volatility
Performance dapat berubah secara volatile.
Volatility harus dapat dibedakan dari:
* genuine improvement;
* genuine deterioration;
* measurement anomaly;
* temporary event;
* structural condition.
Performance volatility tidak boleh langsung ditafsirkan sebagai failure tanpa diagnostic evidence.
 
⸻
 
23. Performance Context
Performance result harus dibaca bersama context.
Context dapat mencakup:
* scope;
* workload;
* organizational condition;
* architecture change;
* governance change;
* operating change;
* dependency;
* external constraint;
* maturity;
* resource condition.
Context membantu mencegah false interpretation.
 
⸻
 
24. Performance Materiality
Performance condition diklasifikasikan sesuai materiality.
Indicative classes:
* Critical;
* Material;
* Significant;
* Routine.
Materiality memengaruhi:
* assessment depth;
* review priority;
* reporting priority;
* escalation;
* improvement priority.
 
⸻
 
25. Critical Performance Condition
Critical condition adalah performance condition yang dapat materially affect:
* strategic outcome;
* architectural integrity;
* governance integrity;
* operational viability;
* capability sustainability;
* critical improvement outcome.
Critical condition tidak boleh disembunyikan oleh aggregate score.
 
⸻
 
26. Overall Performance Assessment
Overall Performance Assessment mengintegrasikan hasil lima dimension.
Canonical model:
Strategic Result
       +
Operational Result
       +
Governance Result
       +
Capability Result
       +
Improvement Result
       +
Context
       +
Trend
       +
Materiality
       +
Evidence Quality
       ↓
Overall Performance Assessment
Overall assessment tidak boleh menjadi arithmetic average secara default.
 
⸻
 
27. Overall Performance Assessment Principle
Overall assessment harus menjawab:
Bagaimana kondisi performance Platform secara keseluruhan setelah seluruh material dimension, context, trend, dan evidence diperhitungkan?
Assessment harus mempertahankan material weakness.
 
⸻
 
28. Overall Assessment Integrity
Overall assessment tidak valid apabila:
* evidence material tidak tersedia;
* dimension critical tidak dinilai;
* material exception disembunyikan;
* context penting dihilangkan;
* measurement definition tidak valid;
* score digunakan untuk menutupi unresolved finding.
 
⸻
 
29. Performance Diagnostic Model
Ketika performance berada di bawah expected condition, evaluasi harus mencari kemungkinan sumber gap.
Minimum diagnostic categories:
Strategic / Objective Gap
        │
Operational Gap
        │
Governance Gap
        │
Capability Gap
        │
Improvement Gap
        │
Measurement Gap
        │
External Constraint
Kategori tersebut membantu menghindari premature attribution.
 
⸻
 
30. Strategic / Objective Gap
Performance gap dapat berasal dari:
* objective yang tidak realistic;
* objective yang tidak jelas;
* objective misaligned;
* strategic assumption yang berubah;
* strategic condition yang berubah.
Performance Management tidak boleh mengubah objective secara unilateral.
 
⸻
 
31. Operational Gap
Gap dapat berasal dari:
* workflow;
* execution;
* resource;
* process;
* dependency;
* operating design;
* operational control.
Operational ownership tetap berada pada owner yang sah.
 
⸻
 
32. Governance Gap
Gap dapat berasal dari:
* unclear authority;
* weak accountability;
* ineffective review;
* delegation problem;
* escalation failure;
* governance control weakness.
Governance conclusion harus diproses melalui applicable governance mechanism.
 
⸻
 
33. Capability Gap
Gap dapat berasal dari:
* insufficient capability;
* capability degradation;
* capability readiness;
* capability dependency;
* capability sustainability.
Capability gap tidak otomatis berarti individual failure.
 
⸻
 
34. Improvement Gap
Gap dapat terjadi apabila:
* improvement tidak mengatasi root cause;
* improvement hanya selesai secara administrative;
* improvement tidak sustainable;
* improvement menghasilkan unintended consequence;
* re-measurement tidak menunjukkan expected change.
 
⸻
 
35. Measurement Gap
Performance conclusion dapat salah apabila measurement system memiliki:
* incorrect definition;
* incomplete evidence;
* unreliable source;
* calculation issue;
* stale measurement;
* insufficient coverage.
Measurement failure harus dibedakan dari performance failure.
 
⸻
 
36. External Constraint
Performance condition dapat dipengaruhi oleh:
* external dependency;
* regulatory condition;
* technology condition;
* ecosystem condition;
* unexpected environmental change;
* cross-platform dependency.
External constraint tidak otomatis menghapus accountability, tetapi harus dipertimbangkan dalam interpretation.
 
⸻
 
37. Diagnostic Discipline
Diagnostic conclusion harus membedakan:
Observed Condition
       ↓
Evidence
       ↓
Possible Cause
       ↓
Validation
       ↓
Root Cause / Contributing Factor
Possible cause tidak boleh langsung dinyatakan sebagai root cause tanpa evidence.
 
⸻
 
38. Performance Attribution
Performance attribution harus bersifat evidence-based.
Tidak boleh langsung menyatakan:
“Performance turun karena execution.”
Sebelum mempertimbangkan:
* objective;
* measurement;
* governance;
* capability;
* operation;
* external condition.
 
⸻

⸻
 
43. Performance Insight
Integrated Performance Assessment harus dapat menghasilkan insight.
Insight dapat menjawab:
* apa yang berubah;
* mengapa perubahan penting;
* dimension mana yang terdampak;
* apakah condition material;
* apa kemungkinan root cause;
* apakah improvement efektif;
* apa yang perlu diperhatikan oleh decision-maker.
Insight bukan decision.
 
⸻
 
44. Performance Recommendation
Performance analysis dapat menghasilkan recommendation.
Recommendation:
* menjelaskan suggested response;
* memiliki evidence;
* memiliki rationale;
* dapat memiliki priority.
Recommendation bukan authorization.
Model:
Performance Insight
       ↓
Recommendation
       ↓
Authorized Decision
 
⸻
 
45. Performance and Decision Authority
Performance result tidak otomatis menghasilkan authority.
Jika performance finding memerlukan decision:
Performance Finding
       ↓
Identify Applicable Authority
       ↓
Decision
       ↓
Authorization
       ↓
Execution by Responsible Owner
E2 tidak menciptakan decision rights baru.
 
⸻
 
46. Performance and Strategic Authority
Strategic Performance tidak menciptakan strategic authority.
Performance Management dapat menunjukkan:
strategic objective tidak tercapai.
Tetapi perubahan strategic objective tetap mengikuti strategic authority.
 
⸻
 
47. Performance and Governance Authority
Governance Performance tidak menciptakan governance authority.
Performance Management dapat menunjukkan:
governance condition is ineffective.
Tetapi governance response tetap mengikuti governance authority.
 
⸻
 
48. Performance and Operational Ownership
Operational Performance tidak memindahkan operational ownership ke Performance Management.
Performance dapat mengidentifikasi:
operating outcome tidak tercapai.
Operational owner tetap bertanggung jawab atas applicable operational response.
 
⸻
 
49. Performance and Capability Ownership
Capability Performance tidak memindahkan capability ownership.
Performance dapat menunjukkan:
capability insufficient.
Capability owner tetap menjadi responsible authority untuk capability response sesuai governance yang berlaku.
 
⸻
 
50. Performance and Improvement
Improvement Performance menilai apakah improvement berhasil.
Relationship:
Performance Gap
      ↓
Improvement
      ↓
Implementation
      ↓
Re-measurement
      ↓
Improvement Performance
      ↓
Effectiveness Assessment
 
⸻
 
51. Improvement Effectiveness
Improvement dianggap effective hanya apabila evidence menunjukkan intended performance condition membaik sesuai criterion yang ditetapkan.
Model:
Baseline
   ↓
Improvement Action
   ↓
New Measurement
   ↓
Comparison
   ↓
Effectiveness Assessment
Completion tidak cukup.
 
⸻
 
52. Performance Coverage
E2 harus memungkinkan Platform mengetahui:
What is performing well?
What is performing poorly?
What is improving?
What is deteriorating?
What is unknown?
Absence of measurement tidak boleh otomatis diubah menjadi:
Good Performance
atau:
Poor Performance.
 
⸻
 
53. Unknown Performance State
Jika evidence tidak cukup:
Insufficient Evidence
atau:
Performance Not Determinable
dapat menjadi legitimate assessment state.
AIOS tidak boleh menciptakan false precision.
 
⸻
 
54. Performance Confidence
Performance assessment dapat memiliki confidence:
* High;
* Medium;
* Low;
* Insufficient Evidence.
Confidence harus mencerminkan:
* evidence quality;
* measurement quality;
* completeness;
* consistency;
* context sufficiency.
 
⸻
 
55. Evidence Traceability
Minimum traceability:
Platform
   ↓
Performance Dimension
   ↓
Objective / Criterion
   ↓
Metric
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Measurement
   ↓
Evaluation
   ↓
Finding
   ↓
Review / Action
Setiap material performance conclusion harus dapat ditelusuri.
 
⸻
 
56. Dimension Double-Counting Control
Satu evidence dapat relevan terhadap beberapa dimension.
Namun:
Relevance tidak berarti automatic duplication.
Jika evidence yang sama digunakan dalam lebih dari satu dimension:
* relationship harus dijelaskan;
* primary classification harus jelas;
* double-counting harus dikendalikan;
* material weakness tidak boleh dihilangkan.
 
⸻
 
57. Weighting
E2 tidak menetapkan universal weighting.
Weighting, apabila digunakan pada layer yang relevan, harus mempertimbangkan:
* Platform mandate;
* strategic importance;
* operational criticality;
* governance sensitivity;
* capability dependency;
* maturity.
Weighting tidak boleh digunakan untuk menyembunyikan critical failure.
 
⸻
 
58. No Universal Threshold
E2 tidak menetapkan threshold numerik universal.
Threshold, target, dan criterion ditentukan pada layer yang sesuai, terutama E4 dan applicable objective/governance framework.
E2 hanya menetapkan model interpretasi.
 
⸻

E2 menjawab:

E4 mengoperasionalkan:

   ↓

   ↓

78. Performance Model Anti-Patterns
Single-KPI Model
Menganggap satu KPI mewakili seluruh performance.
Average-Only Model
Menggunakan average untuk menyembunyikan material weakness.
Activity-as-Performance
Menganggap volume activity sebagai outcome.
Execution-Blame Model
Menganggap seluruh performance failure berasal dari execution.
Authority Leakage
Menggunakan performance result untuk mengambil alih authority.
Dimension Silo
Mengelola lima dimension sebagai lima sistem yang tidak berhubungan.
Double Counting
Menghitung evidence atau condition yang sama berkali-kali.
Health Without Context
Menentukan health tanpa context.
Improvement Completion Fallacy
Menganggap action completion sebagai improvement effectiveness.
Static Performance
Mengabaikan trend dan perubahan context.

81. Canonical Performance Interpretation Rule
A Platform’s performance shall be interpreted through the five performance dimensions as an integrated multidimensional system, using evidence, context, trend, materiality, and confidence, without allowing any single metric, dimension, score, or activity volume to represent the whole Platform by default.
 
⸻
 
82. Canonical Diagnostic Rule
When a performance gap is observed, the assessment shall distinguish among strategic/objective, operational, governance, capability, improvement, measurement, and external causes before attributing responsibility or recommending action.
 
⸻
 
83. Canonical Authority Rule
Performance assessment may identify conditions, findings, risks, and improvement signals, but performance status does not by itself create authority to change strategy, governance, architecture, operation, capability ownership, or domain ownership.
 
⸻
 
84. Canonical Health Rule
Performance Health shall be derived from integrated interpretation of dimension results, context, trend, materiality, and evidence quality rather than from a single KPI or arithmetic average alone.
 
⸻
 
85. Canonical Improvement Rule
Improvement Performance shall be assessed through demonstrated change in the relevant performance condition; completion of an improvement action is not sufficient evidence of improvement effectiveness.
