# 1. Purpose

D7 menetapkan **Collaboration Constitution** bagi PD-02 Architecture Office.

D7 menjawab:

> **Bagaimana PD-02 berkolaborasi dengan individu, Team, Sub Division, Executive Office, Platform Division lain, dan stakeholder arsitektural untuk menjalankan Architecture mandate secara efektif tanpa mengaburkan authority, accountability, atau ownership?**

D7 menjadi arsitektur utama kolaborasi operasional PD-02.

D7 memastikan bahwa collaboration:

- memiliki tujuan;
- memiliki participant yang jelas;
- memiliki interaction model;
- memiliki lifecycle;
- memiliki communication discipline;
- menghasilkan traceable outcome;
- menghormati governance;
- menghormati authority;
- menghormati ownership.

---

# 2. Collaboration Definition

Collaboration adalah:

> **structured interaction between relevant participants to develop shared understanding, coordinate contribution, resolve applicable concerns, and produce an agreed operational or architectural outcome while preserving each participant's authority, accountability, and ownership boundary.**

Collaboration bukan:

- authority;
- governance;
- ownership;
- delegation;
- accountability transfer;
- decision right.

⸻

⸻

* materiality;
* risk;
* dependency;
* impact.
 
⸻

⸻

⸻
 
9.12 Constructive Challenge
Participant boleh menantang assumption, design, interpretation, atau proposed approach berdasarkan evidence dan applicable architecture principle.

12. Collaboration Levels
PD-02 menggunakan collaboration levels:
Level 1 — Information
Level 2 — Consultation
Level 3 — Coordination
Level 4 — Joint Analysis
Level 5 — Collaborative Resolution
Level 6 — Formal Review / Decision Interface
Higher collaboration level digunakan apabila complexity atau materiality meningkat.
 
⸻
 
13. Level 1 — Information
Tujuan:
memastikan participant memiliki context yang diperlukan.
Contoh:
* architecture update;
* artifact notification;
* architecture baseline change notice.
Tidak membutuhkan collaborative decision.
 
⸻
 
14. Level 2 — Consultation
Tujuan:
memperoleh perspective atau domain information.
Contoh:
* meminta domain input;
* meminta architecture impact information;
* meminta implementation constraint.
Consultation tidak memindahkan ownership.
 
⸻
 
15. Level 3 — Coordination
