⸻
 
2. Constitutional Position
Performance Architecture merupakan bagian dari architecture system AIOS yang menyediakan mekanisme untuk:
* memahami performance;
* mengukur performance;
* mengevaluasi performance;
* menghasilkan performance insight;
* mengkomunikasikan performance information;
* mengidentifikasi improvement opportunity;
* memberikan feedback terhadap operating dan organizational system.
Performance Architecture tidak menjadi pengganti:
* Strategy;
* Governance;
* Architecture Authority;
* Operational Authority;
* Domain Ownership.
 
⸻
 
3. Performance Philosophy
3.1 Core Philosophy
Performance PD-02 dipahami sebagai:
Kemampuan Architecture Office untuk mengetahui, melalui evidence yang dapat dipercaya, apakah architectural responsibilities yang dimandatkan kepadanya dijalankan secara efektif, konsisten, dapat dipertanggungjawabkan, dan terus diperbaiki.
Performance bukan sekadar:
* jumlah architecture decision;
* jumlah review;
* jumlah artifact;
* jumlah issue yang ditutup;
* jumlah KPI yang tercapai.
Performance harus menjelaskan kondisi, outcome, effectiveness, quality, dan improvement dari architectural responsibility PD-02.
 
⸻
 
3.2 Evidence-Based Performance
Performance conclusion harus memiliki dasar evidence.
Model:
Architectural Condition
        ↓
Evidence
        ↓
Measurement
        ↓
Evaluation
        ↓
Performance Insight
        ↓
Feedback
Performance conclusion tidak boleh dibentuk hanya berdasarkan:
* assumption;
* perception;
* activity volume;
* isolated anecdote;
* unsupported score.
 
⸻
 
3.3 Performance as Learning
Performance bukan hanya mekanisme penilaian.
Performance juga merupakan mekanisme pembelajaran.
Measure
   ↓
Evaluate
   ↓
Understand
   ↓
Learn
   ↓
Improve
   ↓
Measure Again
Performance information harus dapat membantu PD-02 memahami:
* apa yang berjalan baik;
* apa yang tidak berjalan baik;
* mengapa kondisi tersebut terjadi;
* apa yang perlu diperbaiki;
* apakah improvement benar-benar efektif.
 
⸻
 
3.4 Performance as Feedback
Performance harus menghasilkan feedback terhadap system.
Feedback dapat diarahkan kepada:
* operating model;
* capability;
* governance mechanism;
* architecture practice;
* collaboration mechanism;
* improvement mechanism.
Feedback tidak secara otomatis menjadi decision.
Decision tetap mengikuti authority yang berlaku.
 
⸻
 
4. Performance Principles
4.1 Outcome Orientation
Performance harus berorientasi pada outcome, bukan sekadar activity.
 
⸻
 
4.2 Evidence First
Performance conclusion harus berlandaskan evidence yang dapat ditelusuri.
 
⸻
 
4.3 Multi-Dimensionality
Performance tidak boleh direduksi menjadi satu metric atau score.
Performance harus dapat dilihat dari beberapa dimensi yang relevan.
 
⸻
 
4.4 Context Integrity
Performance result harus selalu dibaca bersama context yang relevan.
Metric tanpa context tidak cukup untuk menghasilkan performance conclusion yang reliable.
 
⸻
 
4.5 Traceability
Setiap material performance conclusion harus dapat ditelusuri:
Conclusion
    ↓
Assessment
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Measurement
    ↓
Source
 
⸻
 
4.6 Separation of Concerns
Performance harus dipisahkan dari:
* governance;
* architecture authority;
* operational execution;
* domain ownership.
 
⸻
 
4.7 No False Precision
Apabila evidence tidak cukup, performance system tidak boleh menciptakan precision atau confidence palsu.
Possible state:
Insufficient Evidence
merupakan hasil yang valid apabila evidence belum memadai.
 
⸻
 
4.8 Materiality
Tidak semua performance condition memiliki tingkat materiality yang sama.
Assessment harus mempertimbangkan:
* impact;
* risk;
* scope;
* criticality;
* persistence;
* architectural consequence.
 
⸻
 
4.9 Comparability with Caution
Performance dapat dibandingkan antar-period atau antar-context apabila basis comparison compatible.
Performance tidak boleh dibandingkan secara mekanis ketika:
* scope berbeda;
* context berbeda;
* measurement definition berbeda;
* maturity berbeda;
* evidence quality berbeda.
 
⸻
 
4.10 Continuous Improvement
Performance system harus menyediakan feedback untuk improvement.
Improvement tidak dianggap berhasil hanya karena action selesai.
Effectiveness harus dapat diverifikasi melalui subsequent assessment atau re-measurement.
 
⸻

6. Performance and Architecture Authority
PD-02 memiliki Architecture Authority.
Namun:
Performance Architecture bukan sumber Architecture Authority.
Architecture Authority berasal dari applicable architecture governance dan organizational mandate.
Performance Architecture hanya:
* measures;
* evaluates;
* reports;
* provides insight;
* identifies improvement signal.
Relationship:
Architecture Authority
        │
        │ owns / authorizes architecture decisions
        ▼
Architecture Domain
        │
        ▼
Performance Architecture
        │
        ├── measures
        ├── evaluates
        ├── reports
        └── provides feedback
Performance result tidak dengan sendirinya mengubah architecture decision.
 
⸻
 
7. Performance and Governance
Performance Governance dan Governance Architecture memiliki concern berbeda.
Governance Architecture
        ↓
Who may act?
Who owns authority?
Who approves?
Who is accountable?

Performance Architecture
        ↓
How is performance understood?
How is it measured?
How is it evaluated?
What does the evidence indicate?
Performance Governance dapat mengatur integrity performance management.
Ia tidak menggantikan enterprise governance authority.

9. Performance and Strategy

Namun Performance Architecture tidak menetapkan enterprise strategy.
Strategy
   ↓
Strategic Objective
   ↓
Performance Reference
   ↓

Performance tidak memiliki authority unilateral untuk mengubah strategic objective.

10. Performance and Organization

Namun:
Performance finding tidak secara otomatis menjadi organizational restructuring decision.
Organizational authority tetap mengikuti organizational architecture dan governance model.

Prinsip:
Measurement relationship does not create ownership.

20. Performance Measurement Boundary
Measurement menjawab:
What is observed?
Evaluation menjawab:
What does the observation mean against an approved reference condition?
Insight menjawab:
What should decision-makers understand from the evaluated condition?
Feedback menjawab:
What system learning or improvement signal should be generated?
Decision authority tetap berada di luar performance measurement mechanism kecuali authority tersebut secara sah didelegasikan.
 
⸻
 
21. Performance Assessment
Performance assessment harus mempertimbangkan:
* expected condition;
* actual condition;
* variance;
* trend;
* materiality;
* context;
* evidence quality;
* confidence.
Performance assessment tidak boleh hanya membaca metric value tanpa context.
 
⸻
 
22. Performance Confidence
Performance conclusion dapat memiliki confidence level.
Contoh:
* High;
* Medium;
* Low;
* Insufficient Evidence.
Confidence harus mencerminkan evidence quality.
Confidence tidak boleh digunakan untuk menyamarkan uncertainty.
 
⸻
 
23. Performance Materiality
Performance condition dapat dikategorikan sebagai:
* Critical;
* Material;
* Significant;
* Routine.
Materiality memengaruhi:
* review depth;
* evidence requirement;
* escalation;
* reporting;
* improvement priority.
 
⸻
 
24. Performance Failure Interpretation
Performance failure tidak boleh langsung diasumsikan sebagai execution failure.
Potential causes dapat mencakup:
* objective issue;
* execution issue;
* measurement issue;
* governance issue;
* capability gap;
* operating design issue;
* dependency;
* external constraint.
Performance Architecture harus membantu membedakan kemungkinan tersebut.
 
⸻
 
25. Performance Integrity
Performance integrity berarti:
Performance conclusion tetap faithful terhadap evidence, context, measurement definition, authority boundary, dan applicable reference condition.
Performance integrity harus dijaga terhadap:
* metric manipulation;
* selective reporting;
* context omission;
* false aggregation;
* target gaming;
* premature conclusion.
 
⸻
 
26. Anti-Gaming Principle
Performance system tidak boleh memberikan incentive untuk:
* mengubah definition demi score;
* menyembunyikan material exception;
* memindahkan scope agar target tercapai;
* menutup action tanpa effectiveness;
* menghapus historical evidence;
* menggunakan aggregate score untuk menyembunyikan material weakness.
 
⸻
 
27. Performance Transparency
Performance information harus cukup transparan untuk mendukung legitimate review dan decision-making.
Namun:
Transparency does not mean unrestricted disclosure.
Access tetap mengikuti:
* authority;
* information classification;
* confidentiality;
* legitimate business need.
 
⸻

29. Performance Review Boundary

* mengubah ownership.

Performance

46. Performance Conclusion Rule
Performance conclusion hanya boleh dibuat apabila:
1. reference condition tersedia;
2. evidence sufficient;
3. measurement valid;
4. context memadai;
5. materiality dipahami;
6. applicable authority boundary jelas.
Jika salah satu condition material tidak terpenuhi:
Do not manufacture a performance conclusion.

56. Canonical Performance Principle
Performance exists to make organizational and architectural conditions observable, interpretable, reviewable, and improvable through reliable evidence, while preserving the authority, ownership, governance, architecture, and operating boundaries of the AIOS system.

57. Final Constitutional Statement
The PD-02 Performance Architecture establishes a disciplined, evidence-based system for understanding how effectively the Architecture Office fulfills its architectural responsibilities, how reliably its architectural capabilities and mechanisms operate, and where improvement is required. It provides measurement, evaluation, insight, reporting, and feedback without becoming a substitute for strategy, governance, architecture authority, operational execution, or domain ownership.
