# 1. Purpose

C10 menetapkan **Governance Success Constitution** bagi PD-02 Architecture Office.

C10 menjawab pertanyaan:

> **Kapan Architecture Governance PD-02 dianggap berhasil?**

C10 menetapkan:

- governance success dimensions;
- success criteria;
- governance assessment model;
- governance maturity direction;
- governance success review cycle;
- governance improvement feedback;
- governance success boundary.

C10 menjadi **success and closure layer** bagi Part C.

C10 tidak menggantikan C1–C9.

---

# 2. Governance Success Constitution

Architecture Governance PD-02 dianggap berhasil apabila governance:

- menjalankan architecture mandate secara konsisten;
- menggunakan authority yang tepat;
- menghasilkan architecture decision yang dapat dipertanggungjawabkan;
- menjaga accountability;
- menghasilkan review outcome yang dapat ditindaklanjuti;
- menyelesaikan atau mengeskalasikan unresolved matter secara tepat;
- menjaga hubungan lintas Platform tanpa mengambil alih ownership;
- mempertahankan governance compliance;
- mampu memperbaiki dirinya berdasarkan evidence dan learning.

# 3. Governance Success Position of PD-02

PD-02 memiliki responsibility untuk menilai keberhasilan **Architecture Governance dalam domain Architecture**.

PD-02 tidak menjadi owner seluruh enterprise governance success.

Canonical principle:

> **Governance success is assessed at the applicable governance domain, while domain success remains owned by the respective Platform.**

```text

6. Governance Success Dimensions
GSC-01 — Architecture Governance Effectiveness
Architecture Governance efektif apabila:
* governance mechanism C1–C10 dapat digunakan secara coherent;
* governance process berjalan sesuai design;
* architecture matter memiliki routing yang jelas;
* governance action dapat ditelusuri;
* governance outcome dapat dievaluasi.
 
⸻
 
GSC-02 — Architecture Decision Quality
Architecture Governance berhasil apabila:
* architecture decision memiliki owner;
* decision menggunakan authority yang tepat;
* decision memiliki sufficient evidence;
* reasoning terdokumentasi;
* decision dapat ditelusuri;
* decision konsisten dengan applicable architecture baseline.
Decision quality tidak berarti seluruh keputusan harus menghasilkan outcome identik.
Decision quality berarti keputusan dibuat melalui governance mechanism yang sah, evidence-based, dan traceable.
 
⸻
 
GSC-03 — Accountability Integrity
Governance berhasil apabila:
* responsibility jelas;
* accountability owner dapat diidentifikasi;
* delegated responsibility tetap traceable;
* outcome memiliki accountable owner;
* tidak terdapat material accountability gap.
Canonical principle:
Delegation
    ↓
Action
    ↓
Accountability
Delegation tidak menghapus accountability.
 
⸻
 
GSC-04 — Authority Integrity
Governance berhasil apabila:
* authority digunakan dalam domain yang tepat;
* authority tidak melampaui scope;
* delegation mengikuti applicable rule;
* approval dilakukan oleh authority yang sah;
* override memiliki basis yang terdokumentasi;
* architecture authority tidak digunakan untuk mengambil alih domain lain.
Canonical principle:
Authority
    ≠
Ownership
dan:
Architecture Authority
    ≠
Enterprise Governance Authority
 
⸻
 
GSC-05 — Review Effectiveness
Governance review berhasil apabila:
* matter dinilai terhadap kriteria yang relevan;
* evidence diperiksa;
* finding dapat dijelaskan;
* condition memiliki owner;
* approval/rejection memiliki basis;
* unresolved matter memiliki escalation path.
Review effectiveness tidak diukur dari jumlah review semata.
Review harus menghasilkan governance outcome yang usable.
 
⸻
 
GSC-06 — Escalation Effectiveness
Governance berhasil apabila unresolved matter:
* dapat diidentifikasi;
* memiliki escalation basis;
* diarahkan ke authority yang tepat;
* tidak menciptakan authority baru;
* mempertahankan historical traceability;
* dapat menghasilkan resolution atau further escalation.
Canonical flow:
Finding
   ↓
Attempted Resolution
   ↓
Unresolved
   ↓
Escalation Condition
   ↓
Applicable Authority
   ↓
Resolution
   ↓
Validation
   ↓
Closure
 
⸻
 
GSC-07 — Cross-Platform Governance Alignment
Architecture Governance berhasil apabila PD-02 dapat bekerja dengan Platform lain tanpa:
* kehilangan architecture integrity;
* menciptakan dual uncontrolled ownership;
* mengambil alih domain Platform lain;
* mengabaikan applicable authority;
* menghindari escalation mechanism.
Cross-platform success berarti:
Architectural alignment without ownership transfer.
 
⸻
 
GSC-08 — Governance Compliance
Architecture Governance berhasil apabila:
* applicable governance requirement diketahui;
* requirement dapat dipetakan;
* evidence tersedia;
* deviation dapat diidentifikasi;
* finding ditindaklanjuti;
* corrective action dapat divalidasi;
* closure dapat ditelusuri.
Architecture compliance tetap dibedakan dari governance policy ownership.
 
⸻
 
GSC-09 — Governance Traceability
Material governance activity harus dapat ditelusuri melalui:
Matter
   ↓
Owner
   ↓
Authority
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Review
   ↓
Decision / Finding
   ↓
Action
   ↓
Validation
   ↓
Closure
Tidak diperbolehkan terdapat material governance action yang tidak memiliki basis traceability yang memadai.
 
⸻
 
GSC-10 — Continuous Governance Evolution
Governance dianggap matang apabila mampu:
* menerima feedback;
* belajar dari finding;
* belajar dari escalation;
* mengidentifikasi recurring governance weakness;
* memperbaiki governance mechanism;
* mempertahankan architecture integrity selama perubahan.
Continuous improvement tidak berarti perubahan governance tanpa control.
Setiap material change tetap harus mengikuti applicable Architecture Change Control.
 
⸻

9. Governance Maturity Direction
PD-02 menggunakan arah maturity berikut:
Initial
   ↓
Defined
   ↓
Managed
   ↓
Optimized
   ↓
Adaptive
Maturity level menunjukkan kemampuan governance system, bukan status atau ranking individu.
 
⸻
 
10. Maturity Level 1 — Initial
Governance berada pada tahap Initial apabila:
* mechanism belum konsisten;
* ownership belum selalu jelas;
* decision traceability masih terbatas;
* review masih bersifat ad hoc;
* improvement belum sistematis.
 
⸻
 
11. Maturity Level 2 — Defined
Governance berada pada tahap Defined apabila:
* governance mechanism terdokumentasi;
* authority boundary mulai jelas;
* review mechanism tersedia;
* decision record mulai konsisten;
* governance lifecycle telah ditetapkan.
 
⸻
 
12. Maturity Level 3 — Managed
Governance berada pada tahap Managed apabila:
* governance mechanism diterapkan secara konsisten;
* decision traceability terjaga;
* accountability jelas;
* review menghasilkan measurable outcome;
* escalation berjalan sesuai mechanism;
* compliance dapat dipantau.
 
⸻
 
13. Maturity Level 4 — Optimized
Governance berada pada tahap Optimized apabila:
* governance performance dianalisis;
* recurring weakness dapat diidentifikasi;
* review mechanism terus diperbaiki;
* governance efficiency meningkat;
* cross-platform governance semakin predictable;
* improvement menggunakan evidence.
 
⸻
 
14. Maturity Level 5 — Adaptive
Governance berada pada tahap Adaptive apabila:
* governance dapat merespons perubahan architecture;
* governance mampu beradaptasi terhadap organizational evolution;
* governance learning menjadi bagian normal operating cycle;
* governance mechanism dapat berkembang tanpa kehilangan constitutional integrity;
* perubahan dapat dilakukan secara controlled dan traceable.
 
⸻
 
15. Maturity Assessment Rule
Maturity assessment tidak boleh:
* digunakan sebagai personal performance ranking;
* digunakan untuk mengubah authority secara informal;
* digunakan untuk menghapus governance requirement;
* digunakan untuk bypass review;
* digunakan untuk mengubah ownership tanpa approved decision.
Maturity assessment hanya menunjukkan kemampuan governance system pada saat assessment dilakukan.
 
⸻
 
16. Governance Success Review Cycle
Governance Success dievaluasi melalui tiga mekanisme.
16.1 Periodic Review
Dilakukan secara berkala untuk mengevaluasi:
* governance effectiveness;
* maturity;
* unresolved findings;
* recurring issues;
* improvement progress.
 
⸻
 
16.2 Event-Based Review
Dilakukan setelah event material seperti:
* major architecture change;
* material architecture decision;
* significant governance issue;
* major cross-platform conflict;
* authority deviation;
* material compliance finding.
 
⸻
 
16.3 Continuous Improvement Review
Dilakukan berdasarkan:
* review findings;
* escalation learning;
* compliance result;
* decision quality;
* cross-platform feedback;
* governance evidence.

26. Cross-Platform Success Boundary
Governance success lintas Platform harus dinilai berdasarkan:
* clarity of responsibility;
* clarity of authority;
* dependency management;
* decision interface;
* conflict resolution;
* preservation of ownership;
* governance traceability.
PD-02 tidak dapat menyatakan Platform lain “unsuccessful” hanya berdasarkan architecture preference.
Assessment harus berada dalam applicable scope dan evidence.
 
⸻
 
27. Governance Failure Conditions
Architecture Governance dianggap degraded apabila terdapat:
* recurring authority ambiguity;
* material decision traceability loss;
* unresolved accountability gap;
* repeated invalid delegation;
* systematic review bypass;
* repeated escalation failure;
* recurring cross-platform conflict;
* material compliance weakness;
* inability to produce governance evidence;
* improvement actions repeatedly failing without reassessment.
Satu isolated issue tidak otomatis berarti governance system gagal.
Severity dan recurrence harus dipertimbangkan.

30. Governance Success Principle
Canonical principle:
Governance is successful when it enables valid decisions, preserves accountability and authority integrity, resolves or escalates material issues appropriately, maintains cross-platform alignment, produces traceable evidence, and continuously improves without exceeding its constitutional boundary.
