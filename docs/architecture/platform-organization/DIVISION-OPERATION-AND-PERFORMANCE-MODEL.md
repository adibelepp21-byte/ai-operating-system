**Against canon:** `Domain Model §7` invariant 1 — *"Every Capability is owned by
**exactly one Platform Division**."* `Freeze §4` — Capability *"Ownership:
exactly one Department (INV-1)."*

**A `Sub Division` is not a Platform Division and is not one of the twelve
ratified entities.** Nor are `Team` (`B4`) or `Role Group` (`B5`). `Freeze §4`
states *"No new entity."*

### Both readings are available, and this corpus adopts neither

**Reading 1 — internal allocation.** `B2` classifies Sub Divisions as
*"Organizational Structure Architecture"* **"inside PD-01"**. On this reading all
ten Capabilities remain owned by **PD-01**, the one Platform Division, and the
`Owner` column records internal stewardship. **Invariant 1 is satisfied.**

**Reading 2 — a fourth Spine level.** `Domain Model §8`: *"The Spine is
intentionally shallow (**three levels**) and is **not to be deepened or bypassed
without an architectural decision**."* If Sub Divisions own Capabilities in the
Domain-Model sense, the Spine has a fourth level and invariant 1 does not hold as
written.

**Reading 1 is the more natural one and is probably intended.** It is **not
adopted**, because adopting it is a **Canonical Domain Model semantic
determination** — `DEL-T4.4-CF-001 §3.2` **exclusion 9**, and `Freeze §8` wall 5
(*Domain-Model change … not delegable*).

### What is done instead

**Recorded as `G-10`**, per the Encyclopedia's own instruction (`E-56`,
`Volume 3 §15` rule 7): *"Treat repository discrepancies as **reconciliation
findings**."* And rule 6: *"Do not redesign frozen architecture during repository
handoff."*

**Nothing in `volume-1/` is modified.** PD-01's bodies are frozen, reviewed,
and are the authority for PD-01. **No Capability is reassigned, no Sub Division
is endorsed or denied, and no invariant is declared violated.**

**Why it matters beyond PD-01:** PD-01 is the **Gold Standard Reference

reference pattern's Capability-ownership vocabulary is unreconciled against
