## 3 — The defect being acted on, and where it came from

`FD-P13-004 §7` finds `O5` a **`MATERIAL BUT BOUNDED REVISION DEFECT`**.

**This office raised that finding against its own work.** `v0.2 §X` recorded it
as the largest remaining `PREP-RECORD` claim carrying architectural weight;
`P13-008 §10` listed it first among unresolved findings; `P13-008 §12` stated
that `EXHAUSTED` without remainder was available only by leaving it unstated.

Recorded because it shows the disclosure requirement working, and for no other
reason. **It does not make the defect smaller and is not an argument for
accepting the artifact** — the Founder's `§7` conclusion stands on its own:
`O5` supports the proposition that a future P13 Blueprint would be *"mostly
citation rather than new architecture"*, and that proposition is carrying
weight it has not earned.

## 4 — What the next revision must do, and its two valid endings

`§15 R-01` admits exactly two outcomes. **Both are successes.**

| | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **A — Directly Verified** | name each of the seven items · its actual canonical source · the source location · record the evidence · preserve the other ten classifications · keep *source existence* apart from *proposed treatment* |
| **B — Not Directly Verified** | **do not reconstruct · do not infer · do not retain as though verified** · downgrade to `PREP-RECORD` or `UNKNOWN / NOT DIRECTLY VERIFIED` · state what evidence remains required |

> *"The revision must not manufacture certainty merely to satisfy the Founder
> gate."* — `§15`

**Outcome B is not failure.** `§22` accepts either `EXHAUSTED` or
`EXHAUSTED_WITH_CLASSIFIED_REMAINDER` *"depending on the evidence actually
available"*. The one forbidden ending is *"an unsupported claim presented as
direct evidence."*

`§16` adds the secondary requirement — `O5` must stop being ambiguous about:

```text
SOURCE EXISTS  ≠  SOURCE DIRECTLY VERIFIED  ≠  SOURCE IS CANONICAL
               ≠  PROPOSED TREATMENT IS CANONICAL
```

## 5 — What the next revision must not do

narrower than `ACT-CC-P13-005`. It must not become a new P13 discovery
programme."*

`GAP-0001`, not *"Super Intelligence"*, not the placement question, not the
autonomy boundary, not exit criteria, not the agent lifecycle, not
`Optimization → Governance`, not `FD-2`, not whether P13 proceeds.

exclusions, preservation, and terminal states.
