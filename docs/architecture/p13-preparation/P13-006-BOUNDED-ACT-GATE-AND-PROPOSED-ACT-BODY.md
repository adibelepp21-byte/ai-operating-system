# PART A — the gate

## A.1 What `FD-P13-002` settled, and what it deliberately did not

| | |
|---|---|
| **Settled** | the **authority mechanism**: a bounded, Founder-issued Act, per `§7` |
| **Deliberately unsettled** | whether `DEL-T4.4-CF-001 §3.3` covers P13 preparation **generally** — `§7`: *"The Founder deliberately does not resolve through this record…"* |
| **Not granted** | the drafting operation. `§20`: *"until the bounded Act itself is issued: `P13 BLUEPRINT DRAFTING = NOT AUTHORIZED`. This is intentional."* |

**The unsettling is the point, not an omission.** Option A would have been the
faster route and would have handed this office a standing interpretation of its
own delegation's reach. The Founder declined to create one. `§21`'s rationale
says why: *"preserving the narrowest necessary authority grant while preventing
technical necessity, precedent, capability, or agreement with the selected
method from becoming an implied expansion of delegated authority."*

## A.2 The ambiguity in `§21`, and the narrower reading taken

`§21`'s grant block — *"`AUTHORITY GRANTED BY THIS RECORD:` To establish and
issue a bounded authority instrument…"* — **names no holder.**

Read broadly, it would let this office write `ACT-CC-P13-004` **and issue it**,
then execute it. That reading is refused:

```text
§6   "through a Founder-issued Act"
§9   "A subsequent Founder-authorized Act shall be established"
§21  "a separate Founder-issued bounded Act"
```

Three clauses say *Founder-issued*; none says otherwise. Independently,
`DEL-T4.4-CF-001 §3.2.11` excludes *"create authority by implication"* and the
Appointment Register excludes *"Authority to authorize itself"*. An instrument
Claude both authored and issued to itself is the exact failure `§5`'s
forbidden-inference list exists to prevent.

**Disclosed because the broader reading was available and self-serving.**

## A.3 What was therefore done, and not done

```text
DONE        FD-P13-002 persisted verbatim with provenance separated
DONE        a proposed Act body prepared against §9's fourteen elements
NOT DONE    ACT-CC-P13-004 issued              ← the Founder's act
NOT DONE    any P13 Blueprint drafting         ← §8 · §16 · §20 · §24
NOT DONE    any P13 corpus synthesis toward a draft
```

`§16`'s sequence places `ESTABLISH BOUNDED ACT` before `EXECUTE ONLY WITHIN
BOUNDED SCOPE`, and adds: *"Claude Code shall not skip the bounded Act stage."*
Preparing a proposal for that stage is not performing it.

`§17` asks that the bounded Act carry enough internal execution authority that
ordinary technical steps need no Micro-Acts, while `NEW AUTHORITY · NEW SCOPE ·
CANONICALIZATION · CONSTRUCTION · NATIVE CORE CHANGE` stay outside it. Part B
`§7` and `§14` are drafted to that shape: broad *within* the named operation,
hard-stopped at its edges.

### `§1` — Exact drafting scope *(`§9.1`)*

Authorizes **one** operation: preparation of a **single non-canonical P13
Blueprint draft**, as one artifact at one named path, superseded by appendix if
revised.

Within that operation, authorized: `DISCOVER · CLASSIFY · RECONCILE · TRACE ·
ANALYZE · DESIGN · DRAFT · VERIFY · PERSIST · RE-DISCOVER` (`FD-P13-002 §17`).

The draft may **propose** P13 identity, scope, required state, architecture,
contracts, agent model, dependencies, verification and exhaustion criteria.
Every such proposal is a **proposal**, carrying its evidence or its absence.

**Not authorized:** any second artifact; any code, test, module, schema,
contract or configuration; any change to `native_core/`; any change to a
certified P10/P11 evidence file; any new Act.

### `§2` — Allowed source corpus *(`§9.2`)*

```text
the six-file P13 corpus            P1–P12 resident implementation and tools
P12 Blueprint v1.0 / v1.1          Native Core Blueprint (11 boundaries)
Platform Organization Master Map   Constitution · Delegation Register
Governance Decision Register       P10/P11/P12 certification records
P13-001 … P13-006                  resident verification evidence
```

**Non-resident sources may not be reconstructed.** Where a cited instrument is
absent — the *Co-Founder Delegation Charter* (`GAP-0006`),
`ACT-CC-R2BC-IMPL-001`, `ACT-CC-P6-066-R2` — the draft reports the absence and
reads no adjacent document in its place.

### `§3` — Evidence requirements *(`§9.3`)*

Every substantive statement carries one of:

```text
CANONICAL SOURCE    file · section · quoted text
MEASURED            the command or tool that produced it, re-runnable
PROPOSED            explicitly the draft's own proposal, with its reasoning
UNKNOWN             with the named absent source
```

`UNKNOWN ≠ FALSE`, and an `UNKNOWN` returned because evidence is absent is a
**correct** answer. A proposal presented as a finding is a defect.

### `§4` — Non-canonical status *(`§9.4`)*

The artifact carries, in its header and its exit state:

```text
STATUS: NON-CANONICAL DRAFT — NOT A FOUNDER DECISION
P13 CANONICAL DEFINITION = NOT ESTABLISHED
```

It may not be registered, indexed, cited or referenced as canonical, and no
machine-readable register may report P13 as defined on its account.

### `§5` — Prohibited authority expansion *(`§9.5`)*

This Act amends, reinterprets and expands **nothing** — not
`DEL-T4.4-CF-001`, not its scope, not any prior Act. It creates no standing
authority and no precedent for future P13 work (`FD-P13-002 §6`, `§21`). Its
authority attaches to the named operation and ends with it.

### `§6` — Prohibited construction *(`§9.6`)*

No construction of any kind: no runtime, daemon, scheduler, queue,
self-activation, subsystem, capability or agent. `docs/architecture/p13/` is not
created. `BLUEPRINT ≠ AUTHORIZATION · AUTHORIZATION ≠ CONSTRUCTION`.

### `§7` — Prohibited canonicalization *(`§9.7`)*

The draft does not become canonical because it is complete, verified,
internally consistent, test-passing, resident, executable, or recommended
(`FD-P13-002 §11`, verbatim list). Canonicalization is Founder-reserved under
`§58`. Claude may **recommend**; recommendation is not adoption.

### `§8` — Native Core boundary *(`§9.8`)*

```text
NATIVE CORE = 11 FROZEN BOUNDARIES
```

The draft may **observe** that a P13 capability would need a home, and must
record it as an open question for a separate authority path. It may not propose
a twelfth boundary as a resolved design.

### `§9` — Required outputs *(`§9.9`)*

```text
O1   P13 identity proposal — with what is proposed vs. sourced, marked
O2   P13 mission / scope proposal
O3   P13 required-state proposal, per §13's eight questions
O4   relationship to P1–P12, grounded in resident capability
O5   architecture proposal across the 17 scope items
O6   agent-model reconciliation (GAP-0009's three disagreeing lists)
O7   dependency and sequencing proposal
O8   governance / authority boundary proposal
O9   verification and exhaustion criteria proposal
O10  gap-closure map — which of the 28 a canonical definition would close
O11  open questions the draft cannot answer, with named absent sources
O12  Founder canonicalization package (§13 below)
```

### `§10` — Verification requirements *(`§9.10`)*

Before the draft is offered: citation audit `0` errors; every cited file and
section resolves; the resident test suite passes unchanged; no certified
evidence modified; `docs/architecture/p13/` absent; `P13 AUTHORIZED` reads
`False`; Native Core reads `11`; and **negative controls** exercised against
each prohibition in `§§4–8`, reported `HELD` or `FAILED` with evidence.

**A failing control is reported, never suppressed**, and the count is never
improved by weakening a control.

### `§11` — Persistence requirements *(`§9.11`)*

One artifact, one named path, committed to the designated branch with the
required trailers. Predecessors are **superseded by appendix, never edited**
(`PRIOR STATE → NEW EVIDENCE → RECONCILIATION → NEW STATE`). No historical
evidence is rewritten.

### `§12` — Exhaustion and re-discovery *(`§9.12`)*

After drafting, re-run discovery against the resulting state and report: new
gaps · new conflicts · reclassifications · newly blocked or unblocked work · new
evidence requirements. Terminal state is one of `EXHAUSTED` ·
`EXHAUSTED_WITH_CLASSIFIED_REMAINDER` · `BLOCKED_AUTHORITY`, with its reason.

### `§13` — Founder canonicalization handoff *(`§9.13`)*

The draft is delivered with a package stating: what it proposes; what it
sources; what it leaves `UNKNOWN`; which of the 28 gaps a canonicalization
would close and which would remain; what canonicalization would and would not
authorize; and the options open to the Founder — **accept · accept with
modification · reject · return for revision · replace with Founder-defined
direction** (`FD-P13-001 §6`).

**No option is recommended as the decision.** `DRAFT → FOUNDER REVIEW → FOUNDER
DECISION → CANONICALIZATION` is mandatory (`FD-P13-002 §11`).

### `§14` — Stop conditions *(`§9.14`)*

`STOP → RECORD → TRACE → ESCALATE`, never self-authorization, on any of
`FD-P13-002 §18`'s ten conditions. Additionally: if the draft cannot be written
without inventing a P13 definition that no source supports, **that is the
finding** — `ACT-CC-P13-003 §39` governs: *"If that statement cannot yet be
established, the correct outcome is not invention."*

did not name — `12. authority boundaries` · `13. Founder / Architect reserved
matters` · `14. explicit completion boundary` — and collapses four prohibitions
into one. The issued Act carries the **union of both lists** (`§15` of the Act
maps them), so neither specification excuses the other.

**Part A's refusal stands and was vindicated.** `A.2` refused to read
`FD-P13-002 §21`'s unattributed grant as permission to self-issue. `FI-P13-004
§3` then ruled on exactly that: *"The authority chain shall not be interpreted
as: `FD-P13-002` → Claude self-issues `ACT-CC-P13-004`. Self-issuance is
expressly prohibited."* Had the broader reading been taken, an instrument would
have been issued in breach of a prohibition the Founder had not yet written but
plainly held.

**The authorship of the body is disclosed, not buried.** The text is Claude's;
the authority is the Founder's. Both the Act's header and `FI-P13-004`'s
provenance section say so, and both state that if the Founder intended different
Act text, this body must be replaced and every output under it re-examined.
