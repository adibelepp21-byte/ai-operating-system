## 1. Purpose

This register is the durable record of scoped delegations issued under
Engineering Constitution §3.2:

> *"The Architect may delegate a bounded portion of architectural-tier approval
> authority. Any delegation must state an explicit scope. A delegate holds only
> the authority stated within that scope."*

Before this register existed, the repository recorded — in two independent
Architecture Decision Records — that **no delegation was in force**:

- `ADR-0008` §"Authority validation": *"No delegation is in force. This ADR is
  Under Review and **not approved**; nothing in it takes effect before approval."*
- `ADR-0009` §"Authority validation": *"…a decision establishing a
  cross-Department convention is not within any scoped delegation currently in
  force."*

Those statements were accurate when written and are retained as historical
record. They are superseded in fact, not in text, from the effective date of
DEL-T4.4-CF-001 below.

## 2. Maintenance Rules

- **Append-only.** Entries are added, never rewritten. A superseded or revoked
  entry is marked as such and retained in place; its text is not altered.
- **Identifiers.** `DEL-<act>-<slug>-NNN`, assigned on entry. A recording
  convention only; it grants no authority and introduces no entity.
- **Entry structure.** Every entry carries: identifier · date · delegating
  authority · delegate · scope · exclusions · effective condition · review
  condition · revocation authority · evidence · status history.
- **Verbatim discipline.** A decision's text is recorded as the deciding
  authority stated it, without reinterpretation, substitution, or paraphrase.
- **No self-authorization.** No delegate may create, approve, expand, or
  activate its own delegation. Recording a delegation is an Implementation-Tier
  act (Constitution §3.3) performed under explicit authorization; it is not an
  approval act.

### 3.1 Delegated Scope

The following architectural-tier and construction authority is delegated for the
AIOS Construction Phase.

**A. Architecture construction**
Coordinate architecture construction · perform architecture design · perform
architecture review · prepare architecture decisions · prepare architecture
recommendations · coordinate architecture implementation · validate
architectural consistency · maintain architecture evidence · identify
architecture conflicts · perform architecture reconciliation within this scope.

**B. Architecture approval**
Architecture Approval may be exercised **only** where the decision is an
architecture concern **and** falls within this delegated scope. Architecture
Approval does not authorize governance policy, strategic direction, resource
allocation, operational execution outside this scope, or constitutional
modification.

**C. Engineering**
Direct construction sequencing · define implementation approach within approved
architecture · perform engineering implementation · perform validation · perform
testing · perform certification · perform repository mutation · commit and push
where separately permitted by the applicable engineering workflow.

**D. Construction coordination**
Coordinate architecture, engineering, governance construction, documentation,
validation, evidence production, cross-platform construction dependencies, and
construction-phase roadmap sequencing. Coordination does not transfer ownership
of another domain.

**E. Conflict resolution**
Resolve conflicts within Implementation Tier, within the delegated Architectural
Tier, and within this explicit construction scope.

### 3.3 Authority Boundary Rule

```
AUTHORIZED ACTION = EXPLICIT DELEGATION × VALID SCOPE × VALID TIER × VALID ARTIFACT
```

If any factor is absent, the action is **NOT AUTHORIZED**.

The delegate may never infer authority from: role · capability · urgency ·
confidence · precedent · silence.

Where a proposed action would require Constitutional-Tier authority,
Founder-reserved authority, non-delegable authority, ownership or legal status,
or a change to the authority relationship itself:

```
STOP → RECORD → ESCALATE → FOUNDER DECISION
```

## 6. Revocation and Suspension

**Revocation.** This delegation is revocable by the Founder or applicable
Architect authority. On valid revocation the delegate reverts to **STATE 0**.
Previously valid actions are not retroactively invalidated merely because
authority is later revoked; historical records remain immutable.

**Suspension.** Fail-closed. May be invoked when authority is disputed,
validation fails, governance integrity is uncertain, a material architecture
conflict is unresolved, or the Founder orders a construction halt. During
suspension no disputed authority action may proceed; independent, non-disputed
work may continue only where explicitly safe and authorized.

**Distinct mechanisms — not interchangeable:** role revocation · authority
revocation · delegation revocation · access revocation · operational suspension ·
operational status. Role revocation, authority revocation, and operational
suspension are not defined in the ratified corpus and remain **UNKNOWN —
Founder decision required**.

**Emergency authority.** None is created. Urgency does not expand authority
(Constitution §6.2 invariant 2). Emergency conditions follow
`STOP → RECORD → PROTECT → EVIDENCE → ESCALATE → DECIDE`.
