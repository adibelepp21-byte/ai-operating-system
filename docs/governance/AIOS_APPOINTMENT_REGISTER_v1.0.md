## 1. Purpose and Scope Limit

This register records **individual appointments explicitly made by the Founder**
to governance-state roles.

**It does not establish a general appointment model.** ACT-CC-T4.2 and
ACT-CC-T4.3 recorded that no appointment model exists in the ratified corpus
(`grep "Appointment" docs/` → 0 results at that time), and that establishing one
would be a Constitutional-Tier matter carried as **FD-5**. **FD-5 remains
UNDECIDED.** This register records what the Founder decided, in the form the
Founder decided it; it does not generalise from that decision, and no lifecycle,
succession, or revocation model may be inferred from its structure.

---

## 2. Maintenance Rules

- **Append-only.** Entries are added, never rewritten. A superseded or revoked
  entry is marked as such and retained in place; its text is not altered.
- **Identifiers.** `APT-<act>-<slug>-NNN` for appointments,
  `ACT-<act>-<slug>-NNN-A` for activations. Recording conventions only; they
  grant no authority and introduce no entity.
- **Verbatim discipline.** A Founder decision is recorded exactly as stated,
  without reinterpretation, substitution, translation, or paraphrase.
- **No self-appointment.** No holder may create, approve, expand, or activate
  its own appointment. Recording an appointment is an act performed under
  authority held **before** the appointment takes effect; it is not an approval
  act.
