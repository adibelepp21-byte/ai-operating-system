5. E10-01 — DEPARTMENT IDENTITY & POPULATION

Requirement

The P10 Department Ecosystem must have an authoritative and evidence-backed Department population.

For every Department included in the P10 ecosystem:

* identity is established;
* identity is unambiguous;
* purpose is defined;
* domain/responsibility boundary is defined;
* organizational placement is established;
* population is supported by authoritative evidence;
* no Department is created merely from conceptual inference;
* Platform Divisions are not automatically treated as Departments.

Pass Condition

E10-01 = PASS

only if:

1. every resident Department is traceable to legitimate authority/evidence;
2. no required Department is demonstrably missing;
3. no unauthorized Department has been introduced;
4. Department identity is consistent with canonical architecture;
5. Department population survives negative/falsification testing.

Evidence

Evidence may include:

* canonical source;
* Founder Decision;
* Architect Decision;
* approved ADR;
* Department specification;
* runtime representation;
* population determination;
* negative controls;
* reconciliation results.

6. E10-02 — OWNERSHIP INTEGRITY

Requirement

Department ownership must be structurally and evidentially consistent.

At minimum:

Department
↓
Capability
↓
Owner

must be coherent.

The relevant ownership invariants must hold.

Pass Condition

E10-02 = PASS

only if:

* required Department-owned Capabilities have valid ownership;
* no unauthorized ownership exists;
* no Capability has conflicting Department ownership;
* ownership declarations agree with the runtime graph;
* relevant ownership invariants pass;
* ownership assignment does not cross reserved authority boundaries;

Agent Instance objects that are explicitly transient and non-owned must not be misclassified as Agent Definitions for purposes of this criterion.
