## How the identifier was determined

The instrument states no identifier. It directs (`§1`): *"Determine the next
valid identifier from the canonical numbering/registration convention. Do not
invent, guess, or assume the Founder Decision ID."*

* **The series.** Founder Decision Records are the `FDR` class of the
  governance index grammar. The Decision Register holds `FDR-1` (`§20`),
  `FDR-2` (`§21`) and `FDR-3` (`§23`). No Register entry or act holds `FDR-4`.
  The next valid identifier is therefore `FDR-4`.
* **Disclosed: an earlier, unused label.** The GOAL-V2-005 completion record
  (`AIOS_GOAL_V2_005_P13_COMPLETION_RECORD_v1.0.md` `§12`, written by Claude)
  once used `FDR-4` as a label for an escalated question, C-1 (ecosystem
  integration). No Founder decision was ever issued under that label. That
  historical record is not rewritten. The same disclosure was made for `FDR-3`.
