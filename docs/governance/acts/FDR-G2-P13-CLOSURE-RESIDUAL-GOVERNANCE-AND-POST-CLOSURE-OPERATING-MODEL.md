## The identifier

The instrument states `FDR-G2` and names its dispositions `FD-G2-C5`,
`FD-G2-C6` and `FD-G2-C8` (`§26`). No Register entry, act or record uses
these identifiers, so nothing collides. `FD-G2-C…` is distinct from `FDR-G1`'s
sub-decision `FD-G2` (P13 closure), which this decision answers.

## One line that grants nothing

`§6.4` contains the line *"P13 CLOSURE = GRANTED"*. It states the form the
Founder's separate Closure Decision shall use. It is not a grant:
`§1` (*"They do NOT themselves constitute the P13 Closure Decision"*), `§19`
(*"FDR-G2 does not itself close P13"*) and `§25` (*"P13 CLOSURE = NOT
GRANTED"*) all say so. Any reader of closure state must not take that line for
a decision.

## What it decides (Claude's reading; the fenced text governs)

| Disposition | Founder decision | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| FD-G2-C5 | residual governance **ACCEPTED AS NON-BLOCKING TO P13 CLOSURE** | covers the classified residual frontier and the four inherited P11/P12 escalations, while their classification stays unchanged. Nothing is solved or closed by it (`§4.2`) |
| FD-G2-C6 | closure evidence = **current closure gate + fresh verification + Founder Closure Decision** | fresh verification is a state check, not a new E13-05 live proof (`§6.3`). The minimum evidence package has 14 items (`§6.5`) |
| FD-G2-C8 | **P13 CLOSED → AIOS CONTINUES UNDER GOVERNED OPERATION** | closure ends P13's phase lifecycle only. P13-derived capabilities remain; no authority is created; `P13-ENV-02` stays retired; S-OPS stays historical (`§7`) |

It also:
- accepts the CEO's A17 exhaustion of `P13-018` `D-1`, and preserves the
  record (`§7.5`);
- keeps `FDR-G1`'s successor model and the certification baseline unchanged;
- authorizes the bounded execution in `§17`, in the order of `§19`, ending in a
  hard stop before the Founder Closure Decision.

**It does not close P13.**
