1. FOUNDER GOAL

The Founder establishes the following Goal:

RESTORE AND ENFORCE THE INTEGRITY OF P12 CERTIFICATION STATE AND ITS ASSOCIATED CERTIFIED EVIDENCE.

The purpose of this Goal is to ensure that the certified state of P12 is
represented consistently and truthfully across governance, self-model,
evidence controls, runtime observations, verification tooling, and any other
relevant system surface.

2. FOUNDER TARGET

The Target is achieved when the CEO has established, verified, and evidenced
that:

1. P12’s certified state is correctly represented by the relevant authoritative
    governance records and operational readers;
2. certified P12 evidence cannot be silently rewritten, invalidated, or
    misrepresented by normal authorized verification activity;
3. runtime observations associated with certified P12 state have an explicit
    and correct authority / protection relationship;
4. self-model and other operational state readers do not materially contradict
    the authoritative P12 certification state;
5. relevant certification guards, verification tooling, and state readers
    recognize the actual certified state correctly;
6. any remaining inconsistency, limitation, dependency, or historical anomaly
    is identified and accurately classified;
7. the resulting implementation and governance state is verified with
    evidence proportional to the claims made; and
8. mandatory re-discovery has been performed after material construction.

* what provenance is required;

* what guard or control prevents unauthorized mutation.
