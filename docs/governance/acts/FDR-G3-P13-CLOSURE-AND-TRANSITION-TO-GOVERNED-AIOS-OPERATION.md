## The identifier

The instrument states `FDR-G3`. No Register entry, act or record uses it, so
nothing collides.

## Where it decides closure

The decision is stated twice, each time as a line on its own after the
Founder's decision label:
- `§1`: *"The Founder hereby decides:"*, then *"P13 CLOSURE = GRANTED"*;
- `§36`: *"The Founder hereby grants:"*, then *"P13 CLOSURE = GRANTED"*.

This differs from `FDR-G2` `§6.4`. There the same line follows *"The Closure
Decision shall explicitly state:"*, so it is a stated form and not a grant.

The status line's words *"CERTIFY CLOSURE"* change no certification: `§5`,
`§32` and the stated *"Certification Effect: No change to certification
status"* say so. The guard reads no certification statement in this text.

## What it decides (Claude's reading; the fenced text governs)

* **P13 CLOSURE = GRANTED**, on the evidence of the closure gate, fresh
  verification and this decision (`§2`–`§4`). P13 closes as a phase lifecycle
  state. P13 construction is complete (`§5`, `§7`).
* **Nothing else changes:**
  - certification; E13-01 … E13-07; the certified root;
  - `FDR-G1`'s evolution model; the certification baseline;
  - state-changing authority NONE; `P13-ENV-02` retired; S-OPS historical;
  - the `P13-018` record, preserved with its scope exhausted;
  - residuals: non-blocking, not solved.
* **AIOS continues under governed operation** (`§18`–`§19`). No new
  authority, no Phase 14 (`§21`).
* It authorizes making the closure machine-recognizable (`§25`), within the
  twelve conditions of `§26` and the state machine of `§27`. The negative
  controls NC-01 … NC-12 (`§28`) and the post-closure verification
  (`§29`–`§30`) must hold.
