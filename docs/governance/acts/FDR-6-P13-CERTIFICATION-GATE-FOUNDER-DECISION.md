## A contradiction inside the instrument, disclosed

The instrument's header reads *"Status: PENDING FOUNDER AUTHORIZATION"*. Its
signature block reads *"Founder Authorization: APPROVED FOR PERSISTENCE AND
BOUNDED EXECUTION"*, and `§15` begins *"After this Founder Decision is formally
persisted and registered, Claude Code shall execute"*. The signature block is
taken as governing, because it is the Founder's authorization statement, and
the header is a status field of the kind a draft carries. This is the same
determination the P12 Authorization Founder Decision records for its own stale
*"Status: PENDING FOUNDER DECISION"* header. It is disclosed here, not resolved
away. A reader who disagrees can see what was overruled.
