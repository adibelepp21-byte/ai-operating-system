## 6. Constraints on execution under this Act

- **Minimum change.** Preserve valid existing state; repair only what is
  necessary; do not rewrite history unnecessarily; do not destroy valid evidence.
- **No silent conflict resolution.** Where two records conflict:
  `inspect → determine provenance → determine authority → reconcile → persist → verify`.
- **Evidence discipline.** Every material remediation must establish
  `SOURCE → DECISION → AUTHORITY → ACTION → ARTIFACT → VERIFICATION`, and must
  distinguish Founder decision, conversational evidence, repository evidence,
  historical evidence, implementation fact, and derived conclusion.
- **No conversational result may be presented as repository fact until
  persisted.**
- **Self-authorization control.** Execution under this Act is Founder-authorized
  and is therefore not self-authorization; but no action may be justified by
  authorship, capability, precedent, role title, previous execution, or assumed
  authority. Material execution actions must record
  `Authorized by: FOUNDER · ACT-CC-F03-009`.
- **No invented statuses.** `CONDITIONAL ELIGIBLE`, `CONDITIONAL FREEZE`,
  `TEMPORARY FREEZE` may not be created.
