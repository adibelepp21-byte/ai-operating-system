# § RULING — issued by the Founder, verbatim

FOUNDER RULING — D-P12-027-04

I rule that §6.7's phrase "has been completed" shall be interpreted as completion of the verification activity/process required by §6.7, including the required verification work, finding classification, and gap recording, rather than as an unstated requirement that every property examined by that activity must itself hold/pass.

This ruling does not declare §6.7 SATISFIED automatically.

The resulting determination must still establish, from evidence, whether the §6.7 activity itself has actually been completed according to its canonical requirements.

Required treatment

Do not use the following inference:

"has been completed"
→ all examined properties must PASS
→ §6.7 PASS

Likewise do not use:

"verification activity completed"
→ therefore every finding is resolved

The correct distinction is:

VERIFICATION ACTIVITY COMPLETED
≠
EVERY VERIFIED PROPERTY SATISFIED

The findings produced by §6.7 remain findings unless another canonical requirement establishes that they must be resolved for P12 completion.

Therefore:

* §33 remaining vocabulary limitations remain findings;
* §34 historical provenance limitation remains a finding;
* §31 workflow connectivity remains a finding;
* §30 runtime verification limitation remains a finding;
* §26 governance findings remain findings;

unless their respective canonical requirements independently establish that their resolution is required for P12 completion.

Do not map EscalationRequired → BLOCKED merely to increase the §33 score.

Do not create governance documents merely to eliminate the §26 ABSENT count.

Do not modify the P12 Exit Contract.

Do not reopen or reinterpret unrelated conditions for the purpose of producing 14/14.

Fresh determination

Under ACT-CC-P12-027, perform a fresh determination of §6.7 based on this ruling.

Specifically establish:

1. the exact canonical activity required by §6.7;
2. whether that activity has actually been performed;
3. whether findings have been classified as required;
4. whether gaps have been recorded as required;
5. whether any additional §6.7-specific activity remains actionable;
6. whether any remaining findings are themselves canonical completion conditions, rather than merely outputs of the verification activity.

Do not infer that unresolved findings automatically prevent §6.7 from being satisfied.

Do not infer that completion of the verification activity automatically resolves those findings.

Completion

After resolving §6.7, perform another complete P12 Exit Contract determination.

Do not assume:

§6.7 SATISFIED
→ P12 COMPLETE

Only declare:

P12 COMPLETE = YES

if the complete canonical Exit Contract is independently satisfied.

If any condition remains NOT SATISFIED or NOT ESTABLISHED, preserve that state and identify its exact canonical basis.

No P13 authorization follows from this ruling.

# § APPLICATION — what the ruling actually changed

**The load-bearing instruction was *"this ruling does not declare `§6.7`
SATISFIED automatically."*** Without it, the semantic grant alone would have
closed the condition. With it, `§6.7` had to be re-derived from its own
canonical requirements — and that re-derivation is what found work.

**Question 5 produced two findings Claude had not enumerated.** Both are
verifier-quality findings, and both are squarely `§6.7`-specific because `§6.7`
*is* the verification activity:

| | Instrument | Why it could no longer demonstrate its negative |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `p12_mutation_verification` | its falsifiability control demonstrated `MISSED` **from the live corpus**, so it went `NOT DEMONSTRATED` the moment `§50` reached 10/10 — a control that reads only in a fire |
| 2 | `p12_runtime_verification` | its control **required** the live system to be `HAND-INVOKED ONLY` before demonstrating `REACHED`, so a genuine improvement made the control look broken |

Both were rewritten to drive their negatives synthetically, as every
neighbouring control already does, and to report the live figure beside the
demonstration rather than depend on it. **Neither instrument's live result
changed**; what changed is that both can now be shown to be able to fail.

**A verifier that cannot report a negative is a formatted assertion, not a
check** — the falsifiability module's own words. Two of thirty-six were in that
state, which is a real shortfall in the verification activity itself, and it was
invisible for as long as `§6.7` was being argued about at the level of its
findings rather than its activity.
