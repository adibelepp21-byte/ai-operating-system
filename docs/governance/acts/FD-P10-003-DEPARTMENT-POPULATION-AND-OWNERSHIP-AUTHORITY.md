## 1–3. Declaration, basis, architectural distinction

Two decision surfaces are decided **separately**, and neither may be inferred
from the other: **A — Department Population**, **B — Ownership Assignment
Authority**.

`PD ≠ P10`. `PD-01`…`PD-10` are the Platform Organization source/definition
track; `P10` is Department Ecosystem operationalization. **PD-01–PD-10 must not
automatically be interpreted as Departments.**

## 4–7. DECISION A — Department Population

**Department Population must be established from the authoritative organizational
source model and shall not be invented by Claude Code.** It may be determined
only from: (1) an explicit Founder Decision; (2) an authoritative Architect
Decision where Architect authority is sufficient; (3) **an authoritative
canonical organizational source that explicitly establishes Department
identity**; (4) a later superseding decision.

**Claude Code shall not convert `PD-01` … `PD-10` into Departments merely because
ten Platform Divisions exist.**

```text
CANONICAL DEPARTMENT = EXPLICITLY ESTABLISHED ORGANIZATIONAL UNIT
                     ≠  PD = DEPARTMENT
```

*"Where `ADR-0029` does not itself establish sufficient authority for population,
this Founder Decision supplies the required Founder-level authority to resolve
the population boundary."*

**P10 shall use an explicit Department population** rather than treating the
entire PD-01–PD-10 Platform Organization as automatically equivalent to the
Department Ecosystem. The list must be persisted only after reconciling
`ADR-0029` + `G-09` + Platform Organization sources + Canonical Architecture +
existing Founder Decisions + current P10 implementation. **Source lineage must be
preserved for every Department included or excluded.** No Department may be added
because it is convenient, technically useful, or architecturally symmetrical.

For every established Department: IDENTITY · PURPOSE · DOMAIN · RESPONSIBILITY ·
CAPABILITY BOUNDARY · AUTHORITY · OWNERSHIP STATUS · INTERFACES · DEPENDENCIES ·
LIFECYCLE · AIOS RELATIONSHIP.

## 8–12. DECISION B — Ownership Assignment Authority

**Claude Code is authorized to establish Department ownership relationships only
to the exact extent expressly supported by this Decision and existing canonical
authority.** This does not authorize arbitrary ownership assignment.

```text
EXPLICIT DEPARTMENT → EXPLICIT OWNERSHIP AUTHORITY
                    → AUTHORIZED OWNERSHIP RELATIONSHIP → OWNERSHIP GRAPH

never:  DEPARTMENT EXISTS → THEREFORE CLAUDE MAY ASSIGN OWNERSHIP
```

**§9 — all eight conditions must hold:** the Department is canonically
established; the owned capability/artifact is identified; ownership authority is
explicitly established; the proposed owner is within the authorized
organizational model; no higher-authority source prohibits it; it creates or
modifies no constitutional authority; it transfers no Founder Reserved Authority;
it silently modifies no other protected authority boundary. **If any condition is
unmet, Claude must not manufacture the missing authority.**

**§10 — Reserved ownership boundaries.** This Decision does not automatically
resolve every owner-to-CPID relationship. **Security Owner**, **Quality
Authority** and **Governance Authority** must be separately reconciled. *"If an
existing Founder Decision or Founder Executive Decision expressly withholds those
assignments, this Decision does not override that withholding unless this
Decision explicitly states otherwise."*

**§11 — prohibited inferences:** Department exists ≠ Department owns capability ≠
Department may authorize execution ≠ Department may modify governance. PD owns
domain ≠ P10 may rewrite PD. **Implementation exists ≠ Ownership is authorized.**

**§12 — where ownership is legitimately authorized**, Claude may instantiate the
ownership representation, populate the ownership graph, establish
Department → Capability relationships, establish authorized ownership and
maintenance/accountability relationships, create derived artifacts, update
dependent implementation, create or update verification, and persist the
evidence. **Claude must not use ownership implementation to create new authority
that does not already exist.**
