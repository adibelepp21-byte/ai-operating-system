Canonical Exit Criteria

The following seven criteria become the P13 exit-contract framework:

P13 can establish the relevant AIOS state from authorized evidence.

P13 can produce traceable reasoning over authorized evidence.

P13 can determine appropriate next actions based on:

* current state;
* evaluation;

* governance;
* authority.

Where D05 permits autonomous execution, P13 can:

* check authority;
* execute authorized action;
* refuse unauthorized action;
* escalate reserved/ambiguous action;
* record evidence;
* verify outcome.

E13-06 — Evolution and Re-Discovery

P13 can:

* identify capability/state gaps;
* propose or execute authorized changes;
* verify changes;
* rediscover resulting state;
* maintain evidence of what changed.

Evolution does not imply unrestricted self-modification.

P13 can demonstrate that:

* authorized scope has been addressed;
* required capabilities are verified;
