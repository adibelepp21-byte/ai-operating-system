## Provenance of this body — read before relying on it

**The authority is the Founder's. The text is Claude's.**

`FI-P13-004 §21` and `§22` issue this Act. `FI-P13-004 §15` holds that the text
prepared under `P13-006` *"shall remain a proposal until incorporated into the
issued Act"*, and `§18` directs that *"the issued Act and this Founder Issuance
shall be persisted in the appropriate canonical governance location."* This
document is that incorporation and persistence.

**No Act body was transmitted with the issuance.** The body below was prepared
by Claude — under `FD-P13-002 §9`'s specification and revised to meet
`FI-P13-004 §6`'s — and is persisted here because `§18` directs it. Persisting
an instrument the Founder has issued is not issuing it: `FI-P13-004 §3`'s
prohibition on self-issuance is observed, the issuing act being the Founder's in
`§21`/`§22` above.

**If the Founder intended different Act text, this body must be replaced and
every output produced under it re-examined against the replacement.** That is
recorded here, before any work was done under it.

**Element lists.** `FD-P13-002 §9` and `FI-P13-004 §6` specify *different*
fourteen-element sets. This body carries the **union of both**; the mapping is

## `§1` — Exact drafting scope *(FD §9.1 · FI §6.1)*

This Act authorizes **one** operation: preparation of a **single non-canonical
P13 Blueprint draft**, as one artifact at one named path. A revision supersedes
by appendix; it does not replace the artifact.

Within that operation the following are authorized:

```text
DISCOVER   CLASSIFY   RECONCILE   TRACE   ANALYZE
DESIGN     DRAFT      VERIFY      PERSIST RE-DISCOVER
```

The draft may **propose** P13 identity, mission, scope, required state,
architecture, contracts, agent model, dependencies, governance boundary,
verification criteria and exhaustion criteria. Every such proposal is a
**proposal**, carrying its evidence or the named absence of it.

**Not authorized by this Act:** any second artifact; any code, test, module,
schema, contract or configuration; any change under `native_core/`; any change
to certified P10/P11/P12 evidence; any new authority instrument; any Act.

## `§3` — Evidence requirements *(FD §9.3 · FI §6.3)*

Every substantive statement carries exactly one of:

```text
CANONICAL SOURCE   file · section · quoted text
MEASURED           the command or tool that produced it, re-runnable
PROPOSED           explicitly the draft's own proposal, with its reasoning
UNKNOWN            with the named absent source
```

`UNKNOWN ≠ FALSE`. An `UNKNOWN` returned because evidence is absent is a
**correct** answer. **A proposal presented as a finding is a defect**, and
disclosing one is required, not optional.

authorize Claude to: canonicalize or declare canonical the P13 Blueprint ·
authorize or perform P13 construction · activate P13 runtime capability ·
certify P13 · establish P13 governance closure · create or authorize Native
Core #12 · expand the standing delegation · issue authority to itself · create

This Act **amends, reinterprets and expands nothing** — not `DEL-T4.4-CF-001`,
not its scope, not any prior Act. It creates no standing authority and **no
precedent** (`FI-P13-004 §13`, `§14`).

```text

NATIVE CORE = 11 FROZEN BOUNDARIES — unchanged and unchangeable by this Act
```
