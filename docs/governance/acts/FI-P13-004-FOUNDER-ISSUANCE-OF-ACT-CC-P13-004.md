## Provenance

The body below `§ RECORD` is the Founder's, persisted **verbatim**. The
surrounding material is Claude's and is separated for that reason.

## What was issued, and what had to be supplied to make it operative

`§21` marks `[X] ISSUE ACT-CC-P13-004`; `§22` states *"I, Founder, hereby issue
the authority instrument identified above"*, signed, `Status: ISSUED`; `§20`
gives the terminal state as `ISSUED = ACTIVE = BOUNDED`.

**The issuance arrived without an Act body.** `§15` conditions operativeness on
the Founder issuing this instrument *"together with the actual Act body"*, and
holds that the text prepared under `p13-preparation` *"shall remain a proposal
until incorporated into the issued Act"*. `§18` then directs that *"the issued
Act and this Founder Issuance shall be persisted in the appropriate canonical
governance location."*

Accordingly the body was incorporated and persisted as
[`ACT-CC-P13-004`](ACT-CC-P13-004-P13-NON-CANONICAL-BLUEPRINT-DRAFTING-AUTHORIZATION.md).
**Its provenance is stated in its own header and is stated here: the text was
prepared by Claude; the authority is the Founder's, granted in `§2`, `§21` and
`§22`.** Persisting an issued instrument is `§18`'s instruction. It is not
issuance, and `§3`'s prohibition — *"the authority chain shall not be
interpreted as: `FD-P13-002` → Claude self-issues `ACT-CC-P13-004`"* — is
observed: the issuing act is the Founder's above, not Claude's.

## The element list changed between instruments, and the Act satisfies both

`FD-P13-002 §9` specified fourteen elements. **`FI-P13-004 §6` specifies a
different fourteen** — it collapses the four prohibitions into one and adds
three that were not previously named:

```text
NEW IN FI §6     12. authority boundaries
                 13. Founder / Architect reserved matters
                 14. explicit completion boundary
```

The proposal prepared under `P13-006` was written against `FD-P13-002 §9` and
therefore did **not** meet `FI-P13-004 §6`'s minimum as named sections. The
issued Act carries the **union of both lists**, so neither specification is
relied on to excuse the other.

## If this is not the body the Founder intended

`§15` reserves the Act body to the Founder's issuance. If the Founder intended
to transmit different text, **this body must be replaced and everything done
under it re-examined against the replacement.** That is stated here, before any
work was done under it, rather than after.
