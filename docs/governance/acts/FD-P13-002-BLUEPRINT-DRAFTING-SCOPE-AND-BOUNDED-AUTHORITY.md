## The one ambiguity in this record, and the narrower reading taken

`§21`'s Authority Grant block reads:

> `AUTHORITY GRANTED BY THIS RECORD:` *To establish and issue a bounded
> authority instrument for one specific P13 non-canonical Blueprint drafting
> operation.*

**It does not name who holds that authority.** Read broadly, it could be taken
as authorizing Claude to *issue* the bounded Act — and then to execute it. That
reading is refused here, for three reasons drawn from the record itself:

```text
§6   "through a Founder-issued Act"
§9   "A subsequent Founder-authorized Act shall be established"
§21  "I require ... to be governed by a separate Founder-issued bounded Act"
```

Three clauses say *Founder-issued*; none says otherwise. An instrument Claude
both wrote and issued to itself would also be barred independently by
`DEL-T4.4-CF-001 §3.2.11` (*"create authority by implication"*) and the
Appointment Register's exclusion 19 (*"Authority to authorize itself"*), and it
is the precise failure `§5`'s forbidden-inference list exists to prevent.

**The narrower reading is therefore taken: the authority to issue
`ACT-CC-P13-004` is the Founder's.** This is disclosed rather than assumed,
because the broader reading would have been available and self-serving.

`§9` specifies fourteen elements the Act's body *"must explicitly establish"*.

It is a **decision package, not an instrument**. It is deliberately **not**

No drafting authority shall be inferred from its filename or intended purpose."*

Preparing it is `architecture decision preparation` under `DEL-T4.4-CF-001
§3.1 A` — the same class of work as the `FD-P13-002` package this decision
answers. **Issuing it is not.**
