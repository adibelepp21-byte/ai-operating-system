## What it directs (Claude's reading; the fenced text governs)

* One integrated governance transition across FQ-1 (certified architecture
  evolution), FQ-2 (P13 closure semantics) and FQ-3 (certification baseline
  scope), plus the maintenance-authority question (`§15`).
* Each action is classified as already authorized, authorized with boundary,
  Founder decision required, outside authority, or unknown authority (`§5`).
* Work already within the delegation may be executed (`§10`). A governance
  artifact may be built only if all eight `§11` conditions hold.
* Where Founder authority is required, compact decision packages are prepared
  (`§12`). They must not contain a predetermined decision.
* One integrated report (`§20`), then a hard stop (`§24`).

It grants no certification, closure, new phase, roadmap, retroactive
certification, certified-architecture change or new authority.
