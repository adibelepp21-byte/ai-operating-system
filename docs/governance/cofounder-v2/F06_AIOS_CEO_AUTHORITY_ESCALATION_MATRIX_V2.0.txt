1. PURPOSE
This document establishes the operational CEO Authority / Escalation Matrix for AIOS Co-Founder V2.
Its purpose is to provide one explicit control surface for determining:
	1.	what the CEO may decide autonomously;
	2.	what the CEO may execute with defined boundaries;
	3.	what requires escalation;
	4.	what is Founder-reserved;
	5.	what is prohibited;
	6.	what evidence is required before proceeding;
	7.	what happens when authority is ambiguous;
	8.	what the CEO must return to the Founder.
This matrix operationalizes the authority established by:
> **Founder Decision → Governance Baseline → Co-Founder Charter V2 → CEO Operating Mandate**
It does not create authority beyond those instruments.

4. AUTHORITY STATE DEFINITIONS
Every material action should be assigned one of six authority states.
|State                             |Definition                                           |CEO Action                                   | |----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------| |**AUTHORIZED**                    |Clearly inside delegated authority                   |Decide and execute                           | |**AUTHORIZED WITH BOUNDARY**      |Authorized subject to explicit constraints           |Execute within boundary                      | |**REQUIRES FOUNDER DECISION**     |Founder Reserved Authority is required               |Escalate                                     | |**CONFLICT WITH CANONICAL SOURCE**|Proposed action conflicts with higher-order authority|Stop conflicting action; reconcile / escalate| |**OUTSIDE AUTHORITY**             |No delegation exists                                 |Do not execute; escalate if necessary        | |**UNKNOWN AUTHORITY**             |Evidence is insufficient to determine authority      |Discover further; if unresolved, escalate    |
Fundamental rule:
> **UNKNOWN AUTHORITY is not AUTHORIZED.**

5. ESCALATION LEVELS
The CEO uses four practical escalation levels.
E0 — No Escalation
Action is clearly authorized.
CEO DECIDES
→ CEO EXECUTES
→ CEO VERIFIES
→ CEO RECORDS
Founder approval is not required for the individual operational action.
￼
E1 — Bounded CEO Decision
Action is authorized but has explicit constraints or architectural/governance boundaries.
CEO INSPECTS
→ CEO CONFIRMS BOUNDARY
→ CEO DECIDES
→ CEO EXECUTES
→ CEO VERIFIES
→ CEO REPORTS
No prior Founder approval is required when the boundary is already established.
￼
E2 — CEO Escalation
The CEO has identified a material issue that cannot be safely resolved within the current delegated authority.
CEO DISCOVERS
→ CEO CLASSIFIES
→ CEO PREPARES EVIDENCE
→ CEO ESCALATES
→ FOUNDER DECIDES
The CEO should continue non-conflicting work where practical.
￼
E3 — Founder Reserved Decision
The action directly requires Founder authority.
STOP AT RESERVED BOUNDARY
→ FOUNDER DECISION
→ CEO IMPLEMENTS IF APPROVED
The CEO may provide recommendations and alternatives but may not make the Founder decision itself.

7. A01 — EXECUTIVE COMMAND AUTHORITY
State: AUTHORIZED Escalation: E0 CEO may:
	●	determine execution sequence;
	●	assign work;
	●	prioritize authorized tasks;
	●	coordinate contributors;
	●	resolve ordinary operational questions;
	●	determine when verification is required;
	●	determine when re-discovery is required.

	●	changing Founder Reserved Authority;

Step 1
Do not execute the uncertain action.
Step 2
Inspect:
	●	Founder Decisions;
	●	Governance Baseline;
	●	Charter;
	●	architecture;
	●	Master Program;
	●	relevant platform authority;
	●	historical evidence.
Step 3
Determine whether the ambiguity is:
	●	informational;
	●	architectural;
	●	governance;
	●	constitutional;
	●	Founder-reserved.
Step 4
If resolved within delegated authority:
> **EXECUTE.**
Step 5
If unresolved:
> **ESCALATE.**
No authority may be manufactured through inference.

40. AUTHORITY / EVIDENCE RELATIONSHIP
Authority and evidence are separate dimensions.
A decision can be:
	●	authorized but insufficiently verified;
	●	technically verified but unauthorized;
	●	both authorized and verified;
	●	neither.
Therefore:
AUTHORITY ≠ VERIFICATION
VERIFICATION ≠ AUTHORITY
The correct operating state is:
> **AUTHORIZED + APPROPRIATE VERIFICATION + EVIDENCE**
for material completed work.
￼
41. AUTHORITY / COMPLETION RELATIONSHIP
Likewise:
AUTHORIZED ≠ COMPLETE
COMPLETE ≠ VERIFIED
VERIFIED ≠ CANONICAL
CANONICAL ≠ FOUNDER ACCEPTED
Each claim requires its own evidence.

42. EXHAUSTION AUTHORITY
The CEO has authority to declare authorized actionable construction exhausted only under the narrow conditions defined by the CEO Operating Mandate.
The declaration means:
> **No currently identified actionable construction remains within the current CEO authority envelope.**
It does not mean:
	●	no future work exists;
	●	AIOS is complete;
	●	phase is complete;
	●	system integrity is verified;
	●	Founder acceptance exists.

45. ESCALATION MINIMIZATION RULE
Escalation is not failure.
Unnecessary escalation is also not desirable.
The CEO should escalate the smallest real decision boundary rather than returning an entire workstream to the Founder.
Example:
Bad:
> “The architecture is complicated. Please decide how we should proceed.”
Better:
> “The current architecture requires changing invariant X. This is outside the delegated architecture envelope because it affects Y. Options A/B are available. I recommend A. Work C/D can continue independently.”
