1. PURPOSE
This document is the Authority Reconciliation Layer preceding the construction of AIOS Co-Founder Delegation Charter V2.0.
It is not the V2 Charter.
Its purpose is to determine, with explicit boundaries, how the authority model proposed in Co-Founder Delegation Charter V1.0 may evolve into a V2 operating model in which:
> **Founder defines the Goal and Target. CEO determines and executes the authorized means of achieving them.**
The Founder remains the ultimate human governance authority.
The proposed CEO is responsible for the autonomous execution required to achieve Founder-authorized Goals and Targets, subject to:
	●	Constitution;
	●	Founder Reserved Authority;
	●	valid Founder Decisions;
	●	canonical governance;
	●	canonical architecture;
	●	authorized authority boundaries;
	●	explicit escalation requirements.
This reconciliation layer determines:
	1.	what is inherited from V1;
	2.	what is expanded;
	3.	what is newly introduced;
	4.	what is compatible with the current Governance Baseline;
	5.	what is compatible with the Master Program;
	6.	what remains Founder Reserved Authority;
	7.	what requires explicit Founder Decision;
	8.	what requires governance reconciliation;
	9.	what Claude Code may perform autonomously;
	10.	what must be escalated;
	11.	what is explicitly prohibited;
	12.	what documents must change if V2 is authorized;
	13.	what operating assumptions must not be treated as canonical before V2 activation.
The objective is to eliminate ambiguous formulations such as:
> “Claude has broad authority.”
Authority must instead be defined by:
Domain + Permission + Scope + Boundary + Trigger + Escalation + Evidence + Terminal Condition.

This principle remains subject to unresolved Founder Decisions identified in this document.
