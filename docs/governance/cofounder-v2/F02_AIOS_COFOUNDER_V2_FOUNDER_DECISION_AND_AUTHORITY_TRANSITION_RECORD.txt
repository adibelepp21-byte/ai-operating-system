This document is the formal Founder Decision and Authority Transition Layer between the V2 Authority Reconciliation Matrix and the construction of the Co-Founder Delegation Charter V2.0.

The CEO becomes the primary executive authority responsible for autonomous execution within the authority envelope approved by the Founder.
