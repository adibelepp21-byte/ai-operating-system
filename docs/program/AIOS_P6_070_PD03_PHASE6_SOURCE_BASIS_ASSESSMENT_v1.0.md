## 1. Executive result

**`C` — SOURCE GAP REMAINS.** Volume 3 PD-03 does not provide canonical Phase 6
source basis. It provides **none at all**, and the reason is structural rather
than incidental.

**[E] The four supplied files contain zero Phase 6 content.** Measured across all
four: the word **"Phase" appears 0 times**. So do *"Phase 6"*, *"Knowledge
Ecosystem"*, *"Language Intelligence"*, *"retrieval"*, *"reasoning"*, *"exit
criteria"*, *"E6-"*, *"Master Program"*, and *"GDR-"*. The word *"Knowledge"*
appears **once**, inside a boundary list; *"Intelligence"* **once**, on the same
line.

**[A] The premise rests on a volume-numbering conflation, and the repository
distinguishes the two systems unambiguously.**

```text
Master Program Volumes  I – VIII    (Roman)   Era 2 program volumes
                                              → Vol II §5 phase list, Vol VI Intelligence categories
                                              → THIS is what ACT-CC-P6-069 found missing

Platform Encyclopedia Volumes 1 – 10 (Arabic) one per Platform Division
                                              → volume-1 = PD-01, volume-2 = PD-02, volume-3 = PD-03
                                              → THIS is what was supplied
```

**[E] Confirmed in the repository:** the only tracked volume directories are
`docs/architecture/volume-1/pd-01-executive-office/` and
`docs/architecture/volume-2/pd-02-architecture-office/`. Arabic volume numbers
index Platform Divisions. Master Program citations use Roman numerals throughout —
*Volume I, II, III, V, VI, VIII*.

**[A] The two are different axes of the roadmap, and the Master Roadmap says so.**
Its §2: *"**Phase** = which system capability is built · **Platform** = which
organization owns the domain capability"*, drawn as `PHASES 0→13` and `PLATFORMS
PD-01→PD-10` on separate branches. Phase 6 sits on the Phase axis; PD-03 sits on
the Platform axis. Supplying a Platform volume cannot close a Phase gap.

**[A] And PD-03 is the wrong Platform even on its own axis.** Master Roadmap §5
platform registry: *"`PD-03` Governance & Compliance · **`PD-04` Knowledge &
Intelligence**"*. The Platform that owns the Knowledge domain is **PD-04**. PD-03
owns Policy, Standards, Approval, Control, Certification, Compliance — and its own
handoff states repeatedly that governance responsibility does not transfer domain
ownership.

**[E] Separately, the Volume 3 corpus itself was not supplied.** The Act's §2
describes *"80 Sections, Part A through Part H"*. **Zero of the 80 canonical
section bodies (`A1`–`H10`) are present in the four files**, and **zero are
resident in the repository** (0 tracked files matching `pd-03` or `volume-3`). What
was supplied is four *control and closure artifacts* totalling 780 lines. The
files say so themselves — Encyclopedia §17: *"**The complete section bodies already
established in the project source/repository remain the authoritative payload.**
This handoff file must not be used to reconstruct missing prose by inference."*
