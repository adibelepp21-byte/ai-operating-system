## 0. What this step is

`R3` is the Roadmap's own entry gate. It requires establishing seven state
inputs and returning **exactly one** of four verdicts:

```text
P10 READY
P10 READY WITH BOUNDED OPEN ITEMS
P10 BLOCKED
P10 REQUIRES FOUNDER DECISION
```

with the instruction: **"Do not manufacture readiness."**

**This artifact returns that verdict.** Reaching a `BLOCKED` result is executing
`R3`, not declining it — `R3` supplies the outcome slot precisely because the
answer may be negative.

## 8. R3 VERDICT

```text
P10 ENTRY BASELINE RESULT:   P10 BLOCKED
```

**Determining condition — a canonical prerequisite, not a delegation limit:**

`Master Program Volume VII §1.2` — Department (P10) *"**baru sah dibangun**
setelah Workflow Ecosystem (Phase 9) matang"* — **only lawfully built after
Phase 9 matures.** `Volume II §5` states the dependency `10 ← 9`.

**Phase 9 stands at 0% · Belum Dimulai. So do Phases 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8.**

**This Roadmap's own `R3` places `P10 BLOCKED` among its four valid outcomes and
instructs "Do not manufacture readiness."** The verdict is the instruction
followed, not refused.

**Secondary conditions, each independently sufficient:**

1. **Exit criteria unratified.** `Volume V §3` reserves ratification of Phase
   5–13 exit criteria into measurable form to the Program Owner, triggered at
   `H-1 Phase`. P10 is not next in line.
2. **Activation non-delegable.** `Volume VII §4.1` — every decision authorizing
   a Department to operate *"tetap berada pada Pemilik Program, bukan
   didelegasikan … **bahkan setelah Executive Office diimplementasikan**"*.
3. **Population undetermined.** `G-09` — P10's six Departments against Track B's
   ten Platform Divisions, one name shared. `R4` requires *"for every **canonical**
   department"*; which departments are canonical is unresolved.

**`P10 REQUIRES FOUNDER DECISION` is also true** for conditions 1–3, which are
Program-Owner-reserved. `P10 BLOCKED` is reported as primary because condition
0 — Phase 9 immaturity — is **not resolvable by any Founder decision alone**;
it requires Phases 4–9 to be built.
