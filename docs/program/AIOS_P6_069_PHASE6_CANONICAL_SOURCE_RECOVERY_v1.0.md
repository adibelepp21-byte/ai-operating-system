## 29.1 Executive result

**`C` — SOURCE GAP.** The authoritative source that `GDR-0005` requires — an
existing Phase 6 statement to transform into measurable form — cannot be
established or recovered. Four independent discovery paths were exhausted; the
Master Program Volumes I–VIII have **never** existed in this repository.

**But the recovery was not empty. Three results change the position materially.**

**[A] 1. The Phase-identity ambiguity is resolved, and it was my error.**
`ACT-CC-P6-067 §4` and `ACT-CC-P6-068 GAP-B` recorded an unresolved conflict:
`GDR-0004 §3.4.5` places **Language Intelligence** at *"future Phase (Phase 6)"*
while the Roadmap names Phase 6 the Knowledge Ecosystem. **Reading the whole table
rather than its Language row dissolves the conflict.** Its column heading is
*"**Deferral recorded by the Master Program**"*, and its other rows read *"future
implementation need"*, *"future repository audit (Gate 2)"* and — for Creative —
*"future **Gate** (Gate 2) **+** future **Phase** (Phase 10)"*. A category deferred
to a Gate *and* a Phase cannot be the identity of either. **The table schedules
categories into phases; it never names a phase.** `GDR-0004` therefore asserts
nothing about what Phase 6 *is*. **GAP-B is CLOSED.**

**[A] 2. One canonical Phase 6 statement does exist, and it is not the one anyone
was looking for.** By the same table, **Language Intelligence is canonically
scheduled into Phase 6** (`GDR-0004 §3.4.5`, citing Vol VI §3.8), and `GDR-0005`'s
`E5-5` requires that deferral to *"retain its recorded status"*. This is the only
canonical statement found that attaches anything to Master Roadmap Phase 6. It
still yields no criterion — §29.8 shows why — but it is canonical, and it means
Phase 6's scope is a live question rather than a settled one.

**[A] 3. The `NCIR §9.5` substitution did not originate where I said it did.**
`ACT-CC-P6-067 §9.1` attributed it to `ACT-CC-P6-064`. It originates in
`AIOS_F03_CLOSURE_AND_NEXT_GATE_v1.0.md §5.2`, executed under **FOUNDER /
ARCHITECT · `ACT-CC-F03-061`**, 2026-08-21, which lists *"**Construction Target** |
`NCIR §9.5` deliverable"*. That record labels the block *"**Candidate**
construction gate"* and records *"Existing Authorization: **NONE**"*. `P6-064` did
not invent the mapping; it treated a **candidate** as **the** target and did not
notice that `NCIR` sequences Phase 3. The correction is recorded here; neither
record is rewritten.
